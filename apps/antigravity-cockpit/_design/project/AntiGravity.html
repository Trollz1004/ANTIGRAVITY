MISSION · #MembershipVerificationSupport

Gravity keeps us grounded.
AntiGravity lifts a kid up.

Every product in this stack is built to earn. A share of net proceeds — after taxes, fees, and the cost of keeping the stack running — is designated for families who need medical care. The long-term goal is a transparent ledger that shows what was earned, what went to taxes and ops, and what actually reached a family. That ledger isn't live yet, which is why the figures in the prototype are marked illustrative. When the ledger goes live, this page will link straight to it.
