Everyday platforms, built with one mission: help kids in need.

How the mission works

No platform charges you a "cause" fee. Each one is priced as an ordinary product; the mission has its own public page where anyone can back it and see the record.

01

The platforms operate as businesses

Each site sells a real product or service — recycling logistics, AI automation, or a game — and is run to be sustainable on its own.

02

The mission has its own public page

Until No Kid In Need lives on Open Collective, separate from the product sites. That page — not this one — is where the current, itemized picture is.

03

Funds are tracked in the open

Every contribution and every expense on the mission is recorded on Open Collective, where anyone can review the ledger.

A few common questions

What is Until No Kid In Need?

The mission name used across this family of platforms, operated by Trash or Treasure Online Recycler LLC. Its public record lives on Open Collective rather than on this site.

How is the mission funded?

Through its Open Collective page, where every contribution and expense is public. This site publishes no figures of its own — the collective page is the source.

Are the recycling, AI tools, and game platforms run by the same team?

They share the same underlying mission and ownership, but each operates as its own product with its own site, its own customers, and its own roadmap.

Where can I follow the game, DREAM Online?

DREAM Online has its own site at dream-online.net and a public GitHub repository for development updates.
