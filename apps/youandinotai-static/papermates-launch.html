Founding Signal / launch concept

Say a little more than “maybe.”

Founding Signal is PaperMates’ limited-launch alternative to a generic super-like: a small, contextual way to name why an introduction caught your attention.

See Orbit in action →Read safety controls

A Founding Signal is not a promise of a match, reply, compatibility, safety result, app-store review, or any donation. Eligibility, limits, and launch terms must be published before use.

What changes

Less performative, more deliberate.

PaperMates is built around an adult-only product scope, choices before connection, and a documented human-help path.

01

Context, not popularity

Signal one specific intention or shared thread. Never imply that the other person owes a response.

02

Control, not surveillance

Use your own pace, private pass, block tools, and optional check-in plan. Trust states are explained, not hidden scores.

03

Support, not a black box

Reports, appeals, and customer help require accountable review. AI-assisted drafts are not active at this launch stage.
