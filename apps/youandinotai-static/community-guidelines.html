Community Guidelines

Short list, hard edges. This platform exists because the rest of the internet let the bots and creeps in. We don't.

01Be the human you verified as

Your photos are you. Your age is your age. One account, no "managed" profiles, no bots, no automation. Catfishing is a ban.

02Adults only. Always.

No minors on the platform, no sexual content involving minors anywhere, no exceptions, no warnings.

Violations are removed, terminated, preserved as evidence, and reported to NCMEC and law enforcement per our Child Safety Standards.

03Respect the person on the other side

  • No harassment, stalking, threats, or hate based on who someone is.
  • No sharing anyone's private info or intimate images without consent — including after a match ends.
  • No unsolicited sexual content. Ask first. It's not complicated.

04No hustles

  • Never ask members for money, gifts, financial help, or "investment opportunities." That is the romance-scam playbook and it is an instant, permanent ban.
  • No advertising, link-dropping, follower farming, multi-level recruiting, or selling.

05Report and block — please actually use them

Every profile and message thread has Report and Block. Blocking is instant and invisible to the blocked member. Reports go to a human. Child-safety reports jump the queue. False reports made to weaponize moderation are themselves a violation.

06What enforcement looks like

Depending on severity: content removal → warning → suspension → permanent ban. Some things skip the ladder entirely: anything involving minors, money hustles, threats of violence, and non-consensual intimate imagery go straight to permanent ban, and where the law is implicated, to the authorities.

07Appeals

Think we got it wrong? Email support@youandinotai.com from your account email with the word APPEAL in the subject. A different human reviews it.
