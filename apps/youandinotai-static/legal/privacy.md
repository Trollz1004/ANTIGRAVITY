# Privacy Policy — Business Exchange & Funding Platform

**Last updated:** July 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Business Exchange and Funding Platform (the "Platform") collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. The Platform is a product surface operated as part of the ANTIGRAVITY / YouAndINotAI product family. It governs the same Platform described in the [Terms of Service](./tos.md).

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Account information** — email, password (hashed), and profile details you provide when creating an account (founder, operator, or investor).
- **Listing and pitch content** — business exchange listings (services, projects, referrals, business sales, asset sales), funding pitches, deal terms, and any files or descriptions you submit.
- **Verification data** — business registration, tax ID, identity documents, proof of funds, and domain ownership information submitted to complete a verification check.
- **Messaging data** — messages exchanged with other users through per-deal threaded conversations.
- **Matching criteria** — investor preferences, stated criteria, and activity signals used to surface relevant listings and pitches.
- **Payment data** — payments for membership, verification, and platform fees are processed by Square. We do not store full card numbers; Square handles payment card data directly.
- **Technical data** — IP address, device/browser information, access times, and error logs collected for security and platform reliability.

## 2. How We Use Information

- Operate core Platform features: listings, pitches, investor matching, deal messaging, and admin tooling.
- Perform verification checks (business registration, tax ID, identity, proof of funds, domain ownership).
- Process membership, verification, and platform fee payments through Square.
- Provide account access, customer support, and maintain platform uptime and security.
- Detect and prevent fraud, misrepresented ownership, or misuse of investor matching.

Data submitted for verification is used solely to perform the verification check.

## 3. What We Do Not Do

- We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
- A purchase, listing, pitch submission, or investor match does not create ownership, equity, securities, voting rights, or control rights, and does not grant access to data beyond what is necessary to operate the Platform.
- We do not use account or verification data to make claims about ownership, control, or investment return — the Platform is a discovery and deal-workflow tool, not a broker-dealer or investment adviser.

## 4. Sharing With Third Parties and Other Users

- **Service providers** — payment processing (Square) and identity/verification vendors, used strictly to operate the features described in these policies.
- **Other users** — you control what is visible in your public listings and pitches. Private deal and verification data is not shared with other users beyond what is necessary to complete a matched introduction or verification check.
- **Legal requirements** — we may disclose information when required by law or to respond to a valid legal process.

## 5. Data Retention

Account, listing, pitch, and messaging data is retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period after closure to support disputes, verification records, or legal obligations. Verification documents are retained only as long as needed to complete and support the verification check, subject to applicable law.

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

- You may review and update your account and listing information at any time.
- You may close your account; certain deal and verification records may be retained as required by law or for dispute resolution.
- You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and contractual exceptions (e.g., completing a transaction or retaining verification records).
- To exercise these rights, contact support through the Platform's support channel.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect account, listing, and verification data, including encrypted storage of sensitive credentials and restricted access to verification documents. No system is completely secure; report any suspected security issue to support immediately.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor is using the Platform, contact support immediately.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the Platform after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests to exercise your privacy rights, can be directed through the Platform's support channel.
