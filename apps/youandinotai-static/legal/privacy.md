# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 12, 2026  
**Product:** YouAndINotAI and associated AI-solutions business exchange / funding platform features

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal data for the services operated at YouAndINotAI, including membership, verification, safety, support, matching/account discovery, checkout, and AI-solutions business exchange and funding workflows.

## 1. Data we collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated through platform use:

- **Account and profile data:** email, display name, profile details, photos, preferences, age eligibility status, and verification status.
- **Safety and moderation data:** reports, blocks, verification signals, and trust/safety actions.
- **Support and account data:** ticket history, message threads, identity recovery requests, and support notes needed to resolve issues.
- **Checkout and membership data:** payment method status metadata, receipt references, subscription/product selection, and plan status.  
  Payment details themselves are handled by Square.
- **Platform and technical data:** device and browser identifiers, IP address, session details, and essential interaction logs for uptime and abuse prevention.
- **Business exchange and funding data:** business profile details, submission materials, campaign updates, investor-facing request fields, and progress notes you share when participating in exchange/funding features.

## 2. How we use your data

We use personal data to provide and improve product value:

- Create and maintain accounts and verified profiles.
- Provide membership access, chat, matching/discovery, and date/event planning flows.
- Operate verification, account recovery, and fraud prevention.
- Process and track membership checkout and entitlement status.
- Run support operations and resolve safety incidents.
- Prevent abuse, spam, scams, impersonation, and policy violations.
- Keep platform reliability, uptime, and quality in line with operating standards.
- Enable AI-solutions exchange/funding workflows where you opt into those tools.

## 3. Data sharing

We share personal data only as needed:

- With service providers that support core operations (hosting, analytics, email, payment processing, anti-fraud tooling, and customer support tooling).
- With platform moderators/support teams for safety and trust enforcement.
- With legal, legal-process, or compliance authorities when required by applicable law.

We do not use your personal data for non-product reasons or unrelated private decisions.

## 4. International transfers

If your data is processed outside your home country, we use standard safeguards and service-provider security practices to protect it. We do not transfer personal data for speculative commercial resale.

## 5. Data retention

We retain personal data as needed to:

- provide ongoing service access,
- complete support and safety reviews,
- keep required financial and tax records related to receipts and membership status,
- meet legal retention obligations.

When no longer required, we delete or de-identify data where feasible, except where we must keep limited records for legal or safety reasons.

## 6. Cookies and device data

Necessary cookies and local storage support sign-in, session security, checkout integrity, and platform abuse controls. Optional analytics or preference tooling may be used where available to improve stability and reliability.

## 7. Your rights

You can request:

- access to your stored personal data,
- corrections to inaccurate data,
- account access support,
- deletion where legally and operationally possible,
- limits on optional usage and sharing described above.

Requests can be started through the support workflow in-product.

## 8. Security

We use technical and organizational controls to reduce unauthorized access, misuse, and unauthorized disclosure. No system is fully risk-free, but we maintain layered protections as part of platform operations.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as products evolve (for example, new verification, safety, support, or exchange/funding features). The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

## 10. Contact

If you have a privacy question, contact support via the in-app support channel.
