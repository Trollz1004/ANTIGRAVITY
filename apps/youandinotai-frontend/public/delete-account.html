Delete your account and data

Two ways to do it. Both are free, both are permanent, and neither involves a retention specialist trying to talk you out of it.

01In the app

Settings → Account → Delete account. Confirm once, and it is done. Your profile disappears from matching immediately.

02By email (no app needed)

Email support@youandinotai.com with the subject line DELETE MY ACCOUNT, sent from the email address on your account. We verify it is really you, then delete. If you cannot use the account email, say so — we will verify identity another way before acting.

03What gets deleted, and when

  • Your profile is removed from the service immediately upon confirmation.
  • Personal data — profile, photos, messages, matches — is deleted within 30 days.
  • Exceptions we are required or permitted to keep: records needed to comply with law; payment records held by the payment processor; and safety reports or content preserved in connection with abuse investigations, in line with our Privacy Policy and Child Safety Standards.

04Want a copy first?

Ask in the same email and we will export your data to you before deletion. Deletion is permanent — there is no undo and no reactivation of a deleted account.
