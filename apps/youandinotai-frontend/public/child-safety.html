Child Safety Standards

You & I, Not AI is an adults-only platform. These are our published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). They apply to every member, every message, and every image on the service.

01Zero tolerance

Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any sexual exploitation, sexualization, grooming, or endangerment of minors is strictly prohibited.

This covers real, synthetic, AI-generated, drawn, and text-based content, on-platform and in any conduct we learn of that uses the platform to reach or harm a minor. There are no warnings for this category — detection means immediate account termination and, where applicable, referral to authorities.

02Prevention: adults only, verified

  • Every member must be 18 or older and must pass identity and age verification through an independent verification provider before they can send or receive messages.
  • Accounts found to belong to minors are terminated and their data deleted per our Privacy Policy.
  • Attempting to help a minor access the service is itself a ban and may be reported to authorities.

03In-app reporting

Every profile and every message thread carries a Report control. Reports in the categories "Involves a minor" or "Child safety" are placed at the front of the moderation queue and receive prioritized human review. Reporters may remain anonymous to the reported member.

04Detection and moderation

  • User reports are reviewed by a human, with child-safety reports prioritized above all other queues.
  • Uploaded images pass automated safety screening, and we expand detection tooling (including industry hash-matching for known CSAM) as the platform scales.
  • Moderators are trained to escalate suspected CSAE immediately rather than resolve it as ordinary abuse.

05Action, preservation, and reporting to authorities

When we identify apparent CSAM or CSAE conduct we: remove the content immediately, terminate the account, preserve the content and relevant records as evidence in accordance with applicable law, and report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the appropriate law-enforcement authorities. We cooperate with valid legal process in resulting investigations.

06Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws, including U.S. federal reporting obligations, and with the child-safety requirements of the app stores that distribute our apps. These standards are reviewed at least annually and whenever law or platform policy changes.

07Designated point of contact

Child-safety concerns, law-enforcement inquiries, and app-store child-safety correspondence: safety@youandinotai.com. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (911 in the U.S.) first.
