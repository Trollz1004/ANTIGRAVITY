Dating safety, from people who kicked the bots out

Everyone you can message here passed identity and age verification. That raises the floor — it does not make anyone owe you trust, and it does not replace your own judgment. Verified is not vetted.

01Before you meet

  • Keep conversation on the platform at first. Scammers rush you to other apps for a reason.
  • Video chat before meeting. A verified human who won't show their face live is telling you something.
  • We do not run criminal background checks. A profile here is a verified identity, not a character reference.

02Money is the tell

The moment anyone asks for money, gift cards, crypto, or your financial details — stop, report, block.

No sob story, emergency, or "investment" changes this rule. Members asking for money are banned on the first report we verify.

03First meetings

  • Public place, daytime if you can, your own transportation both ways.
  • Tell a friend who, where, and when — and check in after.
  • Keep your drink with you, and keep your phone charged.
  • If it feels wrong, leave. You owe a stranger nothing, including an explanation.

04Protect your info

Hold back your home address, workplace details, daily routine, and financial info until trust is earned over time. Watch for profiles that ask a lot and share little.

05If something happens

  • In immediate danger: call 911 (or your local emergency number) first.
  • Report the member in-app — every profile and message has a Report button — or email safety@youandinotai.com.
  • For sexual assault support in the U.S.: RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline, 1-800-656-4673, available 24/7.
