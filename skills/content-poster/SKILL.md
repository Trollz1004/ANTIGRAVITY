# Content Poster

Social posting, calendar management, A/B testing, and compliance checks.

## Scope
- Content pillars and weekly calendars
- Caption hooks, CTA placement, post timing
- A/B tests on hooks, formats, posting windows
- Compliance review before any post ships

## Hard rules
- Cookie-sync / human cadence for browser-based posting
- No automated headless bot posting on flagged platforms
- One CTA per post; approved links only
- No cause, mission, split, cap, kids language
- No invented engagement numbers in copy

## KPIs
- Engagement rate (likes + comments + shares / impressions)
- Saves and shares
- Traffic or conversions from social
- Compliance violations: zero

## Starter checklist
- Define 3 content pillars and 7-day post map
- Write 3 caption hooks; A/B test two variants
- Compliance pass: no banned tokens, no fake metrics
- Schedule posts and review after 7 days
