# YouTube Creator

YouTube strategy, scripts, production basics, editing, SEO, and retention.

## Scope
- Channel pillars, upload cadence, long-to-short funnel
- Hook-retain-payoff scripts and pacing for watch time
- Audio quality, lighting, framing basics
- Editing rhythm, visual variety, thumbnails, YouTube SEO

## Hard rules
- No cocky tone, no bragging language
- No invented numbers, no fake testimonials
- No cause language unless explicitly approved by Joshua
- One clear CTA per video
- Approved links only in description/pinned comment

## KPIs
- Average view duration / retention curve
- Click-through rate on thumbnails
- Subscriber growth from uploads
- Traffic from YouTube to landing

## Starter checklist
- Draft 30-second hook for next video; test aloud
- Record one short clip with improved audio setup
- Create three thumbnail variants; track CTR
- Research top 5 keywords for next upload
