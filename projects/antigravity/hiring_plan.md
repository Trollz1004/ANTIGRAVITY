# ANTIGRAVITY Project Hiring Plan

## Executive Summary

As CEO of the ANTIGRAVITY project, I need to establish a core team to develop and operate the YouAndINotAI platform. The platform's mission is to facilitate real-world human connection through verified interactions, community engagement, and volunteering opportunities. Revenue generated from the platform supports children's charities through contractual revenue disbursement (never donations).

## Key Roles to Fill

### 1. Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
**Responsibilities:**
- Lead technical architecture and development of the platform
- Manage backend (FastAPI/Python) and frontend (React/Next.js) development teams
- Oversee integration with Square for commerce functionality
- Ensure platform security, scalability, and reliability
- Implement Cloudflare hosting and related services

**Required Skills:**
- Senior-level experience with FastAPI, React, and Next.js
- Experience with PostgreSQL databases
- Knowledge of cloud hosting (Google Cloud Run, Cloudflare)
- Experience with payment systems integration (Square)
- Strong cybersecurity and data protection knowledge

### 2. Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
**Responsibilities:**
- Develop and execute marketing strategies for user acquisition and retention
- Create content strategy for blog posts, social media, and community engagement
- Manage brand identity and messaging aligned with the "social platform for good" mission
- Oversee customer support and community management
- Track and optimize key marketing metrics and KPIs

**Required Skills:**
- Digital marketing expertise with focus on authentic community building
- Content creation and social media management experience
- Understanding of privacy-focused marketing practices
- Experience with analytics and data-driven marketing decisions
- Strong communication and brand storytelling abilities

### 3. UX Designer
**Responsibilities:**
- Design intuitive and engaging user interfaces for the platform
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design systems
- Collaborate with development teams to implement designs
- Ensure accessibility compliance and inclusive design practices

**Required Skills:**
- UI/UX design expertise with portfolio demonstrating successful consumer products
- Proficiency with design tools (Figma, Sketch, Adobe Creative Suite)
- Understanding of responsive design principles
- Experience with user research methodologies
- Knowledge of accessibility standards (WCAG)

## Hiring Process

1. Define detailed role descriptions with skill requirements and expectations
2. Identify recruitment channels appropriate for each role
3. Conduct initial screening interviews
4. Technical assessment (for CTO position)
5. Cultural fit evaluation with core team members
6. Reference checks
7. Offer preparation and negotiation
8. Onboarding plan development

## Timeline

- Week 1-2: Finalize role descriptions and begin recruitment outreach
- Week 3-4: Screen candidates and conduct initial interviews
- Week 5-6: Complete assessments and final interviews
- Week 7: Extend offers and begin onboarding

## Budget Considerations

- Align compensation packages with market rates for senior positions
- Consider equity participation options to attract top talent
- Account for benefits and equipment costs
- Plan for ongoing professional development budgets

## Success Metrics

- Time to fill each position (goal: 6 weeks or less)
- Candidate acceptance rate (goal: 80% or higher)
- New hire retention rate at 6 months (goal: 90% or higher)
- Team productivity and deliverables completion rates
