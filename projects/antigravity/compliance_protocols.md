# Governance and Compliance Protocols for Charitable Revenue Disbursement

## Overview

The ANTIGRAVITY project is committed to supporting children's charities through contractual revenue disbursement as part of our mission to be a "social platform for good." This document outlines the governance and compliance protocols necessary to ensure all activities adhere to legal requirements, particularly Florida Statute §496.405, and maintain transparency in our charitable giving practices.

## Legal Compliance Requirements

### Florida Statute §496.405 Compliance

Florida law prohibits the use of "donate," "donation," and "solicitation" terminology in certain contexts. Our compliance protocol requires:

1. **Terminology Standards:**
   - Use "contractual revenue disbursement" instead of "donation" or "charitable contribution"
   - Use "contractual giving program" instead of "fundraising campaign"
   - Use "revenue allocation" instead of "solicitation"

2. **Documentation Requirements:**
   - Maintain detailed records of all revenue transactions
   - Document contractual agreements with recipient charities
   - Track actual disbursements to qualifying organizations
   - Retain records for minimum seven-year period as required by law

3. **Financial Reporting:**
   - Quarterly internal reviews of revenue disbursement calculations
   - Annual external financial audit by certified public accountant
   - Transparency reports published on public website (aggregated data only)
   - Board approval of annual charitable giving budget

### Revenue Allocation Framework

Our platform operates under a strict 10% LLC-controlled revenue cap for charitable disbursement until such time as Joshua Coleman (founder) changes this policy.

#### Calculation Methodology:

1. Monthly LLC-Controlled Revenue Calculation:
   - Gross revenue from subscription fees
   - Gross revenue from transaction fees
   - Gross revenue from advertising/sponsorships
   - Less: Direct operational expenses (hosting, payment processing fees, etc.)

2. Charitable Disbursement Calculation:
   - Maximum allowable: 10% of LLC-controlled revenue
   - Quarterly disbursement schedule
   - Distribution among vetted children's charities

3. Approval Process:
   - CFO prepares monthly calculation report
   - CEO reviews and validates calculations
   - Board ratifies quarterly disbursement decisions
   - Legal counsel verifies compliance with all regulations

## Charitable Partner Vetting Process

### Organization Qualification Criteria:

1. **Legal Status:**
   - Valid 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status
   - Good standing with IRS and state authorities
   - No adverse rulings or sanctions

2. **Mission Alignment:**
   - Focus on children's welfare and development
   - Programs that support disabled or disadvantaged youth
   - Geographic alignment with platform user base where practical

3. **Financial Health:**
   - Audit-ready financial statements
   - Acceptable program expense ratio (minimum 70%)
   - No excessive executive compensation concerns

### Due Diligence Checklist:

- Verify IRS determination letter
- Review Form 990 filings for last three years
- Check Better Business Bureau Wise Giving Alliance ratings
- Verify state registration and compliance
- Contact references from other corporate donors
- Site visits for larger allocations (>$10,000 annually)

## Operational Procedures

### Monthly Revenue Tracking

1. Automated revenue aggregation from all sources
2. CFO certification of preliminary calculations
3. CEO review of methodology adherence
4. Legal review for compliance assurance

### Quarterly Disbursement Process

1. Finalize revenue calculations for preceding quarter
2. Confirm qualifying organizations remain eligible
3. Execute fund transfers with proper documentation
4. Update public transparency report
5. Archive financial records per documentation requirements

### Annual Compliance Audit

1. Engage independent CPA firm for financial review
2. Verify adherence to disbursement limitations
3. Confirm proper use of contractual revenue disbursement terminology
4. Validate charitable organization eligibility
5. Prepare formal compliance report for board

## Roles and Responsibilities

- Oversight of disbursement execution
- Coordination with external auditors
- Maintenance of financial records

### Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

- Communication with board on compliance matters
- Ensuring proper terminology usage throughout organization

### Legal Counsel

- Periodic review of regulatory changes impacting operations
- Guidance on contractual agreements with recipients
- Advice on documentation and record retention requirements
- Review of public communications for legal compliance

### Board of Directors

- Annual approval of charitable giving policies
- Quarterly review of disbursement decisions and amounts
- Oversight of external audit process
- Final authority on compliance program adequacy

## Risk Management

### Potential Compliance Violations

1. **Unauthorized Terminology Use:**
   - Prevention: Regular training sessions for team members
   - Detection: Monthly content reviews of customer-facing materials
   - Response: Immediate correction and staff retraining

2. **Revenue Calculation Errors:**
   - Prevention: Automated reconciliation processes with manual oversight
   - Detection: Independent monthly reviews by finance team
   - Response: Corrective disbursements as necessary with board notification

3. **Ineligible Recipient Payments:**
   - Prevention: Annual vetting renewal process
   - Detection: Quarterly due diligence confirmation
   - Response: Recovery of funds and policy revision as needed

### Incident Response Protocol

1. Identification of potential compliance issue
2. Immediate notification to CEO and legal counsel
3. Internal investigation and documentation
4. Remediation plan development
5. Board notification for material violations
6. External reporting as required by law
7. Post-incident process improvement implementation

## Review and Amendment Process

This protocol will be reviewed annually by the compliance committee and updated as necessary to address:

- Regulatory changes affecting operations
- Organizational structural changes
- Technological advancement in record keeping
- Feedback from audits and compliance reviews
- Industry best practices evolution

Any material changes to this protocol must be approved by the Board of Directors and communicated to all affected team members within 30 days of adoption.
