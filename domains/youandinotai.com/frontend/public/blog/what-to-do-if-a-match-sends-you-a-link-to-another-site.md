# What to do if a match sends you a link to another site

Connecting with someone on a dating app should involve direct communication. When a match sends you a link to an outside website, pause before tapping it. On February 13, 2024, the Federal Trade Commission published an article titled ["Love Stinks – when a scammer is involved"](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/02/love-stinks-when-scammer-involved). The article stated that romance fraud caused $1.14 billion in reported losses in 2023 across 64,003 reports, with a median loss of $2,000 per person. Fraud operators use outside links to direct people to external sites that harvest personal details or credentials. Learning how to handle suspicious links keeps your personal information private.

## Why a match sends you an external link

Fraudulent profiles use external links to bypass in-app protections.

1. Phishing portals: The link leads to a webpage designed to look like a legitimate login page to capture your email and password.
2. Malicious downloads: The link triggers a download of software or scripts onto your phone or computer.
3. Fake investment sites: The link points to an unverified crypto or trading platform controlled by a scam operator.
4. Adult or paid verification websites: The match claims you must verify your identity on a third-party website before meeting in person.
5. Moving off the platform: The link attempts to redirect the conversation away from moderation tools.

## What to do when a match sends you an external link

Taking immediate steps prevents data loss and stops fraud attempts.

1. Do not tap or click the link: Leave the message unopened or unclicked to avoid loading unverified web content.
2. Keep the conversation inside the app: Tell the match you only communicate directly inside the dating platform.
3. Notice the reaction: An honest person understands your boundary. A scammer argues, pressures you, or insists you open the link.
4. Never enter credentials or financial data: Close any browser window that asks for card numbers, passwords, or personal details.
5. Block and report the account: Use the platform tools to report the profile for sending unsolicited external links.

## Warning signs of link-based scams on dating apps

Certain communication habits point to deceptive accounts.

1. Fast link delivery: The account sends a link within the first few messages of matching.
2. Requests for off-app verification: The person insists on identity clearance through an unfamiliar third-party link.
3. Investment talk: The contact describes high financial returns and shares a link to join their platform.
4. Broken explanations: The match claims their camera or app does not work and sends a link to see their pictures.
5. Immediate urgency: The person demands that you click the link right away.
