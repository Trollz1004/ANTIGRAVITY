# How to tell if a match is rushing the relationship

Healthy dating develops at a comfortable pace for both people. When a match pushes for intense emotional closeness right away, it signals a need for caution. In August 2022, the [Federal Trade Commission](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-romance-scams) published guidance titled "What to Know About Romance Scams" stating that scammers profess love quickly, move conversations off dating apps, make up reasons they cannot meet in person, and ask for money. On February 13, 2024, the Federal Trade Commission published an article titled ["Love Stinks – when a scammer is involved"](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/02/love-stinks-when-scammer-involved). The article stated that romance fraud caused $1.14 billion in reported losses in 2023 across 64,003 reports, with a median loss of $2,000 per person. Recognizing the signs of rushed communication helps you keep control of your interactions.

## Why a match rushes the relationship

Deceptive accounts accelerate the pace of conversation for specific goals.

1. Creating emotional leverage: Fabricating rapid intimacy encourages people to overlook inconsistencies.
2. Bypassing critical thinking: Urgency pressures matches into making fast decisions without checking details.
3. Setting up financial requests: Scammers establish trust before asking for help with travel, fees, or emergencies.
4. Moving conversations off the app: Pushing for external phone calls or private messaging channels isolates the target from moderation tools.
5. Account urgency: Operators run unverified profiles that face removal once other users report them.

## Signs that a match is rushing the relationship

Specific patterns indicate that a conversation is moving too fast.

1. Declarations of love within days: The person expresses intense devotion, calls you a soulmate, or plans a shared future before meeting in person.
2. Pressure to leave the platform: The contact insists on personal phone numbers or external chat handles immediately after matching.
3. Demands for constant contact: The person sends continuous messages and complains if you do not reply instantly.
4. Reluctance to verify identity: The match resists scheduled live video calls or in-person meetings while claiming urgent connection.
5. Resistance to personal boundaries: The individual argues, acts hurt, or uses guilt when you ask to slow down.

## What to do when a match moves too fast

Setting clear expectations protects your peace and personal information.

1. State your pace clearly: Tell the person you take time to get to know someone before deepening contact.
2. Keep conversations on the app: Refuse to exchange phone numbers or switch to unmoderated messaging services early.
3. Watch the reaction: A genuine match respects your boundaries. A scammer reacts with anger or pressure.
4. Never send money: Do not transfer funds, share banking details, or buy gift cards for someone you met online.
5. Block and report suspicious profiles: Use platform tools to report accounts that demand money or violate your boundaries.
