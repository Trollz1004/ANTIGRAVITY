# How to report a scam account on a dating app

Encountering a suspicious profile on a dating platform is frustrating. Knowing how to report a scam account helps keep online communities cleaner. On February 2, 2023, a [Pew Research Center article](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/02/02/key-findings-about-online-dating-in-the-u-s/) reported that 52 percent of people who have used dating sites or apps say they have come across someone they think was trying to scam them. Taking quick reporting steps alerts moderators to fraudulent profiles.

## Signs that an account is a scam

Recognizing deceptive patterns helps you decide when to file a report.

1. The person asks for money, wire transfers, gift cards, or cryptocurrency investments.
2. The profile uses stolen photos or images that look altered.
3. The contact refuses phone calls or video chats while professing affection.
4. The user pushes conversation off the platform to private email or messaging apps.
5. The messages repeat generic scripts or fail to answer direct questions.

When you spot these behaviors, stop messaging and prepare a report.

## How to document evidence before reporting

Collecting key profile details helps platform moderators take action.

1. Take screenshots of the profile bio, displayed name, and account photos.
2. Capture screenshots of chat messages showing financial requests or suspicious links.
3. Record the date and time when the suspicious interaction occurred.
4. Note any external payment usernames, email addresses, or phone numbers shared.
5. Save the evidence to your device before blocking the account.

Having clear records supports platform reviews and consumer protection complaints.

## Steps to report a scam account on dating platforms

Every major dating app provides built-in tools to flag abusive or fraudulent accounts.

1. Open the profile of the suspicious account.
2. Select the menu icon or ellipsis on the profile page.
3. Choose the option to report the user.
4. Select the category for fraud, scam, or fake profile.
5. Submit your saved screenshots and details explaining the violation.
6. Use the block option to stop further contact from the user.

Submitting quick reports helps platforms review and remove fake accounts.

## Where to report scams outside the app

If a scammer attempts to steal funds, report the incident to federal agencies.

1. File a report with the Federal Trade Commission at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
2. Submit an online complaint to the FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center at ic3.gov.
3. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if you transferred money.
4. Notify local law enforcement if financial theft took place.
5. Keep copies of all reports for your personal records.

External reporting creates official records that aid consumer fraud investigations.
