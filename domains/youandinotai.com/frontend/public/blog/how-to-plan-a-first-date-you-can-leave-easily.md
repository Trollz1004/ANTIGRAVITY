# How to plan a first date you can leave easily

Meeting someone in person for the first time should feel comfortable and low-stress. A first date is simply a quick conversation to see if two people connect face to face. On February 2, 2023, a [Pew Research Center report](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/02/02/key-findings-about-online-dating-in-the-u-s/) found that 30 percent of U.S. adults have used a dating site or app. Planning a meetup that you can step away from at any point gives you peace of mind.

## Pick a daytime spot with counter service

Counter-service spots make departure simple. At a sit-down restaurant, you must wait for a server to bring the bill.

1. Choose a coffee shop, tea house, or bakery.
2. Order and pay at the counter right when you arrive.
3. Sit near an exit or in an open seating area.
4. Keep the setting public with people around you.
5. Avoid crowded venues where background noise prevents normal speaking volume.

Paying upfront means you can stand up and leave whenever you decide.

## Set a firm time limit before you meet

Agreeing on a duration in advance removes awkwardness when wrapping up.

1. State your time window before finalizing the date.
2. Suggest meeting for thirty to forty-five minutes.
3. Schedule an errand or appointment right after the date.
4. Mention that errand when you sit down.
5. Stand up and say goodbye when the agreed time arrives.

A defined finish time keeps expectations clear for both people.

## Arrange your own transportation

Relying on a date for a ride takes away your control over when you depart.

1. Drive your own car, take public transit, or call a rideshare.
2. Park in a well-lit area close to the venue entrance.
3. Keep your keys and phone in your pocket or small bag.
4. Decline offers from your date to pick you up at your home.
5. Keep your personal vehicle location private.

Independent transit ensures you can leave on your own schedule.

## How to exit politely

You do not need a complicated excuse to end a date. Polite honesty works best.

1. Thank the person for taking the time to meet.
2. Say that your scheduled time has ended and you need to head out.
3. If you do not feel a connection, wish them well directly.
4. Walk to your vehicle or transit stop on your own.
5. If someone acts pushy, step to the counter staff or leave immediately.

Clear boundaries keep dating straightforward and respectful.
