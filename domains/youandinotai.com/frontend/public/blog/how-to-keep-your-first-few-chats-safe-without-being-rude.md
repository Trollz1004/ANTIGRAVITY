# How to keep your first few chats safe without being rude

Starting a conversation with a new match should feel enjoyable. People want to stay safe while also sounding friendly. In August 2022, the [Federal Trade Commission](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-romance-scams) published guidance titled "What to Know About Romance Scams" stating that scammers profess love quickly, move conversations off dating apps, make up reasons they cannot meet in person, and ask for money. On February 13, 2024, the Federal Trade Commission published an article titled ["Love Stinks – when a scammer is involved"](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/02/love-stinks-when-scammer-involved). The article stated that romance fraud caused $1.14 billion in reported losses in 2023 across 64,003 reports, with a median loss of $2,000 per person. You can protect your personal information while keeping your tone polite and open.

## Boundaries that keep early chats safe

Simple personal rules prevent problems in early messages. You do not need to explain your boundaries.

1. Keep messages on the dating app until you decide to meet.
2. Protect your personal contact details, including phone numbers and email addresses.
3. Keep your home address, neighborhood streets, and workplace private.
4. Refuse requests for financial help, gifts, or investment advice.
5. Set your own pace for replies rather than answering pressure for instant messages.

## Polite ways to decline requests in early chats

You can say no clearly without sounding harsh. Direct statements keep conversations respectful.

1. When asked for your phone number right away: "I prefer to chat here on the app until we know each other better."
2. When asked about your workplace or home area: "I keep details about my job and neighborhood private for now."
3. When asked to switch to an outside chat app: "I like keeping our messages here on the platform."
4. When asked personal financial questions: "I do not discuss personal finances or investments on dating apps."
5. When pressured to hurry: "I like taking time to get to know someone."

A genuine match will respect your reply. A dishonest profile will argue, push back, or act offended.

## Warning signs in early conversations

Watch for specific behaviors that indicate a fake profile or automated account.

1. The contact professes strong romantic feelings in the first few messages.
2. The person pushes you to switch to outside messaging services immediately.
3. The match tells stories about personal financial emergencies or travel problems.
4. The profile uses only one image or resists live video verification.
5. The person becomes angry or critical when you set a simple boundary.

## What to do if a match refuses to respect your boundaries

When an account ignores your preferences, take action to protect your account.

1. Stop replying to demanding messages.
2. Refuse to justify your limits or argue.
3. Keep records of suspicious requests or payment demands.
4. Block and report profiles that violate platform rules or demand money.
5. Focus your time on matches who respect your pace.
