# How to ask a match for a video chat before meeting

Meeting a match in person is an exciting step in online dating. Asking for a brief video chat first helps you verify who you are talking to before you plan an in-person date. On February 9, 2023, a [Federal Trade Commission Data Spotlight](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2023/02/romance-scammers-favorite-lies-exposed) reported that nearly 70,000 consumers reported losing $1.3 billion to romance scams in 2022. The publication noted that scammers build excuses not to meet into their profiles, such as claiming to work on a faraway military base or an offshore oil rig. A quick video conversation confirms that your match looks like their photos and communicates genuinely.

## Why a video chat before meeting makes sense

A short video chat gives both people peace of mind before coordinating an in-person meeting.

1. Seeing a match live confirms their photos represent who they are today.
2. A brief video call helps you hear vocal tone and gauge conversational comfort.
3. Live interaction removes doubts about automated scripts or stolen profile pictures.
4. Setting up a short check-in saves travel time and energy for both individuals.
5. Sincere matches understand the desire for mutual reassurance before meeting in person.

Taking five or ten minutes for a video greeting establishes comfort early in the dating process.

## How to ask for a video chat politely

You do not need an elaborate explanation to ask for a video chat. Keep your invitation simple, clear, and low pressure.

1. "I enjoy our conversation and would like to do a quick five-minute video greeting before we plan to meet up. Would you be open to that?"
2. "Before we pick a spot for coffee, I like to do a short video chat to say hello. Does some time this week work for you?"
3. "I always do a quick video call with matches before our first in-person date. Are you free for a ten-minute chat later today?"
4. "I look forward to meeting you. Let us do a brief video hello first so we can introduce ourselves."
5. "To help both of us feel comfortable before meeting, let us hop on a quick video call sometime tomorrow."

Direct wording sets a positive tone and establishes your personal boundaries clearly.

## How to handle excuses or hesitation

Authentic singles understand that safety and verification matter to everyone. How a match reacts to your request tells you useful information about them.

1. Real people might feel shy at first, but they will work with you to arrange a convenient time.
2. If a match expresses camera shyness, suggest a very brief three-minute wave and greeting.
3. Scammers frequently make excuses about broken webcams, sudden hospital trips, or restrictive work rules.
4. If a match becomes defensive or hostile when you ask to video chat, pause contact.
5. If someone flatly refuses every live video check while pushing for funds, stop communicating immediately.

A person who respects you will respect your decision to verify your connection first.
