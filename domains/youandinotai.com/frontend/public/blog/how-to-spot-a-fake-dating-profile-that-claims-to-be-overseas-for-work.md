# How to spot a fake dating profile that claims to be overseas for work

Online dating connects people across cities and backgrounds. When a match claims to work overseas, evaluate the profile carefully before building an attachment. On February 9, 2023, a [Federal Trade Commission Data Spotlight reported](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2023/02/romance-scammers-favorite-lies-exposed) that nearly 70,000 consumers filed romance scam reports in 2022, with losses reaching $1.3 billion. The report noted that scammers build excuses not to meet into their profiles, such as claiming to work on a faraway military base or an offshore oil rig. Recognizing these profile claims helps you identify accounts that avoid meeting in person.

## Common overseas job claims on fake profiles

Profiles that avoid in-person meetings rely on specific remote professions.

1. Military deployments: The profile claims active service on a distant military installation where leaving the base is forbidden.
2. Offshore oil drilling: The profile lists work as a marine engineer, technician, or contractor on an ocean rig without internet stability.
3. International medical aid: The profile describes volunteer surgical or medical work for an overseas humanitarian group.
4. Overseas construction projects: The profile claims an engineering contract building roads, bridges, or facilities abroad.
5. Foreign business travel: The profile claims an extended international buying trip or import-export assignment that delays their return.

These job descriptions provide a ready excuse whenever you suggest a local meetup.

## Warning signs in messages from overseas profiles

Communication from fake overseas accounts follows predictable patterns.

1. Immediate romantic declarations: The account expresses deep affection within days of matching, before establishing real mutual knowledge.
2. Refusal of live video chats: The contact claims weak satellite signals, broken camera hardware, or security regulations prohibit video calls.
3. Fast requests to leave the app: The account urges you to switch to external messaging applications or email.
4. Urgent financial emergencies: The profile asks for funds to cover medical care, customs clearances, travel fees, or replacement equipment.
5. Requests for untraceable payments: The contact asks for gift cards, wire transfers, or cryptocurrency payments to solve their overseas problems.

A genuine match handles their own travel and work expenses without asking an online contact for money.

## Steps to take when an overseas profile contacts you

Simple precautions protect your time and emotional energy.

1. Suggest a short live video greeting before continuing deep personal conversations.
2. Search the profile photo using an image search tool to check if the picture belongs to someone else.
3. Decline every request to send money, gift cards, or financial details.
4. Keep all messages inside the dating application where moderation tools exist.
5. Block and report any account that introduces emergency expenses into the discussion.

Taking these steps keeps your attention focused on sincere people who can meet locally.
