# How to know when to stop talking to a match

Chatting with a new match on a dating app should feel engaging and straightforward. Sometimes a conversation takes an uncomfortable turn or shows clear signs of dishonesty. In August 2022, the [Federal Trade Commission](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-romance-scams) published consumer guidance on romance scams. The guidance states that scammers create fake profiles to build relationships, invent reasons why they cannot meet in person, and ask for money. The Federal Trade Commission advises people to never send money to someone they have not met in person and to stop communication when warning signs appear. Recognizing when to step away saves your time and keeps your personal information private.

## Clear warning signs that mean it is time to walk away

Pay attention to sudden demands and broken promises during early conversations.

1. The match asks for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or banking assistance.
2. The person refuses a voice call, video call, or public in-person meetup while giving elaborate excuses.
3. The conversation moves unnaturally fast toward declarations of deep love within days.
4. The contact pushes you aggressively to leave the app for private chat channels.
5. The profile details change or contradict statements made in previous messages.

## Behavioral signals of bots and scripted profiles

Automated accounts and scripted operators repeat specific patterns when messaging targets.

1. The replies arrive with instant speed and ignore your specific questions.
2. The messages contain awkward phrasing, broken syntax, or generic greetings.
3. The sender sends unsolicited links to third-party websites or external verification portals.
4. The account asks repetitive questions that disregard what you wrote earlier.
5. The photos look like professional modeling shots or stock images found online.

## Practical steps to end the conversation

Take decisive action to protect your peace of mind and your digital security.

1. Stop responding immediately once a clear boundary is crossed.
2. Do not argue, explain, or attempt to investigate the account yourself.
3. Keep records of suspicious links or payment demands before closing the chat.
4. Use the platform block feature to prevent the account from contacting you again.
5. Report the profile to platform moderators so they can review the activity.
