# How to tell if a match keeps changing their story

Dating online requires honest and consistent communication between matches. Learning how to tell if a match keeps changing their story helps you spot deceit and fake profiles early. On February 13, 2024, the [Federal Trade Commission](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/02/love-stinks-when-scammer-involved) reported that romance scams resulted in $1.14 billion in reported losses in 2023 across 64,003 reports, with a median loss of $2,000 per person. People running scams invent complex personas and struggle to keep their fabricated details straight. Tracking contradictions in basic facts protects your time and emotional energy.

## Common inconsistencies in fabricated stories

Fabricated identities unravel when details shift across conversations.

1. The person gives conflicting answers about their job title, daily schedule, or workplace location.
2. The match changes where they grew up, where they currently live, or their family background.
3. Personal milestones such as age, graduation year, or relationship history do not align.
4. The contact forgets previous statements and provides conflicting explanations when asked.
5. The person contradicts themselves regarding why they cannot meet in person or join a phone call.

## Why dishonest matches change their details

Inconsistencies emerge when an account operates under false pretenses.

1. Fake accounts balance conversations with dozens of targets and mix up personal details.
2. Multiple individuals take turns operating the same scam profile on alternating shifts.
3. The match relies on scripted templates rather than authentic life experiences.
4. The person invents urgent personal crises to test your sympathy and financial generosity.
5. The individual uses false excuses to deflect direct questions about their identity.

## Steps to take when stories do not match

You can take direct steps when you spot conflicting claims in messages.

1. Ask for clarification on contradictory statements and observe how the match responds.
2. Note defensive reactions, deflections, or accusations when you point out inconsistent facts.
3. Propose a brief video chat or a phone call to confirm the person matches their profile.
4. Stop communicating if the match gives evasive answers or continues to alter basic details.
5. Use built-in reporting tools to alert platform moderation teams to suspicious behavior.
