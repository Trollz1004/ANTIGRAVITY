# How to stay safe when meeting a match at night

Meeting an online match in person is an important step. When you schedule an evening date, taking practical precautions helps you protect your personal safety and avoid fake accounts. On February 2, 2023, a [Pew Research Center report](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/02/02/key-findings-about-online-dating-in-the-u-s/) found that 52 percent of people who have used dating sites or apps say they have come across someone they think was trying to scam them. Clear safety habits keep evening meetups secure and predictable.

## Choose a busy public venue with bright lighting

Public locations ensure other people are present throughout your date.

1. Select a populated restaurant, café, or well-lit bowling alley.
2. Avoid secluded parks, dark parking lots, or private residences.
3. Confirm that the business stays open for the entire duration of your meetup.
4. Sit in an open dining area near staff members or the main counter.
5. If your match asks to change the location to an isolated spot, decline and keep your original plan.

Public spaces provide visibility and natural boundaries.

## Arrange independent transportation for your arrival and departure

Controlling your own transportation ensures you can leave whenever you choose.

1. Drive your personal vehicle, use a bus line, or call a rideshare service.
2. Park in an open lot under direct street lighting.
3. Keep your car keys and phone in your pocket.
4. Decline offers from your match to pick you up at your home address.
5. Do not accept a ride home at the end of the evening.

Traveling independently keeps your home address private and allows you to leave on your own timeline.

## Share your date details with a trusted contact

Letting someone know your schedule creates a reliable safety checkpoint.

1. Send the venue name and address to a trusted friend or family member.
2. Provide your contact with the name and profile photo of the person you plan to meet.
3. Establish a specific check-in time by phone call or text message.
4. Set an alarm on your phone to remind you to send your check-in update.
5. Inform your contact when you arrive home safely.

A designated check-in contact ensures someone knows where you are.

## Watch for scam behavior and sudden requests

Bots and dishonest profiles can push for urgent personal information or financial favors.

1. Decline requests to send money, wire funds, or purchase gift cards.
2. Keep conversations on the platform until you have met face to face.
3. Refuse requests to move to unmonitored channels before you verify the person.
4. Leave immediately if someone pressures you to share sensitive personal details.
5. Use built-in reporting tools if an account acts suspiciously.

Firm boundaries protect your privacy and reduce exposure to fraudulent profiles.
