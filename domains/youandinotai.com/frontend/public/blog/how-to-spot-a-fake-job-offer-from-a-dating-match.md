# How to spot a fake job offer from a dating match

Dating apps connect individuals seeking personal relationships. Some fraudulent profiles use dating conversations to pitch employment schemes instead of romance. On December 12, 2024, the [Federal Trade Commission](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2024/12/paying-get-paid-gamified-job-scams-drive-record-losses) published a Data Spotlight titled "Paying to get paid: gamified job scams drive record losses". The report stated that reported losses to job scams topped $220 million in the first half of 2024. The report stated that about 20,000 people reported gamified task scams in the first six months of 2024. Recognizing false employment pitches helps you protect your finances and personal data.

## Why scammers pitch jobs on dating platforms

Fraud operators use dating profiles to lower your defenses before pitching a scheme.

1. Established trust: The match spends days chatting before mentioning a work opportunity.
2. Emotional connection: The contact frames the offer as a way to help you earn extra income.
3. Separation from standard hiring: The individual bypasses formal job boards and corporate interviews.
4. Remote work claims: The person describes simple online tasks that require no specific experience.
5. High promised payouts: The match claims quick earnings for simple daily tasks.

## Warning signs of a fake job offer from a match

False employment pitches follow distinct patterns in private chat.

1. Immediate job openings: The match offers employment without asking for a resume or conducting a video interview.
2. Advance payment demands: The contact asks you to pay upfront fees for training, starter kits, or equipment.
3. Fake check deposits: The person sends a check, tells you to deposit it, and instructs you to wire money elsewhere.
4. Requests for sensitive identity data: The contact asks for your Social Security number or bank login before any formal agreement.
5. Task completion deposits: The match directs you to a website where you must deposit funds to unlock tasks or withdraw earnings.

A genuine employer does not ask job applicants to pay money to receive wages.

## Steps to take when a match offers employment

Taking clear actions stops employment fraud quickly.

1. Decline every request to send funds, deposit checks, or buy cryptocurrency for a job.
2. Refuse to provide bank account details or government identification numbers in chat.
3. Search the company name online alongside words such as scam or complaint.
4. Stop messaging any profile that shifts a dating conversation toward employment or investment tasks.
5. Use built-in platform options to block and report the account.
