# How to spot a romance scam that starts with a wrong-number text

An unexpected text message from a stranger can be the start of a romance scam. On February 13, 2024, the [Federal Trade Commission](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/02/love-stinks-when-scammer-involved) reported that romance scams resulted in $1.14 billion in reported losses in 2023 across 64,003 reports, with a median loss of $2,000 per person. Scammers send messages that look like accidental texts to initiate contact and start a conversation. Knowing the signs of this tactic helps you recognize fraudulent messages before sharing personal details.

## How the wrong-number text scam starts

A scammer creates a friendly opening that looks like an honest error.

1. You receive a text asking about dinner plans, travel schedules, golf games, or coffee meetings.
2. The sender uses a greeting addressed to an unfamiliar name.
3. You reply to state that the sender reached the wrong number.
4. The sender sends an apology and remarks that you seem like a kind person.
5. The sender continues messaging to ask about your location, job, or daily life.

## Why scammers use wrong-number texts to build romance scams

Scammers use accidental introductions to establish rapport outside regular platforms.

1. A wrong-number text bypasses dating app profile verification and reporting tools.
2. Replying to the message confirms to the sender that your phone number is active.
3. The sender attempts to transition from an accidental conversation to daily personal chat.
4. The scammer sends photos of an attractive person to generate emotional interest.
5. The contact creates a fake persona to build trust before introducing requests for money or investment schemes.

## Steps to take when you receive a suspicious text

Take straightforward actions to protect your phone number and personal information.

1. Do not reply to unexpected text messages from unknown senders.
2. Do not click links contained in unsolicited text messages.
3. Block the telephone number on your device using built-in phone settings.
4. Forward suspicious spam text messages to 7726 (SPAM) to notify your mobile carrier.
5. Report the fraudulent text message to the Federal Trade Commission at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov).

## Warning signs that an accidental text is a scam

Look for specific patterns that reveal an organized fraud attempt.

1. The sender refuses to end the exchange after you state they reached the wrong person.
2. The sender shares attractive portraits and asks for your photograph in return.
3. The contact quickly shifts to flattery, romantic declarations, or terms of endearment.
4. The person invents excuses why a phone call or in-person meeting cannot occur.
5. The contact mentions cryptocurrency trading, foreign exchange platforms, or financial emergencies.
