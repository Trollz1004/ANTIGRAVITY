# What to do if a match asks for your phone number right away

Connecting with a new match can feel exciting. Sometimes a match asks for your phone number in their very first message. On February 2, 2023, the [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/02/02/key-findings-about-online-dating-in-the-u-s/) reported that 52 percent of people who have used dating sites or apps say they have come across someone they think was trying to scam them. Sharing private contact details too fast can expose your personal information to unverified accounts.

## Why a match asks for your phone number immediately

People ask for a phone number early for different reasons.

1. Unverified accounts try to pull you off the platform before moderators flag their profile.
2. Scammers want your phone number to search public directories or send phishing links.
3. Automated scripts collect active numbers to resell to spam call centers.
4. An impatient match may dislike typing inside the app.
5. Some people mistakenly believe switching to text shows greater interest.

## Steps to take when asked for your number early

You can keep control of the conversation with simple boundaries.

1. Keep your conversation inside the dating app while you get to know each other.
2. Tell the match politely that you prefer chatting on the app for now.
3. Observe whether the match respects your boundary or pushes back.
4. Refuse requests to switch to external chat apps, personal email, or direct phone calls.
5. Stop chatting if the match reacts with anger, guilt, or pressure.

## Signs of a fake profile or automated account

Notice how the other person communicates before sharing details about your life.

1. The profile features only one photo or looks like a stock model picture.
2. The initial message feels generic and ignores your profile details.
3. The match professes deep romantic feelings within hours of matching.
4. The person claims they are about to leave town, deploy overseas, or delete the app.
5. The account requests money, investment help, or gift cards.
