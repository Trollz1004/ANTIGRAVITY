# How to avoid dating app gift card scams and romance fraud

Dating app gift card scams target singles searching for genuine connections online. Fraud operators set up fake profiles to earn trust and request gift cards under false pretenses. On February 9, 2023, a [Federal Trade Commission Data Spotlight](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2023/02/romance-scammers-favorite-lies-exposed) reported that 24 percent of people who reported losing money to a romance scam in 2022 said it was taken using gift cards. The publication also noted a median individual reported loss of $700 when gift cards were used. Recognizing these tactics helps people spot fraud early.

## Why scammers ask for gift cards

Fraud operators prefer gift cards because they function like cash.

1. Gift card transactions do not require personal banking details.
2. Funds can be drained instantly once the card number and PIN are shared.
3. Card purchases are difficult for payment providers to reverse.
4. Operators can resell card codes quickly on digital exchanges.
5. The transaction leaves minimal contact information to trace the operator.

Once card details leave your hands, the funds cannot be recovered.

## Common excuses used to request cards

Scammers invent urgent crises to pressure matches into buying gift cards. Watch for these common scripts.

1. The match claims a broken phone or laptop prevents communication without an immediate gift card.
2. The person claims unexpected hospital bills or prescription costs require rapid assistance.
3. The match asks for cards to pay for travel tickets or fuel to visit you in person.
4. The person claims customs fees or delivery charges block an incoming personal package.
5. The match asks you to buy gaming or entertainment cards for a dependent relative.

Legitimate matches do not ask online dates to cover emergency bills with gift cards.

## Steps to take if a match asks for gift cards

Firm boundaries protect your personal finances while dating online.

1. Refuse every request to purchase, photograph, or send gift card codes.
2. Stop messaging any profile that asks for financial support or gift cards.
3. Keep conversations on the dating platform until you meet in person.
4. Block and report suspicious accounts using built-in platform tools.
5. Report fraud attempts to card issuers and consumer protection agencies.
