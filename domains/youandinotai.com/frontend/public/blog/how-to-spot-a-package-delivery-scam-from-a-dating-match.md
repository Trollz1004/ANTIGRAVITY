# How to spot a package delivery scam from a dating match

Online dating introduces people across different cities and backgrounds. When an online connection offers to ship a gift to your home, examine the situation before accepting. On February 9, 2023, a [Federal Trade Commission Data Spotlight](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2023/02/romance-scammers-favorite-lies-exposed) reported that nearly 70,000 consumers filed romance scam reports in 2022, with reported losses reaching $1.3 billion. The publication listed lies told by romance scammers, including requests for help with an important delivery. Recognizing how shipping schemes operate helps you identify fraudulent accounts before sending money.

## How the delivery scam works

Shipping fraud follows a structured sequence designed to extract money from an online target.

1. The match claims they bought a valuable gift for you, such as jewelry, electronics, or family heirlooms.
2. The match says customs officials or delivery couriers held the package at an international border.
3. A person claiming to work for a shipping agency contacts you by text or email.
4. The contact demands payment for customs clearance fees, import duties, or storage charges before delivery.
5. The match urges you to pay the fee so the parcel will arrive at your home.

The promised package does not exist. The contact who demands payment works directly with the scammer.

## Warning signs of shipping fraud

Dishonest profiles exhibit distinct signs when arranging fake deliveries.

1. The match sends a parcel before meeting you in person.
2. The delivery service uses an unfamiliar name and an unofficial email address.
3. The contact demands payment through gift cards, wire transfers, or cryptocurrency.
4. The sender insists they cannot pay the shipping charges from their own account.
5. The contact warns of legal trouble if you refuse to pay the fee.

Legitimate delivery companies do not request untraceable payment methods to complete a delivery.

## Steps to take when a match asks for delivery money

Straightforward precautions protect your personal funds and peace of mind.

1. Decline every request to pay delivery fees, import duties, or transit taxes.
2. Recognize that genuine couriers do not require gift cards or wire transfers.
3. Avoid sharing your home address with people you know only through messages.
4. Keep communication inside the dating app so platform safety tools remain active.
5. Block and report any account that requests financial assistance.

These habits keep your attention on genuine people who respect your personal boundaries.
