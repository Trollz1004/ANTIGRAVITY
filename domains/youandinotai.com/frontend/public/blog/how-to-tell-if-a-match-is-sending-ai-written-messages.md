# How to tell if a match is sending AI-written messages

Chatting on a dating app should involve real people sharing real thoughts. Some profiles use automated tools and language models to generate chat replies. Learning how to tell if a match is sending AI-written messages helps you spot automated interactions early.

## Signs that messages come from an automated tool

Automated chat generators display distinct messaging traits.

1. The text reads like an essay with formal transitions, perfect punctuation, and structured paragraphs.
2. The messages offer grand compliments while ignoring specific details in your profile pictures or bio.
3. The replies arrive within seconds and contain several long paragraphs.
4. The sender answers direct questions with generic summaries that avoid personal memories or specific opinions.
5. The text repeats phrases from your previous message without adding genuine human reactions.

## Questions to test for a real conversational partner

You can ask specific questions to see if your match writes their own replies.

1. Ask about a specific street, neighborhood park, or local diner in their stated city.
2. Ask how they felt about an unusual hobby or what they cooked for dinner yesterday.
3. Share a short typo or casual phrase and see if the reply understands the informal tone.
4. Ask a two-part question that connects a recent local event with a personal preference.
5. Suggest exchanging a brief voice note to hear their voice and cadence.

## Steps to take when messages seem automated

You can take action to protect your time when a conversation feels artificial.

1. Stop spending time typing detailed answers to generic paragraphs.
2. Do not click links to external chat platforms, investment websites, or verification portals.
3. Ask directly for a phone call or a video chat before continuing the conversation.
4. Use the in-app reporting tool to alert moderators to suspicious automated messaging.
5. Block the profile so the account cannot send you further messages.
