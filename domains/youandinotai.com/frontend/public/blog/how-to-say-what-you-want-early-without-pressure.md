# How to say what you want early without pressure

Stating what you want early in online dating saves time and prevents misunderstandings. Real matches appreciate honest intentions. On February 2, 2023, a [Pew Research Center article](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/02/02/key-findings-about-online-dating-in-the-u-s/) reported that 52 percent of people who have used dating sites or apps say they have come across someone they think was trying to scam them. Clear communication helps you identify serious members while weeding out bots and fraudulent profiles.

## Why clarity early on prevents wasted time

Setting expectations early establishes comfort and direction.

1. You discover if you and your match share similar relationship goals.
2. Honest statements remove guesswork from early messaging.
3. You avoid spending weeks chatting with someone seeking a different outcome.
4. Clear intentions filter out automated accounts that cannot hold a real conversation.
5. You protect your emotional energy by dating with purpose.

Clear words keep dating straightforward and transparent.

## Simple phrases to share what you want

You can express your goals without making demands on the other person.

1. "I am looking for a long-term relationship, but I like taking things one step at a time."
2. "I am dating with the goal of finding a partner, and I prefer getting to know someone at a relaxed pace."
3. "Right now I am interested in casual dating and meeting new people."
4. "I value open communication and want to see if we share mutual interests first."
5. "Here is what I am hoping to find, but there is zero pressure if your goals are different."

These statements share your perspective without imposing expectations.

## How bots and scammers react when you state your goals

Dishonest accounts struggle when you ask direct questions or state personal limits.

1. Automated bots ignore your goals and paste generic pickup lines.
2. Scammers profess instant love regardless of what you say you want.
3. Deceptive accounts steer the conversation toward personal finances or investments.
4. Fake profiles rush to move the conversation to external chat apps.
5. Inauthentic users avoid discussing local plans or realistic meeting ideas.

A real person listens to your goals and responds thoughtfully.

## Ways to keep conversations low-pressure

Sharing your preferences works best when you keep the tone light and welcoming.

1. Frame your goals around what you want rather than what the other person must provide.
2. Give your match space to reply in their own time.
3. Acknowledge that different dating goals are completely valid.
4. Keep early chats focused on shared values and everyday topics.
5. Remember that stepping away is normal if your goals do not align.

Low-pressure honesty creates room for genuine connection.
