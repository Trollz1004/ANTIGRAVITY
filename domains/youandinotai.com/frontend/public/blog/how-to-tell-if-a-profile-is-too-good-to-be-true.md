# How to tell if a profile is too good to be true

A new match can look appealing on paper. Some accounts present pictures and descriptions that seem too good to be true. On February 13, 2024, a [Federal Trade Commission Business Blog post](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/02/love-stinks-when-scammer-involved) stated that romance fraud caused $1.14 billion in reported losses in 2023 across 64,003 reports. Fraud operators create profiles with polished details to attract attention. Learning the signs of an unrealistic profile helps you protect your time.

## Examine the profile photos

Photos provide direct clues about the authenticity of an account.

1. Every picture looks like a professional catalog shoot with studio lights.
2. The account displays only one photo or repeats the same angle with minor changes.
3. The background architecture or street signage conflicts with the person's listed town.
4. The pictures show an extravagant lifestyle of sports cars, luxury yachts, and private jets without genuine context.
5. Visual distortions appear along hands, hair strands, accessories, or background textures.

Genuine members share ordinary pictures taken across regular settings.

## Check the bio and lifestyle claims

Unrealistic accounts pair glamorous photos with grand written claims.

1. The profile lists high-status careers such as international finance executive, high-fashion model, or overseas contractor.
2. The bio claims perfection across every personal trait and promises an instant soulmate connection.
3. The text avoids naming specific hobbies, favorite dishes, or local weekend activities.
4. The profile includes contact handles for external messaging platforms in the text.
5. The stated age does not match the visual age shown in the photographs.

Specific local details show that a person lives in the community.

## Watch how the match communicates

A conversation reveals how a match handles normal interaction.

1. The person sends instant long paragraphs of flattery after your first message.
2. The match avoids answering questions about local parks, streets, or cafes.
3. The person makes excuses to avoid a five-minute video call or a public meeting.
4. The match claims a sudden financial emergency and asks for funds, gift cards, or crypto investments.
5. The account holder pressures you to move to a private messaging application right away.

Honest matches communicate at a reasonable pace and respect your boundaries.
