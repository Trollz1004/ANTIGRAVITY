# What to say when a match asks for a favor before you meet

Connecting with an online match should lead to an easy conversation and a plan to meet in person. When a match asks for a favor before meeting, pause and evaluate the request. On February 13, 2024, the Federal Trade Commission published an article titled ["Love Stinks – when a scammer is involved"](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/02/love-stinks-when-scammer-involved). The article stated that romance fraud caused $1.14 billion in reported losses in 2023 across 64,003 reports, with a median loss of $2,000 per person. Dishonest profiles and scammers test boundaries by asking for small favors before asking for larger sums. Knowing clear phrases to use helps you maintain boundaries.

## Why a match asks for a favor before meeting

Dishonest accounts use favors to create an artificial sense of intimacy or test compliance.

1. The person wants to see if you will agree to small requests before asking for money.
2. The operator creates a manufactured emergency to make an excuse not to meet in person.
3. The match requests personal assistance like receiving a package or cashing a check to involve you in fraud.
4. The contact asks for gift cards, phone reloads, or small transfers under the guise of temporary hardship.
5. The account attempts to make you feel responsible for their personal comfort.

A genuine match respects your boundaries and handles personal obligations through their own family, friends, or local resources.

## Clear phrases to use when a match asks for a favor

Direct and polite phrases establish your boundaries without debate.

1. "I do not do personal or financial favors for people I have not met in person."
2. "I prefer to keep our conversations focused on getting to know each other."
3. "I cannot assist with personal errands, payments, or account requests."
4. "That sounds like something you need to resolve through your local bank or family."
5. "I am stepping away from this conversation because I am not comfortable with this request."

Keep your response brief. Do not apologize or negotiate your personal limits.

## What to do if the match persists

How a person reacts to your refusal provides clear evidence of their intentions.

1. Stop replying if the match tries to guilt-trip, insult, or pressure you.
2. Do not offer alternative forms of help or money.
3. Keep the conversation inside the dating application where moderation exists.
4. Use the platform block feature to stop further messages immediately.
5. Report the profile so the platform can review the account.
