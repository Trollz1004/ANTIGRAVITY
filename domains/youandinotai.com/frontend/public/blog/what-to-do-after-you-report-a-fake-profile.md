# What to do after you report a fake profile

Reporting a suspicious account on a dating app is an important step. Taking the right actions after filing that report helps protect your personal information. On February 13, 2024, the Federal Trade Commission published an article titled ["Love Stinks – when a scammer is involved"](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/02/love-stinks-when-scammer-involved). The article stated that romance fraud caused $1.14 billion in reported losses in 2023 across 64,003 reports, with a median loss of $2,000 per person. Following up after a report reduces the risk of further unwanted contact.

## Block the account across all channels

Filing a report inside an app does not always block contact automatically.

1. Use the built-in block tool to prevent the profile from sending further messages.
2. If the person reached out through text or phone, block the phone number on your device.
3. If conversation moved to third-party messaging apps, block and report the account on those services.
4. Avoid sending a final message explaining why you reported the account.
5. End all communication immediately.

Cutting off contact stops the account operator from attempting new manipulation tactics.

## Preserve documentation of the incident

Keeping a record of the encounter helps if external authorities investigate fraud.

1. Store screenshots of profile photos, usernames, and written bios.
2. Keep records of chat logs that show requests for money or suspicious external links.
3. Record any payment usernames, bank account details, or wire instructions shared by the account.
4. Save the exact date and time of the interactions.
5. Keep these records stored in a secure folder on your device.

Clear records provide evidence for platform review teams and law enforcement agencies.

## Review your account security and privacy

Fake accounts sometimes gather personal details before a report is filed.

1. If you shared an email address or username, check that account for unusual password reset requests.
2. Review your social media profiles to confirm privacy settings hide your friends list and location.
3. Never use the same password across dating accounts and email services.
4. Enable two-factor authentication on your primary email and phone accounts.
5. Remove sensitive personal details such as workplace names or home neighborhoods from public bios.

Updating your privacy settings prevents bad actors from finding you on other platforms.

## File external reports if financial theft occurred

If the interaction involved financial demands or stolen funds, submit reports to regulatory agencies.

1. Report the incident to the Federal Trade Commission at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
2. File a complaint with the FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center at ic3.gov.
3. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if funds were transferred.
4. Report identity theft to IdentityTheft.gov if government identification details were shared.
5. File a report with local police if financial theft took place.

Filing official complaints creates documented records that support fraud investigations.
