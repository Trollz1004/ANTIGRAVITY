# How to tell if a match is a real person

Connecting with a new match online should lead to honest conversation. Knowing how to tell if a match is a real person saves time and emotional energy. On February 9, 2023, the [Federal Trade Commission reported](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/02/new-ftc-data-reveals-top-lies-told-romance-scammers) that nearly 70,000 consumers filed romance scam reports in 2022, with losses reaching $1.3 billion. Scammers and automated operations often copy photos and rely on scripted messages to trick singles. Checking for simple communication signals helps you confirm whether a match is authentic.

## Watch the timing and flow of messages

Automated scripts and copy-paste operators follow rigid messaging habits. Paying attention to response patterns gives you quick clues.

1. Watch how fast replies arrive after you send a message. Instant long paragraphs often signal scripted tools.
2. Notice whether the match answers your exact questions. Automated accounts often ignore specific details and send generic compliments.
3. Check if the conversation shifts immediately to an off-platform messaging application or phone number.
4. Look for awkward repetitions where the match repeats introductory greetings after you already replied.
5. Notice whether the match asks for money, gift cards, or investment opportunities. Real matches do not ask for financial favors.

Direct and thoughtful answers show that someone is reading your words.

## Ask specific local questions

Real people can describe their local surroundings with ease. Asking about nearby places helps verify their location.

1. Ask about their favorite neighborhood coffee shop or park for a weekend walk.
2. Mention a well-known local landmark or current weather event in your city.
3. Ask about their commute or a popular local street.
4. Listen for vague answers that avoid naming real places in your community.
5. Suggest a brief in-person coffee meetup in a busy public venue.

A match living in your area will recognize local references and discuss realistic plans.

## Request a short voice or video exchange

Real matches are comfortable with live communication before meeting in person. Scammers avoid real-time voice or video.

1. Suggest a five-minute video call to say hello before scheduling a date.
2. Send a short voice note and see if the match sends one back.
3. Pay attention if a match makes repeated excuses about broken phone cameras or bad connections.
4. Notice if their live voice matches the background details on their profile.
5. Trust your judgment if someone refuses every form of live interaction.

A short video or voice chat confirms the person behind the screen in minutes.
