# How to keep a conversation going without small talk

Repetitive small talk makes dating conversations feel like chores. Exchanging simple greetings like "how are you" or "how was your day" often leads to stalled chats. On February 9, 2023, a [Federal Trade Commission Data Spotlight](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2023/02/romance-scammers-favorite-lies-exposed) reported that nearly 70,000 consumers reported romance scams in 2022, with total reported losses of $1.3 billion. Automated accounts and scammers rely on vague pleasantries because generic greetings require no effort. Focusing on specific topics helps you bypass small talk and connect with real people.

## Why small talk stalls online conversations

Small talk rarely leads to memorable interactions. Understanding why it fails helps you choose better discussion topics.

1. Generic questions put the work of creating an interesting topic on the other person.
2. Scripted bots and dishonest accounts use vague pleasantries to hide that they have not read your profile.
3. Repetitive small talk fails to show what someone actually values or how they spend their time.
4. One-word answers create dead ends that drain enthusiasm.
5. Surface chit-chat makes it hard to tell whether you share common interests.

Shifting to concrete subjects gives both people something real to discuss.

## Five ways to skip small talk and connect

Direct questions about specific experiences create engaging conversations.

1. Ask about a specific detail mentioned in their bio, such as a favorite trail, book, or hobby.
2. Ask for a recommendation, such as their favorite local restaurant or a recent podcast they enjoyed.
3. Share a brief observation about your day and ask how they approach similar situations.
4. Discuss shared interests, such as cooking projects, music events, or neighborhood activities.
5. Ask open-ended questions about what projects or activities they are currently excited about.

Concrete questions encourage thoughtful replies and make conversations flow naturally.

## How to spot scripted or scam replies

Dishonest accounts often follow predictable patterns during early conversations.

1. A match ignores your specific question and sends a pre-written greeting or flattering comment.
2. The account asks to switch immediately to an outside messaging platform or private email address.
3. The match tells urgent emotional stories or asks for financial assistance.
4. The replies feel vague and never reference local details or your profile content.
5. The profile avoids phone calls or audio chats while repeating generic compliments.

Recognizing these warning signs helps you identify insincere accounts early.

## Moving from conversation to an in-person meeting

The goal of chatting is to determine whether you want to meet in person.

1. Keep the initial online message exchange focused on mutual interests for several days.
2. Suggest a low-pressure meeting in a public setting once you establish mutual interest.
3. Choose a simple public location, such as a casual coffee shop, public museum, or outdoor park.
4. Confirm details directly within the messaging interface before heading out.
5. Respect their pace if they prefer exchanging a few more messages first.

Clear communication keeps expectations realistic for both people.
