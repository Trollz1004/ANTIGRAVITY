# How to end a conversation kindly when you are not interested

Ending a conversation on a dating app can feel uncomfortable. Many people worry about hurting someone's feelings or inviting an angry reply. On February 2, 2023, a [Pew Research Center article](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/02/02/key-findings-about-online-dating-in-the-u-s/) reported that 52 percent of online daters believe they have encountered someone trying to scam them on dating platforms. The same publication found that roughly four in ten users experienced someone continuing to contact them after they stated they were not interested. Fake accounts and scammers often push conversations aggressively, while real people appreciate honest closure. A clear, kind message lets both people move forward with respect.

## Why a short honest message works best

Leaving a match waiting or disappearing without word often causes confusion. Sending a short note brings calm closure to the chat.

1. Direct honesty saves time for both people.
2. Clear closure prevents repeated check-in messages.
3. A polite boundary shows respect for genuine daters.
4. Short notes keep expectations clear and unambiguous.
5. Polite communication models good habits across the community.

You do not owe anyone an extensive explanation. A brief statement of preference is enough.

## How to distinguish genuine daters from bots and scams

Before choosing how to close a conversation, consider who is on the other side. Genuine people and automated operators act differently.

1. Genuine people accept a boundary and wish you well.
2. Bots and scammers ignore your messages and repeat generic scripts.
3. Fraud operators steer conversations toward investment links or gift cards.
4. Scam profiles often demand your personal phone number or outside messaging handle right away.
5. Dishonest accounts react with anger or guilt when you decline to move off the app.

If an account refuses to accept your decision or asks for funds, use built-in tools rather than continuing to reply.

## Respectful messages to end a conversation

Here are simple messages you can use when you realize a connection is not a good match.

1. "Thank you for chatting with me. I enjoyed learning about your hobbies, but I do not feel a romantic connection. I wish you the best in your search."
2. "It was great talking about music with you. After reflecting, I do not see a long-term match here. Thank you for your time, and good luck out there."
3. "I appreciate our conversation today. I am looking for a different type of connection, so I want to be honest rather than take up your time. Best wishes to you."
4. "Thank you for the pleasant chat. I do not think we are quite the right fit for each other, but I truly wish you well."
5. "I have enjoyed getting to know you a bit. I do not feel the chemistry needed for a date, so I want to be direct. Have a wonderful week."

These statements are polite, firm, and leave no false hopes.

## Safe steps when a match reacts poorly

Most real people respond courteously to a polite rejection. If someone becomes hostile or persistent, take immediate steps.

1. Do not engage in a debate or defend your preferences.
2. Stop responding once you have clearly stated you are not interested.
3. Take note of any abusive language or scam attempts.
4. Use the platform block tool to stop further contact.
5. Report suspicious accounts or harassment to platform moderators.

Your comfort comes first when interacting online.
