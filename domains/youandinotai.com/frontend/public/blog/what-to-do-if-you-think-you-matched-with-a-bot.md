# What to do if you think you matched with a bot

Matching with an account on a dating app should lead to a conversation with an actual person. Sometimes a match sends replies that feel strange, scripted, or disconnected from your questions. On February 2, 2023, the [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/02/02/key-findings-about-online-dating-in-the-u-s/) reported that 52 percent of people who have used dating sites or apps say they have come across someone they think was trying to scam them. Knowing what steps to take when you suspect a match is an automated bot helps you avoid wasting time and protect your private information.

## Pause the conversation and observe reply patterns

When you suspect a bot, slow down and watch how the account communicates.

1. Watch the speed of the incoming messages. Automated scripts can send long paragraphs within seconds of matching.
2. Ask a direct question about a specific detail in one of their photos. Bots reply with generic phrases that ignore your question completely.
3. Ask about a specific local spot or community detail in your area. Automated accounts give vague answers that fit any city.
4. Check whether their responses address what you wrote. Scripted replies introduce unrelated topics or sudden compliments.
5. Notice whether the account repeats the same greeting or phrase multiple times.

If the replies do not connect with your questions, you are interacting with automated software.

## Never click external links or share contact details

Automated accounts are designed to steer people away from dating platforms.

1. Do not click any web links sent by a suspicious match. External links can lead to phishing pages or credential harvesting forms.
2. Keep the conversation inside the dating application. Do not move to third-party messaging apps, text messages, or phone calls.
3. Keep your personal contact information private. Never share your phone number, email address, home address, or social media handles.
4. Refuse requests for financial help, investment opportunities, or gifts.
5. Avoid sharing verification codes or passwords of any kind.

Refusing external links and guarding your contact details protects your personal accounts from unauthorized access.

## Use built-in reporting and block tools

Once you determine that an account is automated, take action inside the platform.

1. Open the profile options menu on the dating app.
2. Select the report tool and choose the category for a bot, fake profile, or automated spam.
3. Block the account immediately so it cannot send you further messages.
4. Do not send a final message warning the bot operator or explaining why you are blocking them.
5. Clear the chat thread from your active message inbox.

Reporting the profile alerts the platform moderation team to review and remove the account.
