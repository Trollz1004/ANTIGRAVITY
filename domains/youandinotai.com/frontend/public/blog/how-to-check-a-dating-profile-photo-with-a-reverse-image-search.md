# How to check a dating profile photo with a reverse image search

Checking a dating profile photo with a reverse image search helps you find where else an image appears on the web. Online scammers take photos from social media accounts, modeling portfolios, and public websites to build fake profiles. In August 2022, the [Federal Trade Commission](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-you-need-know-about-romance-scams) published consumer advice explaining that scammers use photos of real people to create fake profiles. The Federal Trade Commission advises performing a reverse image search on a profile picture to check if the image connects to another name or details that do not match what the person told you.

## How to perform a reverse image search

You can run an image search using a web browser on a computer or a mobile phone.

1. Save the profile image to your device or copy the image web address.
2. Open an image search engine such as Google Images or TinEye.
3. Upload the saved picture or paste the image link into the search bar.
4. Review the returned results to see matching images and linked web pages.
5. Check whether the image appears under other names, stock photo galleries, or different locations.

## What search results tell you

A reverse image search returns links to websites that host the same picture or similar visual patterns.

1. **Stolen social media photos:** The search may lead to an active personal profile under a different name in another city or country.
2. **Stock photography:** The picture may link to a commercial stock photo catalog where anyone can license photos of models.
3. **Known scam reports:** Consumer forums and alert boards sometimes index images used in earlier scams.
4. **Zero search results:** A search that returns no matches does not confirm that an account is authentic. New photos, cropped screenshots, and synthetic images may yield no search matches.

## Steps to take if you find a stolen photo

When an image search reveals conflicting identities, stop communicating with the profile.

1. Do not send funds, gift cards, or personal banking information.
2. Stop messaging the account across all channels.
3. Use platform tools to block and report the profile.
4. Report the incident to consumer protection agencies such as the Federal Trade Commission at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
