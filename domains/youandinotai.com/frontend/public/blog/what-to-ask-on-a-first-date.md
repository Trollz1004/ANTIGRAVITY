# What to ask on a first date

Meeting someone in person for a first date should feel engaging and comfortable. Many people worry about awkward silences or running out of things to say. On February 9, 2023, a [Federal Trade Commission Data Spotlight](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-insights/data-spotlights/2023/02/romance-scammers-favorite-lies-exposed) reported that nearly 70,000 consumers reported losing $1.3 billion to romance scams in 2022. The report noted that scammers often invent elaborate backstories to avoid meeting in person or answering specific local questions. Real people enjoy sharing simple details about their everyday lives and interests. Asking direct, open questions helps you learn about your date while confirming they are genuine.

## Why open questions make first dates easier

Interviews feel stiff, but natural conversations flow back and forth. Open-ended questions encourage your date to share stories rather than one-word replies.

1. Open questions invite relaxed personal stories.
2. Shared details highlight common values and interests.
3. Casual follow-ups keep conversation balanced on both sides.
4. Specific questions about daily life reveal real routines.
5. In-person discussion shows how well you communicate together.

When both people listen attentively, conversation develops without forced effort.

## Great questions to break the ice

Starting with light, pleasant topics helps both people relax.

1. "What is something you worked on this week that you enjoyed?"
2. "How do you usually like to spend your time on weekends?"
3. "What is the best meal or local spot you found recently?"
4. "Do you have any upcoming trips or personal projects planned?"
5. "What kind of music, books, or movies have caught your attention lately?"

These questions keep the atmosphere positive and easy to navigate.

## Questions that reveal values and habits

Once you feel comfortable, deeper questions help you understand how your date approaches life.

1. "What is a routine or habit you try to keep every day?"
2. "Who are the people in your life you feel closest to?"
3. "What kind of environment helps you recharge after a busy day?"
4. "What is a goal you hope to achieve in the coming year?"
5. "How do you typically like to spend your holidays or time off?"

Genuine people give thoughtful answers that reflect real daily experience.

## How natural questions help spot bots and scams

Conversations with real people sound different from interactions with automated accounts or scammers.

1. Real dates give specific answers about local neighborhoods and daily routines.
2. Bots and scammers often repeat generic scripts without answering your question.
3. Fraud operators frequently deflect questions about their local area or in-person work.
4. Dishonest contacts quickly shift topics toward financial hardships or investments.
5. Honest partners welcome mutual curiosity without pressuring you for money or personal details.

If someone avoids basic questions or asks for funds, end the date safely.
