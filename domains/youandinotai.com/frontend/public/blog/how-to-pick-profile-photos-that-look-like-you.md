# How to pick profile photos that look like you

Choosing profile photos that look like you makes dating easier for everyone. When photos show your true appearance, in-person meetings feel natural and comfortable. On February 9, 2023, a [Federal Trade Commission press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/02/new-ftc-data-reveals-top-lies-told-romance-scammers) reported that nearly 70,000 consumers filed romance scam reports in 2022, with reported losses totaling $1.3 billion. Scammers frequently steal glamour shots or generate artificial faces to hide their identities. Using authentic, unfiltered pictures shows you are a genuine person looking for honest connections.

## Core rules for clear profile photos

Selecting good photos requires simple attention to lighting and honesty. You do not need professional equipment to take believable pictures.

1. Take pictures in natural outdoor light or near a bright window.
2. Look directly toward the camera with a relaxed facial expression.
3. Include at least one full-body shot taken from eye level.
4. Show your face clearly without heavy sunglasses, wide hats, or masking shadows.
5. Use recent photos taken within the past six months.

Clear pictures establish immediate recognition when you meet face to face.

## Photo habits to avoid

Certain photo styles create confusion or look identical to bot accounts. Avoiding these habits helps your profile stand out.

1. Avoid heavy face filters that smooth skin texture or reshape facial features.
2. Avoid using old photos from several years ago where your hairstyle or appearance differs.
3. Avoid uploading only group photos where matches cannot tell which person you are.
4. Avoid extreme overhead or distant angles that obscure your natural frame.
5. Avoid wearing dark sunglasses in every single image in your profile.

Honest presentation builds early confidence with other members.

## How to build a balanced photo set

A complete photo set shows different sides of your regular weekly routine. Five distinct photos give matches a reliable impression.

1. One clear headshot with good lighting and a natural smile.
2. One full-length picture showing you standing comfortably.
3. One candid photo engaged in a hobby you enjoy, such as hiking or cooking.
4. One picture outdoors in a casual neighborhood or park setting.
5. One picture with friends or family where your face remains easy to identify.

This combination provides a balanced view of your everyday life.
