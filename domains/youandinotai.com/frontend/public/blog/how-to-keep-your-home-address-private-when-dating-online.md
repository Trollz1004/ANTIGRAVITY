# How to keep your home address private when dating online

Protecting your physical location is a basic part of dating online. When you start chatting with a new contact, keep your home address to yourself until you establish trust in person over time. On February 2, 2023, a [Pew Research Center report](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/02/02/key-findings-about-online-dating-in-the-u-s/) found that 30 percent of U.S. adults have used a dating site or app. Keeping your residence private helps you control your personal space while getting to know someone.

## Meet at neutral public locations

Choose public spots for your first few dates. Meeting in public avoids revealing where you live.

1. Pick a busy coffee shop, restaurant, or bookstore.
2. Select a venue in a commercial area rather than your immediate neighborhood.
3. Agree on the address of the venue before you head out.
4. Arrive and depart independently.
5. Do not invite a date back to your residence on early meetings.

Neutral locations give you an easy exit if the date does not go well.

## Provide your own transportation

Relying on a date for a ride reveals your street address immediately. Managing your own travel protects your location.

1. Drive your own vehicle, take public transit, or book a rideshare.
2. Decline offers from a match to pick you up at your house or apartment.
3. Decline offers for a ride home at the end of the date.
4. If using a rideshare, set your pickup and drop-off point a block away from your front door.
5. Park in a public lot near the venue.

Independent travel gives you full control over when you arrive and when you leave.

## Check your photos for location details

Images can reveal your home address without your knowledge. Review each picture before posting it to your profile.

1. Crop out house numbers, street signs, and identifiable landmarks.
2. Avoid pictures showing distinctive views from your apartment window.
3. Remove mail, delivery boxes, and paperwork from the background of indoor photos.
4. Turn off location tags and metadata in your camera settings before uploading photos.
5. Use photos taken in public parks or standard indoor settings with plain backgrounds.

Clean backgrounds prevent strangers from locating your building.

## Keep your neighborhood name private early on

Sharing specific neighborhood details allows people to narrow down your street. Keep your general area broad.

1. Name your broader town or city rather than your exact subdivision.
2. Avoid naming your local grocery store, neighborhood gym, or daily dog-walking route.
3. Keep details about nearby cross streets private.
4. Decline questions asking for your exact intersection or building complex.
5. Change the subject if someone pressures you for your exact living area.

Keeping your location general gives you time to verify who you are talking to.
