# What to do when a match pushes you to leave the app

A match asking to move off a dating app right after connecting is a familiar experience in online dating. In August 2022, the [Federal Trade Commission](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-romance-scams) published consumer guidance on romance scams stating that scammers quickly try to move the conversation off the dating site or app to direct messaging, text, or phone calls. Moving the conversation helps operators avoid platform safety tools and account moderation. Knowing what steps to take keeps your personal contact information secure.

## Why scammers push you to leave the app

Scammers want to separate you from the platform before their profile is flagged.

1. Dating platforms monitor reported accounts and can remove scam profiles from discovery.
2. Direct messaging or external chat apps remove built-in platform reporting protections.
3. Getting your personal phone number or email provides scammers with private contact details.
4. Moving to external channels makes it easier for an operator to introduce emergency requests or investment schemes.
5. Scammers know unverified accounts can be shut down quickly by platform moderators.

## What to do when a match asks to move off the app

Take simple steps to protect your personal information when a match requests your phone number or external messaging handle.

1. Keep the conversation inside the dating app where moderation tools exist.
2. Say that you prefer to get to know someone on the app before sharing phone numbers or personal handles.
3. Watch how the match responds to your boundary.
4. Stop communicating if the match pressures you, insults you, or refuses to chat on the app.
5. Use platform features to block and report accounts that push for immediate contact transfers.

## Warning signs of fake profiles and off-platform scams

Pay attention to how the match communicates in early messages.

1. The profile has few photos or uses professional modeling images.
2. The match professes strong affection within hours of matching.
3. The person claims to live nearby but is temporarily traveling, stationed overseas, or working in a remote area.
4. The match invents reasons why they cannot make a voice call or meet in person.
5. The contact asks for financial assistance, gift cards, or investment help soon after moving to external chat.
