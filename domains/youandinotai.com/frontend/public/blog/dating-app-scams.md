# How to spot a dating app scam before you waste a week

Modern dating app scams rarely open with an immediate demand for cash. Instead, organized fraud operators play a patient game designed to pull you off the dating platform, isolate the conversation on a private chat app, and build daily emotional habits before introducing a financial angle.

## Three scripts that show up in almost every dating scam

Once you recognize the opening chapters of these scripts, you can shut down a fraudulent match in under five minutes instead of losing a week of your time.

1. The immediate platform exit: the match claims they rarely check the dating app and insists on texting your personal phone number or moving to an encrypted chat app after two messages.
2. The unavailable local: their profile lists your city, yet their first message explains they are temporarily out of the country on an engineering contract, military deployment, or estate settlement.
3. The casual wealth mentor: the conversation pivots toward their success with cryptocurrency, foreign exchange trading, or a private financial advisor who can supposedly teach you their system.

## Rules that protect your time and wallet

Never send money, gift cards, wire transfers, or crypto to anyone you met online, regardless of how urgent their medical, travel, or banking story sounds. Keep your chat inside the dating app until you have met in person at a busy public coffee shop. If a match refuses to meet locally or pressures you for outside contact details, block and report the profile right away.
