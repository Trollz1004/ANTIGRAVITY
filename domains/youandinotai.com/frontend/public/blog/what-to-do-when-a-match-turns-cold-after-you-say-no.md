# What to do when a match turns cold after you say no

A match withdrawing or becoming distant after you decline a request is a known occurrence in online dating. On February 13, 2024, the [Federal Trade Commission](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/02/love-stinks-when-scammer-involved) reported that romance scams resulted in $1.14 billion in reported losses in 2023 across 64,003 reports, with a median loss of $2,000 per person. When a match stops replying or shows sudden frustration after you say no, understanding why this happens helps you decide how to respond.

## Why a match turns cold after you say no

When you decline an early request, an individual who had insincere intentions alters their communication.

1. The person realizes you will not provide money, gift cards, or financial assistance.
2. The match moves their time toward targets who agree to outside requests.
3. The individual was following a scripted routine rather than seeking an authentic connection.
4. The person attempts to use silence or guilt to pressure you into changing your response.
5. The contact ends communication because their sole objective was financial transfer.

## Signs to observe when communication changes

Watch for changes in message frequency and tone after you decline a request.

1. The match switches from rapid replies to one-word messages or delayed answers.
2. The person expresses hostility, irritation, or blame because you set a boundary.
3. The match claims you lack trust or do not care about the budding relationship.
4. The individual repeats the rejected request with added urgency or pressure.
5. The person stops sending messages entirely without offering an explanation.

## Practical steps to take when a match goes cold

You have clear options when a match becomes distant after you decline a request.

1. Maintain your personal boundaries and decline to apologize for saying no.
2. Avoid sending repeated follow-up messages seeking reassurance or explanations.
3. Keep conversations on the dating platform where you have a clear record of interactions.
4. Block the account if the individual becomes demanding, manipulative, or hostile.
5. Report suspicious accounts to platform moderation teams for review.
