# How to tell a bot from a shy person on a dating app

Starting a conversation on a dating app requires attention to how someone communicates. When a match sends brief messages or pauses before answering, you might wonder if you are talking to an automated script or someone who feels nervous. On February 2, 2023, a [Pew Research Center article](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/02/02/key-findings-about-online-dating-in-the-u-s/) reported that 52 percent of online daters believe they have encountered someone trying to scam them on dating platforms. Recognizing the difference between automated software and an introverted dater helps you navigate conversations with confidence.

## Watch how your match replies to specific questions

The difference between automated scripts and quiet people appears in the content of their messages.

1. A shy person reads your exact words and responds to the topic you brought up.
2. An automated account ignores specific questions and delivers generic compliments or prewritten sentences.
3. A shy person might write short phrases, but the details reflect genuine personal thoughts.
4. An automated account sends identical greetings or changes the subject abruptly.
5. An automated script sends messages at inhuman speeds, while a shy person takes human pauses to reflect.

Direct responses to personal questions show that a real human is reading your messages.

## Look for local knowledge and real-world context

Testing for real-world awareness separates genuine people from scripts designed to operate anywhere.

1. Ask your match about a local park, coffee spot, or neighborhood landmark.
2. A shy person can mention specific details about their area, even if they keep the description brief.
3. An automated account avoids naming local places or offers vague descriptions that fit any city.
4. Suggest a casual topic like local weather or a recent community event.
5. Watch whether the match acknowledges your shared geography or gives evasive answers.

Local knowledge confirms that your match lives and spends time in your actual community.

## Notice requests for money, outside links, or rapid escalation

Financial motives and pressure tactics identify automated fraud operations, not introverted daters.

1. A shy person moves at a gentle pace and never asks for money, gift cards, or financial help.
2. Automated accounts try to move conversations to external messaging platforms or suspicious web links.
3. A shy person never pitches cryptocurrency investments or asks for account verification codes.
4. Automated scripts push aggressive praise or rapid declarations of affection to build artificial urgency.
5. A quiet person respects your conversation pace and takes time to build comfort before opening up.

Anyone who asks for financial assistance or sends unverified external links is demonstrating scam behavior.

## How to make quiet daters feel comfortable

Introverted people need a relaxed setting to share their interests.

1. Ask open-ended questions about hobbies, books, or pets.
2. Give your match time to reply without sending repeated follow-up messages.
3. Share a small story about your own day to establish an easy conversational anchor.
4. Keep early chats focused on low-pressure topics rather than intense personal interrogations.
5. Suggest moving to a short voice message or a casual public coffee meetup when both of you feel ready.

Patience allows real connections to develop at a natural pace.
