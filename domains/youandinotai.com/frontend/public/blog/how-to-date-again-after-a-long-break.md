# How to date again after a long break

Returning to dating after a long break can feel unfamiliar. The online dating landscape changes quickly, and people often feel unsure about how to begin. On February 9, 2023, the Federal Trade Commission published a Data Spotlight titled [Romance scammers' favorite lies exposed](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2023/02/romance-scammers-favorite-lies-exposed). The report noted that nearly 70,000 people reported romance scams in 2022, with reported losses reaching $1.3 billion. Many of those scams began on dating apps with fake profiles and automated accounts. Taking your time, setting simple boundaries, and looking for genuine signals helps you meet real people at your own pace.

## Five ways to ease back into dating

Starting slowly helps you build confidence without feeling pressured.

1. Decide what pace feels comfortable before you start talking with matches.
2. Choose one or two hobbies you genuinely enjoy to discuss in conversation.
3. Keep initial chats focused on everyday routines and local interests.
4. Plan short first meetings in public spaces during daytime hours.
5. Take regular breaks from your phone whenever browsing starts to feel tiring.

Setting small goals makes returning to dating manageable.

## How to spot fake profiles after a break

If you have been away from dating platforms for years, automated accounts and scam patterns might look unfamiliar. Genuine people behave differently from scripts.

1. Watch for profiles that use only professional modeling photos with no local context.
2. Notice accounts that declare intense affection within hours of matching.
3. Be careful when a match refuses quick voice or video calls before meeting in person.
4. Watch out for matches who quickly ask you to move the conversation to external chat apps.
5. Stop talking immediately if someone asks for money, gift cards, or financial investments.

Real people understand your desire to take things one step at a time.

## Setting boundaries that protect your peace

Clear boundaries keep your dating experience positive.

1. Keep personal details like your home address or workplace private during early chats.
2. Say no politely whenever a request or meeting time does not suit your schedule.
3. Suggest simple first dates like a coffee shop or a walk in a public park.
4. Arrange your own transportation to and from every meeting.
5. Trust your instincts and end conversations that feel disrespectful or demanding.

Comfortable boundaries give you full control over your dating journey.
