# What to do if a match wants to video chat but never turns the camera on

Online dating conversations sometimes move to a video call before an in-person meeting. A video chat lets both people verify that their match looks like their profile pictures. Problems arise when a match joins a video chat but keeps their own camera off. On February 13, 2024, the [Federal Trade Commission](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/02/love-stinks-when-scammer-involved) published an article titled "Love Stinks – when a scammer is involved". The article stated that romance fraud caused $1.14 billion in reported losses in 2023 across 64,003 reports, with a median loss of $2,000 per person. When a match asks to see your live video while hiding their own face, you need clear steps to protect yourself.

## Why a match keeps their camera off during a video call

A one-sided video call creates an unfair imbalance between two people.

1. Stolen profile pictures: The person does not match the photos displayed on their dating profile.
2. One-way surveillance: The individual wants to see your appearance and room while remaining hidden.
3. Recording your video: Deceptive callers may attempt to record your live video without your knowledge.
4. Fake technical difficulties: The caller fabricates issues with their lens, application permissions, or internet speed.
5. Lack of verification: Hiding behind a dark screen allows a person to avoid confirming their real identity.

## Excuses people use to avoid showing their face

Deceptive profiles use repeated explanations when their camera stays off.

1. "My camera hardware is broken": The match claims their phone lens cracked or their laptop webcam stopped working right before the call.
2. "My internet connection is weak": The caller states that video streaming lags, so they turned off their outgoing video feed.
3. "The room is too dark": The individual claims poor lighting prevents them from turning on their video.
4. "I am shy today": The match agrees to a video chat, but asks you to keep your camera on while they stay off-screen.
5. "My workplace forbids cameras": The caller claims strict employer security policies prevent them from displaying video.

## Clear steps to take when a match refuses to turn on the camera

Protect your privacy by taking immediate action during a one-sided call.

1. Turn off your camera: Do not stay visible if the other person refuses to show their face.
2. Require mutual visibility: State clearly that a video call requires two active cameras.
3. End the call: Hang up if the match makes excuses and leaves their video feed black.
4. Do not engage in private actions: Never let a hidden caller convince you to undress or do anything private on screen.
5. Refuse financial requests: If a match who hides their face asks for funds or gift cards, stop all contact.
6. Document suspicious behavior: Take note of the account name and behavior if the person pressured you.
7. Block and report the account: Use the platform tools to report an account that misrepresents their identity.
