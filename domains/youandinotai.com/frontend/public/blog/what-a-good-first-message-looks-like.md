# What a good first message looks like

People searching for dating advice want to know what a genuine greeting looks like compared to an automated script. Fake accounts, bots, and fraud operations create noise on unverified dating services. On February 2, 2023, a [Pew Research Center article](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/02/02/key-findings-about-online-dating-in-the-u-s/) reported that 52 percent of people who have used dating sites or apps say they have come across someone they think was trying to scam them. Knowing what a real introductory message looks like helps you focus on genuine members.

## Characteristics of a genuine first message

A thoughtful opening message comes from a person who took time to look at your profile.

1. The message references a specific detail from your written bio or photo background.
2. The sender asks a single question that is easy to answer.
3. The greeting uses a conversational tone without exaggerated affection.
4. The person keeps the exchange on the platform rather than pushing to leave immediately.
5. The message does not ask for money, gift cards, or financial details.

## Signs of a bot opener or scam script

Automated accounts and scammers rely on repeatable patterns to contact people at scale.

1. The account sends an impersonal greeting that mentions none of your profile details.
2. The sender uses pet names or declares intense romantic feelings right away.
3. The message includes an external link to a website or third-party platform.
4. The contact pushes to move to phone calls, email, or off-app messaging immediately.
5. The message shifts the conversation toward investment schemes or sudden emergencies.

## How to reply to a good first message

When a match sends a thoughtful opening message, a direct response keeps the dialogue moving forward.

1. Answer the question asked in the opening message with a short sentence.
2. Share a brief detail about your day or a hobby mentioned on your profile.
3. Ask a relevant question in return to give the match a turn to speak.
4. Keep early messages on the platform while you establish mutual rapport.
5. Take your time deciding when you feel ready to suggest a brief in-person meeting.
