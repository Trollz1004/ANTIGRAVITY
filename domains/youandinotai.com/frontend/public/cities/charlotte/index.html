Dating in Charlotte without bots

Blocking spam accounts after they land in your inbox is a losing battle when a dating app hands out new accounts for free. Adults looking to meet real people in Charlotte should not have to act as unpaid moderators just to have an honest conversation. The practical fix is to require human verification before any account can post or message.

What stands between scripts and your feed

YouAndINotAI enforces a mandatory Bot-Shield check for every member. After you register an 18+ account and verify your date of birth, you complete two straightforward steps before swiping, messaging, or posting:

  1. Answer one timed question to confirm a real adult is present.
  2. Complete a one dollar check attached directly to your account.

Until those two steps are finished, an account cannot send likes, open private chats, or write on topic boards. Pairing one timed question with a one-time one dollar payment eliminates zero-cost bot creation without requiring you to buy a recurring monthly subscription just to prove you are human.

Open conversation on topic boards and the USA group chat

Your verified badge unlocks every conversation space on the platform. You can browse individual profiles in discovery, join discussions on topic boards, and open the USA group chat at /app/usa. The USA group chat is a live nationwide board where verified adults in Charlotte and across the country talk in one shared room.

Because topic boards, discovery, and the USA group chat all require the same timed question and one dollar check, bots are locked out before the conversation starts. youandinotai.com is for adults 18 and over.
