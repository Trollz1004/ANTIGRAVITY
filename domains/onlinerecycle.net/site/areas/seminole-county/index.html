Free electronics recycling pickup in Seminole County

Seminole County homes and businesses can schedule free electronics pickup any day of the week through OnlineRecycle.net, operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, Florida. Our calendar offers five daily 2-hour route pickup windows Monday through Sunday during our 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM operating hours, supporting small household cleanouts as well as multi-pallet business loads.

Seven-Day Route Pickup and Sorrento Drop-Off Options

If dropping off gear is more convenient for your schedule, you can book one of nine daily 30-minute Sorrento drop-off windows from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM, or reach dispatch by phone or text at (321) 396-2008.

What You Can Turn In on Seminole County Routes

If an item plugs in or uses a battery, you can include it in your booking request. Accepted hardware includes notebooks, MacBooks, desktop PCs, workstations, rack servers, network switches, routers, firewalls, NAS units, internal PC components, phones, tablets, monitors, docks, printers, cables, and UPS battery backups. We also accept game consoles, handhelds, accessories, video games, DVDs, Blu-rays, CDs, home stereo receivers, speakers, soundbars, car stereos, amplifiers, and subwoofers.

Data Wiping, Refurbishment, and Responsible Recycling

Storage security comes first at intake: hard disk drives and solid-state drives are removed for multi-pass erasure or physical destruction before any system is prepared for secondary reuse or dismantled for commodity recovery of circuit boards, steel, aluminum, and copper wiring. Customers should still back up important data and factory-reset devices before turning them over whenever possible.
