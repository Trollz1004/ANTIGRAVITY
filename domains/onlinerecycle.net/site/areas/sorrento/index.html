Free electronics recycling pickup in Sorrento

Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC operates OnlineRecycle.net directly in Sorrento, Florida, giving households and small businesses two free options for clearing out unwanted electronics seven days a week between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM. You can reserve one of nine daily 30-minute Sorrento drop-off windows running from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM, or book a scheduled 2-hour route pickup window if you prefer curbside or loading-dock collection in Sorrento and Lake County.

Local Sorrento Drop-Off and Route Pickup Windows

Five daily 2-hour pickup windows are available on our online scheduler for batches ranging from 1 to 5 household items up to 50+ item commercial pallet loads. You can also call or text (321) 396-2008 to coordinate arrival details.

Accepted Computers, Consoles, Media, and Stereo Gear

We accept desktop towers, workstations, MacBooks, notebooks, rackmount servers, managed switches, routers, firewalls, NAS units, motherboards, GPUs, RAM, processors, smartphones, tablets, monitors, docking stations, printers, cables, and UPS battery backups. Entertainment hardware is welcome too, including PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, and Steam Deck game consoles, handhelds, controllers, video game discs, cartridges, movie DVDs, Blu-rays, audio CDs, home stereo receivers, speakers, soundbars, car stereos, amplifiers, and subwoofers.

Storage Media Wiping and Physical Destruction

Every device turned in at Sorrento intake is checked for personal or business data storage components. Solid-state and magnetic drives, including NVMe M.2 modules, SATA SSDs, and 2.5-inch or 3.5-inch hard drives, are pulled at intake for multi-pass erasure or physical destruction before working hardware is refurbished for resale or non-working material enters commodity recycling streams. Customers should still back up files and factory-reset devices before handoff whenever possible.
