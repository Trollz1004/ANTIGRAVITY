Free electronics recycling pickup in Orange County

Households and commercial workplaces across Orange County can clear out retired electronics without landfill dumping by booking a free 2-hour route pickup window with OnlineRecycle.net. Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC dispatches seven days a week between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM, offering five daily 2-hour pickup windows to fit single-office batches of 1 to 5 items, garage cleanouts of 6 to 15 items, IT cleanouts of 16 to 50 items, or 50+ item pallet loads.

Daily Dispatch Windows for Orange County Cleanouts

Customers who prefer self-delivery may instead reserve one of nine daily 30-minute drop-off windows in Sorrento between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM. Our dispatch line at (321) 396-2008 is open daily for pickup questions.

Computers, Networking, Gaming, and Audio Equipment

Orange County pickups cover x86 and Apple Silicon laptops, desktop towers, engineering workstations, 1U to 4U rackmount servers, blade enclosures, NAS arrays, motherboards, graphics cards, memory, processors, routers, switches, firewalls, smartphones, tablets, monitors, docking stations, printers, cords, and UPS towers. In addition to IT gear, we collect video game consoles, handhelds, controllers, game discs, cartridges, movie DVDs, Blu-rays, CDs, home audio amplifiers, receivers, soundbars, and car stereo systems.

Intake Assessment and Storage Media Handling

We treat every collected computer, server, and mobile device as data-bearing hardware. During intake assessment, storage media is identified and routed through multi-pass erasure or physical destruction before usable electronics are refurbished or non-resalable metals and circuit boards are recycled. We recommend backing up your files and factory-resetting devices ahead of collection whenever possible.
