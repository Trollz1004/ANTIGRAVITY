Free electronics recycling pickup in Lake County

OnlineRecycle.net provides scheduled free electronics pickup across Lake County alongside local drop-off in Sorrento. Operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC, our intake and dispatch team works Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time. Lake County residents and offices can select from five daily 2-hour pickup windows on our calendar: 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM, 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM, 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM, or 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

Scheduled 2-Hour Pickup Routes and Sorrento Intake

If you want to bring equipment in yourself, our Sorrento hub offers nine daily 30-minute drop-off slots between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM. Call or text (321) 396-2008 if your pickup involves loading-dock access.

Hardware Accepted for Reuse and Recycling

Our Lake County pickup routes collect computing and office hardware across all batch tiers, from a few laptops, PCs, phones, or tablets to full server racks, patch panels, enterprise switches, firewalls, monitors, printers, power supplies, cabling, and battery backups. We also take modern and retro game systems, handhelds, controllers, game cartridges, optical discs, DVDs, Blu-rays, 4K UHD discs, CDs, home stereo receivers, floor speakers, car head units, and subwoofers.

Drive Separation and Data Sanitization

Data-bearing equipment is isolated as soon as a Lake County batch arrives. We separate SATA SSDs, NVMe drives, SAS enterprise disks, magnetic hard drives, and external storage arrays for multi-pass wiping or physical destruction prior to any parts recovery, resale, or material recycling. Please back up needed records and perform a factory reset on phones and computers before your pickup window whenever possible.
