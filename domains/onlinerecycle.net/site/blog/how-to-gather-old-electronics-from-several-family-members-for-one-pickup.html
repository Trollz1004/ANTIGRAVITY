How to Gather Old Electronics from Several Family Members for One Pickup

Many households across Central Florida have relatives with unwanted computers, audio equipment, and game systems in closets or garages. Gathering items from several family members into a single batch simplifies recycling. OnlineRecycle.net provides free scheduled route pickup across Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County, as well as free drop-off in Sorrento.

Step 1: Check Accepted Devices with Family

Make a Shared List of Accepted Electronics

When coordinating with parents, siblings, or adult children, start by checking the list of accepted equipment. OnlineRecycle.net, operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC, handles personal computing, enterprise networking, media, and stereo hardware.

Ask family members to look for desktop towers, workstations, MacBooks, notebooks, and all-in-one PCs. They can also gather loose computer parts such as motherboards, GPUs, RAM, and processors. Household office hardware includes smartphones, tablets, routers, firewalls, monitors, docking stations, printers, cables, and UPS battery backups.

Relatives can also contribute home entertainment and audio equipment. Accepted items include modern and retro game consoles, handheld systems, controllers, and accessories. Optical media collections are accepted, including video game discs, cartridges, DVDs, Blu-rays, and audio CDs. For audio setups, family members can clear out stereo receivers, amplifiers, speakers, soundbars, subwoofers, and car audio head units.

Step 2: Bring Gear to One Location and Pick a Batch Size

Centralize Equipment and Count Your Items

Pick one family member address in Lake County, Orange County, or Seminole County to serve as the pickup point. Having all devices in one place allows for a single scheduled stop. Group power cables and accessories into boxes alongside the equipment.

Count the items to select the correct quantity tier on the booking form:

  • 1 to 5 items: Household drop-off or small batch.
  • 6 to 15 items: Home office or garage cleanout.
  • 16 to 50 items: Small business, multi-family, or estate cleanout.
  • 50+ items: Commercial pallet load or multi-room cleanout.

Combining items from several relatives often reaches the 6 to 15 items or 16 to 50 items tier. Entering an accurate count helps route planning.

Step 3: Select Data Handling and Book Your Window

Select Data Preferences and Confirm Your Appointment

Family laptops, desktop towers, and external drives may contain personal files. On the booking form, choose one of three data handling preferences:

  • Standard intake & storage wipe/destruction
  • Includes laptops/PCs/drives needing priority data wipe
  • No storage media (monitors, printers, cables, peripherals)

Solid-state and magnetic drives, including NVMe M.2, SATA SSD, and 2.5-inch or 3.5-inch hard drives, are separated at intake. Storage media is wiped or destroyed with reasonable effort before chassis refurbishment or material recovery.

Choose your service type and time window on the form. Scheduled route pickup runs Monday through Sunday between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM with five daily 2-hour pickup windows. If your family prefers drop-off, free drop-off in Sorrento is available Monday through Sunday with nine daily 30-minute drop-off windows between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM.

Submit the form with your name, phone number, and pickup address. The site gives a booking reference ID and a calendar file. Share the booking reference ID with family members so everyone knows the collection window. For questions, call or text (321) 396-2008 Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
