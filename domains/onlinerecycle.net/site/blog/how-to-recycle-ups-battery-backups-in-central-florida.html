Where to Recycle UPS Battery Backups in Central Florida

Uninterruptible power supply battery backups and UPS towers protect servers and office computers from sudden power loss. When internal batteries wear down or offices decommission server racks, these heavy units become difficult to store and discard. OnlineRecycle.net provides direct intake options across Central Florida for UPS battery backups, UPS towers, power supplies, and connected computer hardware.

Step 1: Preparation

Preparing UPS Battery Backups for Local Intake

Desktop UPS units and rackmount power enclosures contain heavy sealed battery packs, power transformers, copper windings, and thick steel frames. Safe handling starts before transport or handoff.

Begin by powering off each connected device. Unplug desktop towers, workstations, network switches, and monitors from the rear surge and battery outlets. Next, switch off the main power button on the front of the UPS and disconnect the main wall plug from electrical power. Leave the internal battery packs inside the original chassis enclosure. Keeping the batteries inside their factory metal or plastic casing prevents loose terminal contact during transport and intake handling.

If you are decommissioning a server closet or network rack, label the units by approximate weight. Server room battery enclosures can weigh fifty pounds or more. Taping cables neatly to the side of the unit keeps walkways clear during loading and handoff.

Count your hardware batch to match our intake capacity tiers. Our booking system accommodates four batch tiers: 1 to 5 items, 6 to 15 items, 16 to 50 items, or 50+ commercial pallet loads.

Step 2: Processing

How UPS Towers and Battery Packs Are Handled

Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC operates OnlineRecycle.net from Sorrento, Florida. We accept uninterruptible power supplies, desktop battery backups, 1U to 4U rackmount UPS gear, and standalone power supplies.

Incoming units are sorted based on condition, chassis type, and battery chemistry. Functional UPS enclosures with good circuit boards can be refurbished with new battery packs for secondary use and equipment resale. Units with blown inverters, melted casings, or obsolete control circuitry are dismantled for material recovery. Copper transformers, aluminum heat sinks, internal wiring, and steel chassis panels are separated into commodity streams.

Many UPS systems are retired alongside computers and storage arrays. If your cleanout includes laptops, MacBooks, desktop towers, servers, or NAS units, storage drives are isolated at intake. Storage media is wiped or destroyed with reasonable effort before chassis resale or component recycling.
