Where to Recycle Workstations in Central Florida

Engineering workstations and high-performance desktop towers accumulate in commercial offices, design studios, and home workspaces when hardware cycles turn over. Central Florida residents and business teams have straightforward options for free Sorrento drop-off and scheduled route pickup.

Step 1: Staging Workstations and Components

Accepted Workstation Hardware and System Preparation

Desktop workstations, tower systems, and high-performance engineering computers often remain in storage after office upgrades or team refreshes. These heavy computing systems contain modular hardware that can be sorted for reuse and responsible material reclamation.

OnlineRecycle.net accepts computing equipment across Central Florida. We accept all x86 and Apple Silicon engineering workstations, desktop towers, and all-in-one workstations. We also accept individual computing components including motherboards, processors, RAM modules, GPUs, power cables, and network gear.

Preparing a workstation for recycling requires only simple steps. First, shut down the computer and unplug all connected cords from wall outlets. Detach power cables, monitor cables, and external cords from the back panel. Coil the power cords neatly beside the computer case. If you have extra RAM modules or spare expansion cards stored in drawers, group them alongside the computer chassis.

Engineering firms, architecture practices, video editors, and home labs often retire multiple systems during hardware refresh cycles. We accept mixed hardware batches of any volume. You can stage individual desktop workstations or full equipment groups for intake.

Step 2: Internal Storage Media Separation

Storage Wiped or Destroyed at Intake

Workstations frequently store sensitive technical data, project schematics, financial spreadsheets, client archives, and operational files. Proper management of data-bearing media is a fundamental part of responsible computing intake.

Our intake workflow handles every standard internal storage format. We process NVMe M.2 solid-state drives, SATA SSDs, SAS enterprise drives, and traditional 2.5-inch and 3.5-inch hard disk drives. At intake, internal drives are separated from workstation chassis and drive bays.

We make reasonable efforts to wipe or destroy data using standard processes. Solid-state and magnetic drives undergo multi-pass erasure or physical destruction before any chassis refurbishment or material processing occurs.

If your organization prefers to keep internal storage or remove drives prior to turnover, you are welcome to do so. We accept workstations and desktop towers with or without their original drives installed.

Step 3: Central Florida Scheduling

Free Sorrento Drop-Off and Scheduled Route Pickup

OnlineRecycle.net is operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, Florida. We provide free drop-off in Sorrento and scheduled free route pickup across Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County.

Our online booking system supports four intake batch tiers. You can book 1 to 5 items for a household or single workstation, 6 to 15 items for an office room refresh, 16 to 50 items for a small business cleanout, and 50+ items for larger commercial batches.

For Sorrento drop-off, choose from nine daily 30-minute intake windows between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM, Monday through Sunday. For route pickup, choose from five daily 2-hour windows available across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties.

When you submit your appointment through our scheduler, you receive a booking reference ID and a downloadable calendar file for your records. For questions regarding batch sizes, curbside coordination, or loading access, you can call or text (321) 396-2008.
