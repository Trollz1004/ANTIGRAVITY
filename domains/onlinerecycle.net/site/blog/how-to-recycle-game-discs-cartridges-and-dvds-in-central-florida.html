How to Recycle Game Discs, Cartridges, and DVDs in Central Florida

Boxes of optical discs and game cartridges often sit untouched in closets or cabinets. Here is how to sort, pack, and recycle them with free local intake.

Step 1: Check Your Media

Optical Discs and Cartridges We Accept

Check your shelves and closets for video game discs, game cartridges, movie DVDs, Blu-rays, 4K UHD discs, and audio CDs.

OnlineRecycle.net accepts game discs and cartridges, DVDs, Blu-rays, 4K UHD discs, and audio CDs alongside home electronics. You can also include related hardware in the same batch. We accept game consoles, handheld systems, controllers, cables, stereo receivers, soundbars, speakers, desktop towers, workstations, and laptops.

After intake, tested working consoles and audio decks are refurbished for secondary use, and non-working boards, chassis steel, aluminum and copper wiring go to commodity recovery. If a batch has no storage media, such as a box of discs and cartridges, you can mark it that way on the booking form.

Step 2: Pack and Check for Storage

Pack Your Items and Separate Storage Media

Packing physical media is straightforward. Place your discs and cartridges into cardboard boxes or sturdy tote bags. Keeping games and media together prevents discs from sliding loose during transit. If you are also including consoles or handhelds, bundle their power adapters and cables alongside them.

Game consoles and computers can hold personal profiles and saved logins. At OnlineRecycle.net, data-bearing drives are separated at intake.

Internal solid-state and magnetic hard drives are wiped or destroyed with reasonable effort using standard processes before any hardware is refurbished for resale or processed for material recycling. If you are surrendering a console, desktop PC, or laptop with your media, you can choose priority data wipe when scheduling.

Step 3: Choose Intake

Free Sorrento Drop-Off or Scheduled County Pickup

Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC operates OnlineRecycle.net in Sorrento, Florida. We provide two easy options to turn in your electronics and media collections.

First, you can select a free 30-minute Sorrento drop-off slot on our online calendar. Drop-off times are available Monday through Sunday between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM. Second, if you have multiple boxes or larger equipment, you can request free route pickup across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties. Route pickups are scheduled in 2-hour windows.

Select your estimated item tier on the booking form. We offer four tiers: 1 to 5 items for a small batch, 6 to 15 items for a home cleanout, 16 to 50 items for larger cleanouts, and 50+ items for commercial loads. When you submit your booking, you receive a booking reference ID and a calendar file to save to your personal schedule. You can also call or text (321) 396-2008 between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM Monday through Sunday.
