Where to Recycle Phones and Tablets in Central Florida

Old mobile devices often accumulate in desk drawers and storage boxes across Central Florida homes and offices. Following a few simple steps helps you clear out retired smartphones and tablets without leaving personal accounts attached.

Step 1: Account Removal and Device Reset

Sign Out and Factory Reset Your Devices

Smartphones and tablets store photos, text messages, contact directories, and active login sessions. Before turning over an old handset or tablet for recycling, copy any files or photos you wish to keep to another device or backup drive.

After copying files, sign out of cloud backup services, email accounts, and app store accounts. Remove screen passcodes, PIN numbers, and biometric locks. Once accounts are disconnected, run the built-in factory reset option in the device settings. A factory reset removes stored data partitions and restores the mobile device to its default factory state.

Remove any physical SIM cards and memory cards from the side card trays. Gather any charging cables and power adapters that belong with the phones and tablets so everything can be processed together. Bundling loose cables together helps keep your equipment organized before intake.

We accept phones and tablets regardless of cosmetic condition. Units with cracked front glass, worn casings, or failing batteries are accepted. You do not need to replace damaged screens or charge batteries before dropping them off or scheduling a pickup.

Step 2: Media Separation and Wiping

Storage Wiped or Destroyed at Intake

Many retired mobile devices have cracked screens, broken charging ports, or drained batteries that prevent them from powering on for a manual reset. OnlineRecycle.net, operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, handles non-working hardware with storage media separation.

At intake, devices with internal flash storage chips or integrated circuit boards are isolated. We make reasonable efforts to wipe or destroy data using standard processes. Storage components are wiped or physically destroyed before chassis evaluation or refurbishment. We always advise customers to back up and reset devices prior to handoff whenever the screen and controls still function.

Step 3: Central Florida Scheduling

Free Sorrento Drop-Off and Route Pickup

Whether you have two personal phones from home or a box of retired business tablets from an office upgrade, local intake options are available across Central Florida.

You can choose free drop-off in Sorrento. You can also request free scheduled route pickup available across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties. Drop-off appointments are set in 30-minute arrival windows. Pickup route appointments are scheduled in 2-hour arrival windows. Service hours run Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time. You can also call or text (321) 396-2008 with intake questions.
