How Booking Works for Free Electronics Recycling

Booking electronics recycling in Central Florida is simple and organized. OnlineRecycle.net provides free drop-off in Sorrento and free scheduled pickup across Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County. Understanding how scheduling works helps you prepare your equipment and pick the right appointment time.

Step 1: Select Your Service Method

Choose Free Drop-Off in Sorrento or Free Pickup

OnlineRecycle.net, operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC, offers two clear paths to clear out unwanted hardware. You can drop off items in Sorrento or schedule a pickup at your address.

Drop-off in Sorrento is free seven days a week, Monday through Sunday. When booking drop-off on the site, select from nine daily 30-minute drop-off windows between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM. Bringing your electronics during your scheduled slot ensures intake is ready when you arrive.

Free pickup covers three counties: Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County. Pickups run Monday through Sunday between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM. For pickup appointments, the form offers five daily 2-hour pickup windows. Choosing a 2-hour window gives you a clear timeframe for your collection day.

Step 2: Choose Your Quantity Tier

Select the Matching Batch Tier on the Form

The booking form asks you to pick an intake tier that matches your pile of equipment. Grouping items beforehand helps route planning stay accurate. OnlineRecycle.net uses four clear tiers on the form:

  • 1 to 5 items: Household drop-off or small personal cleanout.
  • 6 to 15 items: Home office, spare room, or garage cleanout.
  • 16 to 50 items: Small business, departmental, or commercial IT cleanout.
  • 50+ items: Commercial pallet load or multi-room office cleanout.

Accepted computing gear includes desktop towers, workstations, MacBooks, notebooks, and all-in-one PCs. You can also include 1U to 4U rackmount servers, network switches, routers, and firewalls. Computer peripherals include monitors, docking stations, printers, cables, and UPS battery backups. You can also include smartphones, tablets, game consoles, handheld systems, and controllers. Accepted audio hardware includes stereo receivers, amplifiers, speakers, soundbars, car head units, and subwoofers.

Step 3: Storage Handling and Confirmation

Storage Handling and Instant Confirmation Details

Many computers, laptops, and storage arrays contain internal storage media. These drives include NVMe M.2 cards, SATA SSD units, and magnetic hard drives. Solid-state and magnetic drives are separated at intake. Storage media is wiped or destroyed with reasonable effort using standard processes before any equipment refurbishment or material recovery.

To complete your booking, enter your contact details on the form. Required fields include your name, email address, phone number, and physical address for pickups. After submitting the form, your appointment is recorded right away. The site gives a booking reference ID and a calendar file.

Save your booking reference ID for your records. Open the calendar file to place the appointment on your personal schedule. If you have questions before your scheduled arrival or collection window, call or text (321) 396-2008 Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
