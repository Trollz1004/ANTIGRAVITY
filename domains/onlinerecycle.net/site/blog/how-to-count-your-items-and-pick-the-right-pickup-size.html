How to Count Your Items and Pick the Right Pickup Size

When scheduling free electronics recycling in Central Florida, counting your hardware and selecting the matching batch tier ensures an orderly intake for your home or business.

Step 1: Understand the Batch Tiers

The Four Intake Tiers on the Schedule

OnlineRecycle.net provides scheduled route pickup throughout Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County, as well as drop-off at our location in Sorrento. When you schedule an intake appointment, our booking system asks for an estimated device count. The calendar organizes equipment into four distinct tiers:

  • 1 to 5 items: Household drop-off or small personal batch.
  • 6 to 15 items: Home office, spare room, or garage cleanout.
  • 16 to 50 items: Small business, departmental, or commercial IT cleanout.
  • 50+ items: Commercial pallet load or multi-room office cleanout.

Choosing the correct tier helps route planning so pickups remain on schedule across Central Florida communities.

Step 2: Counting Major Hardware and Accessories

What Counts as an Individual Item

Count each primary standalone electronic unit as a single item. For personal and business computing, each desktop tower, workstation, MacBook, notebook, all-in-one PC, and rackmount server counts as one item. Network hardware such as enterprise switches, routers, and firewalls each count as individual devices. Standalone office peripherals including monitors, docking stations, printers, and UPS battery backups also count as one item each.

For home entertainment systems, each game console, handheld game system, home stereo receiver, power amplifier, soundbar, or car stereo head unit counts as one unit. You do not need to count individual cables or power cords separately. Bundle loose cords, power supplies, and patch cables into a single box or bag, and count that entire container as one accessory item. Collections of video game discs, movie DVDs, Blu-rays, or audio CDs can likewise be packed into a single box and counted as one media batch.

Step 3: Note Storage Media and Battery Packs

Handling Data Storage and Lithium Batteries

Data-bearing storage media and lithium battery packs require special attention during intake. Desktops, laptops, workstations, and servers often house NVMe M.2 drives, SATA solid-state drives, 2.5-inch or 3.5-inch magnetic hard drives, SAS enterprise drives, or external storage arrays.

At intake, data-bearing drives and lithium battery packs are immediately separated from general hardware. OnlineRecycle.net makes reasonable efforts to wipe or destroy data on storage media using standard processes. When completing your booking form, select your data handling preference. You can request priority data wiping for laptops, PCs, or standalone drives, or indicate that your batch contains only non-storage peripherals like monitors, printers, and cables.

Step 4: Schedule Your Intake Window

Confirm Your Appointment on the Calendar

Once you have an estimated item count, select an open window on the booking calendar. We provide five daily 2-hour pickup windows across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties. If you prefer to drop off your items directly in Sorrento, we offer nine daily 30-minute drop-off windows between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM.

Fill out your street address, city, ZIP code, estimated count tier, and a brief description of the electronics you have ready. Once submitted, your appointment is confirmed immediately with a booking reference ID and a calendar file. If you have questions about curbside or loading-dock coordination, call or text (321) 396-2008 Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
