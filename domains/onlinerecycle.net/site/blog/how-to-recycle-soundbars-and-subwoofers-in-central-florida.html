How to Recycle Soundbars and Subwoofers in Central Florida

Home theater soundbars and bulky subwoofers often take up storage space after an audio upgrade. Here is how to sort, disconnect, and recycle soundbars and subwoofers with free local intake.

Step 1: Check Your Audio Equipment

Soundbars and Subwoofers We Accept

Upgrading a living room television setup or replacing an automotive stereo often leaves behind heavy audio hardware. Soundbars and subwoofers can be awkward to store in closets or garages across Central Florida.

OnlineRecycle.net accepts powered soundbars, passive soundbars, home theater subwoofers, and car audio subwoofers. You can also include matching audio hardware in the same batch. We accept stereo receivers, power amplifiers, bookshelf speakers, floor speakers, home stereo systems, DIN head units, and car stereos.

Soundbars and subwoofers are accepted in any cosmetic condition. Enclosures with scratched finishes, worn fabric grilles, or missing remote controls are welcome at intake. You do not need to clean dust from speaker cones or test audio outputs before turning in equipment.

You can also include accompanying digital media and computing gear. We accept audio CDs, movie DVDs, Blu-rays, 4K UHD discs, video game discs, and game cartridges. Other accepted electronics include desktop towers, workstations, laptops, MacBooks, game consoles, handheld systems, computer monitors, and cables.

Step 2: Disconnect Cords and Stage Hardware

Disconnect Cords and Separate Storage Media

Preparing soundbars and subwoofers is straightforward. Unplug power cords, optical audio cables, HDMI cords, and speaker wires from the rear connection panels. Group detached power adapters and cables together in boxes or bags alongside the matching units. For car subwoofers and automotive amplifiers, disconnect wiring harnesses before handoff.

Certain modern smart soundbars, streaming amplifiers, and connected subwoofers contain internal storage or network modules with stored network settings and user profile data. If you are recycling audio equipment alongside computers, game consoles, or servers, data security is essential.

OnlineRecycle.net, operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, isolates data-bearing storage components at intake.

We make reasonable efforts to wipe or destroy data using standard processes. Solid-state drives, magnetic hard drives, and internal storage boards are separated for multi-pass erasure or physical destruction before chassis refurbishment or material recycling.

Free Sorrento Drop-Off or Scheduled County Pickup

First, you can select a free drop-off in Sorrento. Drop-off appointments are arranged in 30-minute arrival windows on our online calendar. Drop-off times run Monday through Sunday between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM.

Second, you can request free scheduled route pickup across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties. Route pickup appointments are arranged in 2-hour arrival windows. Service hours run Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time. You can also call or text (321) 396-2008 with intake questions.

Select your estimated item tier on the booking form.
