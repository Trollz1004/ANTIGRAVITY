How to Clear Out a Home Office Before a Move

Moving to a new home or workspace is the best time to clear out unused desktop computers, old laptops, extra monitors, and tangled cables. Planning your home office cleanout ahead of moving day helps you avoid packing equipment you no longer need.

Step 1: Inventory and Sort

Sort Your Home Office Equipment by Category

When preparing for a move in Central Florida, begin by gathering all electronic equipment from your desk, drawers, and storage closets. Setting aside older machines before packing prevents unnecessary clutter on moving day. Moving is an ideal time to assess what gear remains useful and what should be cleared out. OnlineRecycle.net, operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, Florida, provides free drop-off in Sorrento and scheduled pickup across Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County.

Group your computing gear first. Collect desktop towers, all-in-ones, MacBooks, notebooks, and workstations. Gather loose computer hardware such as motherboards, graphics cards, RAM sticks, and processors. If you run a network storage setup, set aside your NAS units, network routers, and firewalls.

Next, gather office peripherals and mobile devices. Look for computer monitors, docking stations, printers, cables, and UPS battery backups. You can also include old smartphones and tablets found in office drawers. Keeping accessories and power cords together makes loading and inventory checking straightforward.

Step 2: Storage Media and Data Security

Protect Personal Information on Hard Drives and Solid-State Drives

Home office computers often contain sensitive tax forms, financial records, and personal documents. Before letting go of any machine, back up any files you want to keep. If a device still powers on, perform a factory reset to wipe user accounts.

Identify every drive inside your hardware. Personal computers, laptops, and external backup drives use NVMe M.2 cards, SATA SSD units, and 2.5-inch or 3.5-inch magnetic hard drives. At OnlineRecycle.net, solid-state and magnetic drives are separated at intake. Storage media is wiped or destroyed with reasonable effort using standard processes before any chassis refurbishment or material recovery takes place.
