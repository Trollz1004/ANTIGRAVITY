Where to Recycle Game Controllers and Accessories in Central Florida

Old game controllers, cords, and gaming accessories often sit forgotten in entertainment centers. Clearing them out is simple with local Central Florida intake.

Step 1: Preparation

Preparing Game Controllers and Gaming Accessories for Intake

Video game setups collect extra controllers, connecting cords, and peripheral hardware over time. Getting these items ready for recycling takes minimal effort.

Start by gathering your gaming gear. At OnlineRecycle.net, all media and storage devices received during intake are isolated. Storage media is wiped or destroyed with reasonable effort before items are evaluated for reuse or parts recovery.

Collect companion items such as cables and power cords. Place your controllers and accessories together in a cardboard box or plastic tote.

Estimate your total item count so you can pick the right appointment tier. The scheduler offers four options: 1 to 5 items for a small batch, 6 to 15 items for a home cleanout, 16 to 50 items for larger collections, and 50+ items for commercial batches.

Step 2: Processing

Accepted Entertainment Gear and Recycling Standards

OnlineRecycle.net is operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, Florida. We accept game systems, consoles, handhelds, controllers, cables, and accessories. We also take game discs, cartridges, DVDs, Blu-rays, and CDs.

You can include game controllers alongside other electronics in the same appointment. We accept desktop computers, workstations, laptops, monitors, docking stations, home stereo receivers, audio speakers, and network equipment. Combining all your unwanted electronics into one reservation keeps cleanouts simple.

Step 3: Scheduling

Scheduling Free Drop-Off or Route Pickup

OnlineRecycle.net provides free drop-off in Sorrento and scheduled free pickup across Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County. You can reserve your appointment directly on our site.

Sorrento drop-off offers nine daily 30-minute intake windows between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM, Monday through Sunday. Route pickup offers five daily 2-hour windows across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties.

Enter your contact details and estimated device count on the booking form. When scheduling a pickup, enter your street address and city. Confirmed appointments receive an instant booking reference ID and a downloadable calendar file. You can also call or text (321) 396-2008 for curbside coordination.
