Where to Recycle Car Amplifiers and Subwoofers in Central Florida

Replacing an automotive stereo or upgrading a vehicle sound system often leaves behind old car amplifiers and subwoofers. Many car owners across Central Florida have automotive audio components stored in closets, storage sheds, or garages. Car amplifiers and subwoofers contain metal heat sinks, internal circuit boards, copper wiring, and heavy speaker magnets that should be handled through proper electronics recycling.

OnlineRecycle.net provides electronics recycling options across Central Florida. You can schedule a free drop-off in Sorrento or request a free route pickup at your home or business location.

Step 1: Check Accepted Audio Gear

Accepted Car Audio Equipment and Mixed Electronics

OnlineRecycle.net accepts car audio equipment alongside other entertainment and computer hardware. The accepted equipment list includes:

  • Head units and car stereos, amplifiers and subwoofers.
  • Stereo receivers, power amplifiers, bookshelf speakers, floor speakers, soundbars, DIN head units, and subwoofers.

If you are clearing out other electronics at the same time, you can combine them into the same pickup or drop-off appointment. Accepted items include:

  • Desktop towers, workstations, laptops, and MacBooks.
  • Servers and networking gear.
  • Phones and tablets.
  • Computer monitors and printers.
  • Cables and power supplies.
  • UPS battery backups.
  • Game consoles, handheld systems, and game controllers.
  • Game discs, movie DVDs, Blu-rays, and audio CDs.
Step 2: Choose Your Item Tier

Four Item Tiers on the Booking Form

When booking an appointment on the site, you select an estimated device count from four batch tiers:

  • 1 to 5 items (Household drop-off / small batch)
  • 6 to 15 items (Home office / garage cleanout)
  • 16 to 50 items (Small business / IT cleanout)
  • 50+ items (Commercial pallet / office load)

Whether you have one car amplifier, a single subwoofer box, or a large batch of mixed electronics, choose the tier that matches your total item count. You can describe your specific items in the notes field on the form.

Step 3: Select Data Handling Preference

Three Data Handling Preferences

Car amplifiers, subwoofers, and audio hardware do not contain digital storage drives. However, cleanouts often include computers, laptops, or external drives alongside stereo gear. The online booking form includes three data handling options:

  • Standard intake & storage wipe/destruction
  • Includes laptops/PCs/drives needing priority data wipe
  • No storage media (monitors, printers, cables, peripherals)

If your batch contains only car audio gear, you can select the choice for no storage media. If your batch includes computers or drives, storage media is wiped or destroyed with reasonable effort.

Step 4: Intake Processing

What Happens After Intake

After your equipment is collected at a drop-off or pickup appointment, items go through processing. Tested working gear is refurbished for resale. Non-resalable material is routed through responsible recycling streams where materials are separated into base metals, circuit boards, and clean plastics. Storage media is wiped or physically destroyed with reasonable effort. Nothing is mailed back.

Step 5: Booking Windows

Drop-Off and Pickup Scheduling Windows

OnlineRecycle.net offers two scheduling methods for Central Florida:

  • Sorrento Drop-Off: Nine daily 30-minute intake windows between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM, Monday through Sunday.
  • Central FL Route Pickup: Five daily 2-hour windows across Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County, Monday through Sunday.

Booking is completed directly through the online form. Submitting your appointment produces an instant booking reference ID and a downloadable .ICS calendar file for your records. You also receive a text confirmation link upon submission.
