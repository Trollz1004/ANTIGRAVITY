Where to Recycle Old Computers in Lake County, Florida

If you have retired desktop towers, laptops, workstations, or servers sitting in your home or office, you have two practical intake options in Lake County: local drop-off in Sorrento or scheduled route pickup.

Option 1: Sorrento Drop-Off

Direct Electronics Drop-Off in Sorrento

Old computers take up valuable closet space and contain storage drives that require proper handling. Operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, Florida, OnlineRecycle.net provides direct intake services for households and businesses across Lake County.

If you want to bring equipment in yourself, our Sorrento intake hub offers nine daily 30-minute drop-off windows between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM, seven days a week. Operating Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time gives you flexibility before work, during lunch, or over the weekend. Reserving a dedicated slot means your drop-off is expected and intake details are recorded promptly upon arrival.

Option 2: Free Route Pickup

Free Route Pickup Throughout Lake County

When you manage multiple desktop workstations, heavy server towers, or full office cleanouts, transporting electronics yourself is inconvenient. To make disposal simple, OnlineRecycle.net offers free scheduled route pickup across Lake County communities including Sorrento, Mount Dora, Eustis, Tavares, Leesburg, and Clermont.

Pickups run in five daily 2-hour windows across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties. Instead of transporting equipment yourself, you can request a scheduled route pickup directly through our calendar scheduler. You can also call or text (321) 396-2008 for curbside or loading-dock coordination.

Equipment Scope

Accepted Computers, Storage Media, and Peripherals

Our intake policy is clear: if it plugs in or has a battery, book a slot or ask us. We accept all varieties of personal and enterprise computer hardware. This includes desktop towers, workstations, MacBooks, notebooks, all-in-ones, and engineering workstations across x86 and Apple Silicon architectures. For office IT closets, we take 1U to 4U rackmount servers, blade enclosures, NAS units, patch panels, enterprise switches, routers, and firewalls.

Alongside complete computer systems, we accept computer parts and office peripherals such as motherboards, GPUs, RAM, processors, monitors, docking stations, printers, cables, power supplies, and UPS battery backups. Data security is handled strictly: solid-state and magnetic storage media, including NVMe M.2 drives, SATA SSDs, 2.5-inch and 3.5-inch hard drives, SAS enterprise drives, and external storage arrays, are isolated at intake. Drives go through multi-pass erasure or physical destruction before any refurbishment, resale, or responsible materials recovery.

Scheduling

How to Book Your Drop-Off or Pickup Online

Scheduling an appointment on OnlineRecycle.net takes under two minutes. Navigate to our online scheduler and choose whether you prefer a 30-minute Sorrento drop-off slot or a 2-hour route pickup window. Select an open date and time that fits your schedule, enter your contact information, and pick your batch size tier.

Our batch tiers cover every scale: 1 to 5 items for household drop-offs, 6 to 15 items for home office cleanouts, 16 to 50 items for small business upgrades, and 50+ items for commercial pallets or office loads. You can also indicate your storage wipe preference and list your items. Once submitted, the system instantly confirms your booking with a reference ID, a Google Calendar link, and a downloadable .ICS calendar file.
