Accepted Gear

Equipment Accepted for Pickup and Drop-Off

We accept computers, laptops, servers, networking gear, phones, tablets, printers, game consoles, cables, batteries, and most other consumer or office electronics. If an item plugs in or has a battery, you can book a slot or ask us.

Computing and server equipment includes desktop towers, workstations, MacBooks, notebooks, all-in-ones, engineering workstations across x86 and Apple Silicon systems, 1U to 4U rackmount servers, blade enclosures, patch panels, managed switches, NAS units, motherboards, GPUs, RAM, and processors. Mobile and office hardware includes smartphones, tablets, enterprise switches, routers, firewalls, monitors, docking stations, printers, cables, and UPS battery backups.

Entertainment and audio hardware includes PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Steam Deck, retro cartridge decks, docks, controllers, video game discs, cartridges, movie DVDs, Blu-rays, 4K UHD discs, audio CDs, stereo receivers, power amplifiers, bookshelf and floor speakers, soundbars, DIN head units, car stereos, and subwoofers.

Storage media includes NVMe M.2 drives, SATA SSDs, 2.5-inch and 3.5-inch hard drives, SAS enterprise drives, and external arrays. Solid-state and magnetic drives are separated at intake and wiped or destroyed with reasonable effort before any chassis refurbishment or resale. Customers should still factory-reset devices before turning them in whenever possible. Tested working gear is refurbished for resale, and non-working boards, chassis steel, aluminum, and copper wiring enter commodity recovery.

Scheduling

Booking a Pickup or Sorrento Drop-Off

To book on the online scheduler, select Central Florida pickup or Sorrento drop-off, choose an open date and window, and enter your name, phone number, pickup street address, city and ZIP, and device list. You can select an estimated device count tier of 1 to 5 items for a household drop-off or small batch, 6 to 15 items for a home office or garage cleanout, 16 to 50 items for a small business or IT cleanout, or 50+ items for a commercial pallet or office load, along with your data handling preference.

Your appointment is logged with a booking reference ID and a downloadable .ICS calendar file.
