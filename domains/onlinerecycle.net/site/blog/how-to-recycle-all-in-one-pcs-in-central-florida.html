Where to Recycle All-in-One PCs in Central Florida

All-in-one computers combine the motherboard, processor, and display into a single chassis. When upgrading or clearing out office hardware, Central Florida residents and businesses have clear drop-off and pickup options.

Step 1: Staging All-in-One Desktops

Accepted All-in-One Hardware and System Preparation

All-in-one desktop computers are common in business offices, medical clinics, schools, and home study areas. These machines integrate the display screen, motherboards, RAM, GPUs, and processors inside the display enclosure. When work systems are refreshed, older units often sit idle in back rooms or closets.

OnlineRecycle.net accepts all-in-one computers across Central Florida. We take all x86 and Apple Silicon all-in-ones, desktop towers, MacBooks, notebooks, and engineering workstations. We accept equipment regardless of age or operating condition. Units that do not power on, systems with damaged internal boards, and computers with cosmetic screen flaws are fully accepted for intake.

Before staging your all-in-one PC, shut down the system and unplug the power connection. Gather any detachable power cables or external power bricks that belong to each unit. If the system rests on an attached desk stand, keep the stand secured to the computer chassis. Coiling cords neatly alongside each computer keeps your hardware organized during handoff.

You can combine all-in-one PCs with other accepted equipment in a single batch. Many offices retire all-in-one systems alongside desktop towers, monitors, docking stations, network switches, routers, and UPS battery backups. Keeping all computing hardware grouped together simplifies loading and intake verification.

Step 2: Internal Storage Media Handling

Storage Drives Wiped or Destroyed at Intake

All-in-one computers store business records, accounting files, saved passwords, and personal information on internal storage media. Handling data-bearing hardware properly is a standard requirement for electronic recycling.

All-in-one computers use internal storage components, including NVMe M.2 solid-state drives, SATA SSDs, and 2.5-inch or 3.5-inch hard disk drives. At intake, data-bearing storage drives are separated from the computer chassis.

We make reasonable efforts to wipe or destroy data on storage media using standard processes. Solid-state and magnetic drives undergo multi-pass erasure or physical destruction before chassis refurbishment or component recovery proceeds.

If your organization requires drive removal prior to pickup or drop-off, you may pull the storage media ahead of time. We accept all-in-one computers and desktop towers with or without their original storage drives intact.
