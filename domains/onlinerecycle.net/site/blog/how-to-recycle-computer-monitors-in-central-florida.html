Where to Recycle Computer Monitors in Central Florida

Desktop monitors, flat panels, and display accessories clutter desks when offices reconfigure. Here is how to stage monitors and book local intake in Central Florida.

Step 1: Staging

Gathering and Staging Retired Computer Displays

When businesses or home offices replace workstation monitors, old screens tend to stack up quickly. Modern offices use LED and LCD monitors on desk mounts, adjustable stands, or dual-screen arms. Before scheduling your equipment handoff, disconnect the video and power cables. If the display includes an external power brick or detachable desk stand, keep those accessories together with the screen.

Staging display panels upright or nested screen-to-screen prevents glass scratches and cracked plastic bezels during handling. If you have original monitor boxes or bubble wrap, you can use them, but bare monitors with their cables gathered are equally acceptable.

Standard computer monitors do not contain user storage media or internal memory drives. However, many people turn in monitors alongside desktop towers, MacBooks, laptops, or external backup drives. At OnlineRecycle.net, all incoming data-bearing storage drives are separated at intake. We wipe or destroy storage media with reasonable effort before any system is tested for secondary use or material recycling.

Count your screens and any accompanying devices to determine your batch size. Our scheduler accommodates four batch tiers: 1 to 5 items for a household drop-off or small batch, 6 to 15 items for a home office cleanout, 16 to 50 items for a small business cleanout, and 50+ items for a commercial pallet or office load.

Step 2: Processing

Evaluation, Reuse, and Material Sorting

OnlineRecycle.net is operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, Florida. Each intake lot is logged and reviewed for physical condition, resale potential, and component recovery. Working computer monitors are inspected for display clarity, backlight condition, and port functionality for potential resale or reuse.

Monitors with damaged panels or internal board failures are dismantled for parts and materials. Circuit boards, internal metal chassis, power supplies, and cabling are separated for material recovery streams.

You can include desktop PCs, rackmount servers, docking stations, and UPS battery backups in the same intake batch. Combining monitors with computing equipment allows you to clear out an entire workstation setup at one time.

Step 3: Scheduling

Booking Sorrento Drop-Off or Central Florida Route Pickup

We provide free drop-off in Sorrento and scheduled free route pickup across Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County. Booking is completed directly through our website scheduler.

For Sorrento drop-off, choose from nine daily 30-minute intake windows between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM, Monday through Sunday. For route pickup, choose from five daily 2-hour windows available across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties. Once you submit your contact information and device count, you receive a booking reference ID and a downloadable calendar file. You can also call or text (321) 396-2008 for curbside or loading-dock coordination.
