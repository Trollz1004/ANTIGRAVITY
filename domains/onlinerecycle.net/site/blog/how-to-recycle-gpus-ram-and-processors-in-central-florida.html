Where to Recycle GPUs, RAM, and Processors in Central Florida

Upgrading desktop towers and workstations often leaves loose graphics cards, memory sticks, and CPUs behind. OnlineRecycle.net accepts surplus GPUs, RAM, and processors across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties with free Sorrento drop-off and scheduled route pickup.

Step 1: Preparation

Preparing Pulled Components for Safe Handover

Upgrading desktop towers, engineering workstations, and servers leaves valuable internal hardware behind. When you replace old graphics cards, system memory, or CPUs with newer parts, proper handling keeps the pulled items ready for intake.

Start by removing your GPUs, RAM modules, and processors carefully from their motherboards. Handle circuit boards by their outer edges. Avoid bending contact pins on processors or touching gold PCIe interface teeth.

Keep loose parts organized in clean boxes. You can place graphics cards flat inside a sturdy cardboard container. Bundle RAM sticks together or place them in slotted trays if available. Store processors flat with pin covers or inside small rigid boxes so contacts remain straight.

Gather any matching power cables, connection brackets, or cooling heatsinks that came with the upgraded hardware. You can combine loose components with other computing gear in the same intake batch. OnlineRecycle.net accepts desktop towers, workstations, MacBooks, notebooks, rackmount servers, NAS units, motherboards, GPUs, RAM, and processors. We also accept monitors, docking stations, and cables.

Step 2: Storage Media Processing

Handling Storage Media and Associated Hardware

Upgrades often involve moving operating systems or data across drives. When upgrading graphics cards or processors, check whether any retired storage media is included in your handover.

OnlineRecycle.net accepts NVMe M.2 modules, SATA solid-state drives, 2.5-inch and 3.5-inch hard disk drives, SAS enterprise drives, and external arrays.

At intake, data-bearing storage drives are separated from chassis and motherboards. We make reasonable efforts to wipe or destroy data using standard processes. Solid-state and magnetic drives undergo multi-pass erasure or physical destruction before any chassis refurbishment or parts sorting begins.

Hardware is evaluated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, Florida. Working components are tested and cleaned for resale and reuse. Parts that cannot be reused are separated into base materials, including circuit boards, copper heatsinks, aluminum brackets, and metal chassis.

Step 3: Scheduling Intake

Schedule Free Sorrento Drop-Off or Central Florida Route Pickup

Residents, home offices, and commercial teams in Central Florida can hand over upgraded hardware through two free intake options.

You can drop off your equipment at our Sorrento location. Drop-off appointments operate in nine daily 30-minute windows between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM, Monday through Sunday.

You can also request free route pickup across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties. Route pickup visits operate in five daily 2-hour arrival windows between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM Eastern Time.

Select your batch size when submitting your request. We support batches of 1 to 5 items for personal upgrades, 6 to 15 items for multi-system refreshes, 16 to 50 items for office cleanouts, and 50+ items for larger inventories.

Submitting the booking form provides a booking reference ID and a downloadable calendar file for your schedule. To coordinate large collections or commercial pickups, call or text (321) 396-2008.
