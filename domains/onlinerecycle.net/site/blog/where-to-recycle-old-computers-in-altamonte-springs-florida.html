Where to Recycle Old Computers in Altamonte Springs, Florida

If you live or work in Altamonte Springs and need to clear out old computers, you have two simple choices. You can drop them off in Sorrento or book a free pickup at your home or office.

Option 1: Sorrento Drop-Off

Drop Off Old Computers in Sorrento

Old desktop towers, laptops, and monitors take up closet space. OnlineRecycle.net is run by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, Florida. We provide free drop-off for households and businesses.

Our intake facility offers nine daily 30-minute drop-off windows from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM. We are open Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time. Option 2: Free Route Pickup

Free Computer Pickup in Altamonte Springs

You do not have to load heavy computer equipment into your car. OnlineRecycle.net provides free route pickup throughout Altamonte Springs and Seminole County. Our pickup vans travel across Altamonte Springs, Casselberry, Longwood, Lake Mary, and Sanford. We also serve Lake and Orange Counties.

Pickups run seven days a week in five daily 2-hour windows between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM. You can choose a window that works best for your schedule. Accepted Equipment

What Computers and Electronics Are Accepted

Our policy is simple. If it plugs into a wall or runs on a battery, we accept it. We take desktop towers, all-in-one PCs, workstations, and broken laptops. We also accept rackmount servers, network switches, routers, firewalls, and power supplies.

You can also turn in computer parts such as motherboards, graphics cards, RAM sticks, and processors. We take office peripherals including monitors, printers, docking stations, cables, and UPS battery backups. In addition, we accept game consoles, handheld devices, stereo receivers, and speakers.

Storage Handling

How Storage Drives and Materials Are Handled

Personal files and business records often remain on old computers. At intake, data-bearing storage drives are separated from computer towers and laptops. This includes NVMe solid-state drives, SATA SSDs, and magnetic hard disk drives.

Storage media is wiped or destroyed with reasonable effort before any device is tested for reuse or recycling. Usable circuit boards and metals enter recovery streams instead of heading to a local landfill. We recommend that customers back up files and run a factory reset when a device can still turn on.
