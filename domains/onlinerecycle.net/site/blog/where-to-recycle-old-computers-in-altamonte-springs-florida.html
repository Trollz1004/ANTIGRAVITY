Where to Recycle Old Computers in Altamonte Springs, Florida

If you have retired desktop towers, laptops, workstations, or servers sitting in your Altamonte Springs home or office, you have two practical intake options: free scheduled route pickup or direct drop-off in Sorrento.

Option 1: Free Route Pickup

Free Route Pickup Throughout Altamonte Springs

Altamonte Springs is a bustling community in Seminole County along the Interstate 4 corridor. Many residents and local businesses upgrade their computers regularly. When desktop workstations, laptops, monitors, and cables are replaced, the old units often sit in storage rooms or closets. Storing old equipment takes up valuable office space and leaves stored data sitting on unmanaged drives.

OnlineRecycle.net offers free scheduled route pickup across Seminole County, including Altamonte Springs, Casselberry, Longwood, and Maitland. Pickups run in five daily 2-hour windows across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties. Our service runs Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time. Instead of hauling equipment yourself, you can reserve a 2-hour route pickup window that fits your workday. Scheduling takes under two minutes on our website. You can also call or text (321) 396-2008 for curbside or loading-dock coordination.

Option 2: Sorrento Drop-Off

Direct Electronics Drop-Off in Sorrento

Some people prefer to bring electronics directly to an intake location. Operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, Florida, OnlineRecycle.net provides direct drop-off options for households and businesses across Central Florida. Sorrento is located just northwest of Altamonte Springs via State Road 46 or State Road 414 and State Road 429.

If you choose to drop off your equipment, our Sorrento intake location offers nine daily 30-minute drop-off windows between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM, seven days a week. Operating Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM gives you convenient scheduling options on weekdays and weekends. Reserving a dedicated drop-off slot means your visit is expected and your equipment is received without delay. Both Sorrento drop-off and route pickup are completely free.

Equipment Scope

Accepted Computers, Storage Media, and Peripherals

Our intake policy is simple: if it plugs into a wall or runs on a battery, book a slot or ask us. We accept complete computers, individual components, and office peripherals. This includes desktop towers, workstations, notebooks, MacBooks, and all-in-one systems. For office IT rooms, we accept 1U to 4U rackmount servers, blade servers, NAS storage units, network switches, routers, firewalls, and patch panels.

In addition to complete computers, we accept computer components and office hardware such as motherboards, processors, RAM, power supplies, monitors, printers, cables, and UPS battery backups. Storage media handling is prioritized: solid-state drives, NVMe M.2 drives, SATA SSDs, and magnetic hard drives are separated at intake. Storage media is wiped or physically destroyed with reasonable effort before any equipment is refurbished for resale or dismantled for responsible recycling.

Scheduling

How to Book Your Drop-Off or Pickup Online

Booking an intake appointment on OnlineRecycle.net takes only a few moments. Visit our website and decide whether you want a 30-minute Sorrento drop-off slot or a 2-hour Central Florida pickup window in Altamonte Springs.

Select an open date and time slot from the live calendar, enter your contact information, and select your batch tier:

  • 1 to 5 items for personal cleanouts.
  • 6 to 15 items for home office upgrades.
  • 16 to 50 items for small business computer replacements.
  • 50+ items for commercial office cleanouts.

You can also select your data handling preference, such as priority data wipe or standard intake. Once submitted, your appointment is confirmed immediately with a reference number, a Google Calendar link, and a downloadable calendar file.
