What Business Electronics Can Be Recycled

From desktop workstations and rackmount servers to switches, monitors, printers, cables, and UPS battery backups, most office and commercial hardware can be cleared out through one scheduled drop-off or route pickup.

Category 1: Computing and Server Rooms

Workstations, Laptops, and Enterprise Server Hardware

When a business relocates, refreshes employee desks, or decommissions a server closet, mixed hardware builds up fast. OnlineRecycle.net, operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, Florida, accepts commercial and office equipment for reuse, resale, parts recovery, and responsible recycling across Central Florida. Our simple rule for households and organizations alike is straightforward: if it plugs in or has a battery, book a slot or ask us.

In the computing and server category, we accept desktop towers, workstations, MacBooks, notebooks, all-in-ones, and engineering workstations across both x86 and Apple Silicon systems. For server rooms and IT closets, we take 1U to 4U rackmount servers, blade enclosures, NAS units, patch panels, and loose internal components including motherboards, GPUs, RAM, and processors.

Category 2: Networking, Mobile, and Peripherals

Switches, Phones, Monitors, Printers, and UPS Units

Office cleanouts almost always include more than computers. We accept enterprise switches, managed switches, routers, firewalls, company smartphones, and tablets. Around the workstation, we take monitors, docking stations, laser and office printers, power supplies, and boxes of mixed network and power cables. Cleared desk accessories, power cords, video adapters, and charging docks can all be packed into the same intake batch.

We also accept UPS battery backups and loose rechargeable batteries. Lithium battery packs and backup power units are isolated at intake so they are handled safely alongside your computing gear. If your office breakroom, lobby, or conference room has older media or audio equipment, we also accept game systems, DVDs, Blu-rays, CDs, stereo receivers, power amplifiers, speakers, soundbars, and car audio gear.

Category 3: Storage Media and Material Recovery

Drives, Refurbishment, and Responsible Recycling

Businesses often hold boxes of loose drives pulled during past IT upgrades. We accept NVMe M.2 drives, SATA SSDs, 2.5-inch and 3.5-inch hard drives, SAS enterprise drives, and external storage arrays. Every device is treated like it may contain personal or business data. Solid-state and magnetic drives are separated at intake for multi-pass erasure or physical destruction before any chassis refurbishment or recycling, though customers should still back up and factory-reset devices before turning them in whenever possible.

After assessment and media sanitization, tested working gear is refurbished for secondary use or evaluated for parts recovery. Non-resalable equipment, including non-working circuit boards, steel and aluminum chassis, and copper wiring, is sorted into responsible commodity recovery streams rather than dumped in a landfill.

Category 4: Commercial Batch Scheduling

Schedule a Sorrento Drop-Off or Central Florida Pickup

Whether you have 1 to 5 items, 6 to 15 items from a home office, 16 to 50 items from a small business IT cleanout, or 50+ items on a commercial pallet load, you can schedule directly on our live calendar.
