03 / ACCEPTED EQUIPMENT & STORAGE MEDIA STOP 03 / 06 · DEVICES

Accepted Equipment

  • Computing and Servers: Desktop towers, workstations, MacBooks, notebooks, rackmount servers (1U–4U), blade enclosures, patch panels, managed switches, NAS units, motherboards, GPUs, RAM, and processors.
  • Mobile and Office Electronics: Smartphones, tablets, enterprise switches, routers, firewalls, monitors, docking stations, printers, cables, and UPS battery backups.
  • Storage Media: NVMe M.2, SATA SSD, 2.5"/3.5" HDD, SAS enterprise drives, and external arrays. Solid-state and magnetic drives are separated at intake for multi-pass erasure or physical destruction before any chassis refurbishment.
  • Game Consoles and Digital Media: PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Steam Deck, retro cartridge decks, docks, controllers, video game discs, cartridges, movie DVDs, Blu-rays, 4K UHD discs, and audio CDs.
  • Home and Car Stereo: Stereo receivers, power amplifiers, bookshelf/floor speakers, soundbars, DIN head units, and subwoofers.
  • General Electronics: Most other consumer or office electronics. If it plugs in or has a battery, book a slot or ask us.

04 / CENTRAL FLORIDA SERVICE AREAS FAQ STOP 04 / 06 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I recycle electronics for free in Central Florida?

OnlineRecycle.net offers free electronics drop-off in Sorrento and free pickup throughout Central Florida, including Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County.

What hours are available for drop-off and pickup?

Intake and dispatch hours are Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Sorrento drop-off has nine 30-minute windows from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Central Florida route pickup has five 2-hour windows.

Do you accept game consoles, DVDs, and stereos?

Yes. We accept game consoles, handhelds, video games, movie discs, DVDs, Blu-rays, CDs, and home and car stereo equipment.

Do you offer secure data destruction?

Yes. Devices are assessed for storage media and handled through wipe or physical destruction workflows before resale or recycling. Customers should still factory-reset devices before turning them in whenever possible.
