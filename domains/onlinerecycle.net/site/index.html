Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I recycle electronics for free in Central Florida?

OnlineRecycle.net offers free electronics drop-off in Sorrento and free pickup throughout Central Florida, including Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County.

What electronics do you accept?

We accept computers, laptops, servers, networking gear, phones, tablets, printers, gaming consoles, cables, batteries, and most other consumer or office electronics. If it plugs in or has a battery, book a slot or ask us.

Do you offer secure data destruction?

Yes. Devices are assessed for storage media and handled through wipe or physical destruction workflows before resale or recycling. Customers should still factory-reset devices before turning them in whenever possible.

Who operates this service?

OnlineRecycle.net is operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC as a for-profit recycling, resale, pickup, and refurbishment service in Sorrento, Florida. Customer purchases and bookings are commercial service transactions.
