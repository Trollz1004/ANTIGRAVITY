## Why this works

Comments on existing posts inherit distribution from the parent post. A tagged
comment on a post that is already circulating reaches that post's audience plus
the tag's followers — the same post compounds instead of competing. Research
basis (2025–26): hashtags no longer drive reach alone, but 3–5 deliberate tags
(niche + branded + location) lift engagement ~12%+; trending/niche tags reach
~42% more new viewers on TikTok. Hard cap: **3 tags max** per action, per Joshua.

## Comment mechanics (the reach multiplier)

1. Pick 2–3 existing posts already getting traction (ours or niche-relevant viral posts).
2. Comment adds value first (a take, a stat, a question) — tags ride at the end.
3. Never identical text across posts; vary opening line per parent post.
4. Max 3 comments/day/account/platform to stay organic.
