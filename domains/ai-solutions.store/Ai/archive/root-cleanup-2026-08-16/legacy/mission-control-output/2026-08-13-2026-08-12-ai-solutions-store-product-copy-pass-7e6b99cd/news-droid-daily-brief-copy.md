# News-Droid Daily Brief: Your Morning News, Aggregated Automatically from Public RSS Sources

- **Reliable, consistent output** — Every brief is generated on a fixed daily schedule by automatically aggregating headlines from public RSS sources, so you get the same dependable format each morning without gaps or guesswork.

- **Full source transparency** — Each item in the brief links directly back to the public RSS sources it came from, so you can verify every story at its original publisher with one click.

- **Effortless consumption** — Instead of checking dozens of sites, you get one concise digest compiled from public RSS sources you can scan in minutes — no filtering, no hunting, no noise.
