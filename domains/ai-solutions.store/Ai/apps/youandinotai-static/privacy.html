Privacy Policy

Plain English, because you should actually be able to read this. You & I, Not AI is a dating platform for verified humans. Here is exactly what we collect, why, who sees it, and how you get it deleted.

01Who we are

The service at youandinotai.com and its mobile apps is operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("we"). Contact: support@youandinotai.com.

02What we collect

  • Account data — name or display name, email, date of birth, password (stored hashed, never in plain text), profile photos, and bio text you write.
  • Identity and age verification — every member must pass identity and age verification through an independent verification provider before messaging anyone. We receive the verification result (pass/fail) and date-of-birth confirmation. We do not store images of your government ID on our servers; the verification provider processes those under its own privacy policy.
  • Content you create — messages, swipes, matches, reports you file, and game activity.
  • Technical data — device type, app version, IP address, and security logs used to keep bots and abusers out.
  • Approximate location — only if you enable location-based matching, and only at city-level precision. You can turn it off in settings.

03What we use it for

  • Running the service: matching, messaging, and showing you profiles.
  • Safety: verifying humans, detecting bots and fraud, enforcing our Community Guidelines, and protecting minors by keeping them off the platform entirely.
  • Support: answering you when you write in.
  • Legal: complying with law and valid legal process.

We do not use your messages to train advertising profiles. We do not run third-party ad networks inside the service.

04Who we share it with

  • Service providers under contract, only what they need: our identity verification provider (to verify you), our payment processor Square (for purchases made on our website — we never see or store your full card number), and cloud hosting infrastructure.
  • Law enforcement when required by valid legal process, or when necessary to report child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and applicable authorities, per our Child Safety Standards.
  • No sale. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

05How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, personal data is deleted within 30 days, except: records we must keep by law, payment records held by the processor, and safety reports or content preserved in connection with abuse investigations or legal obligations.

06Your rights

Wherever you live, we honor the basics: access, correction, deletion, and export of your data. Residents of certain jurisdictions (for example California under the CCPA/CPRA, or the EU/UK under the GDPR) have additional statutory rights; we honor verified requests under those laws.

To delete your account and data, use the in-app path or follow Delete Account. For anything else, email support@youandinotai.com.

07Minors

This service is for adults 18 and older, full stop.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Age is checked at signup and through identity verification. If we learn an account belongs to a minor, we terminate it and delete its data. See our Child Safety Standards.

08Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed. Access to production data is restricted to the minimum people and systems needed to run the service, and security-relevant actions are logged. No system is unbreakable; if a breach affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

09Changes

If this policy changes materially, we post the new version here and update the effective date, and we notify active members in the app before it takes effect.
