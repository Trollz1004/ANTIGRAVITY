What the game is being built around

These are the design pillars guiding development right now. They describe intent and direction, not a finished feature list.

Combat

Real-time action combat

Combat is being designed around positioning, timing, and player skill rather than target-and-rotate menus.

Progression

Life skills as main progression

Gathering, crafting, fishing, cooking, and trading are planned as core paths forward, not optional side activities bolted onto combat leveling.

World

A world that changes on a schedule

Day and night, and recurring world events, are meant to shift what's available, what's dangerous, and where the opportunity is.

PvP

Dangerous, but rule-bound

Open-world PvP is being designed with real consequences and a level-scaling approach, so risk stays meaningful without being arbitrary.

NPCs

NPCs meant to feel alive

NPC systems are planned to react to events and player actions instead of standing in place waiting for a quest to be accepted.

Monetization

Convenience and style, not power

The current intent is that anything sold affects convenience or appearance only — never combat power.

A few common questions

When does DREAM Online launch?

There's no launch date yet. The project is in active development, and this page will be updated once a date is set.

What will it cost to play?

Pricing hasn't been decided. Whatever the model ends up being, the stated intent is that paid items affect convenience or cosmetics only, never combat power.

How can I support the project?

Development can currently be backed through the "Back development" link on this page, and you can follow progress directly on GitHub.

Is DREAM Online connected to other projects?

DREAM Online is part of a small family of independently operated platforms that carry one mission: help kids in need. That mission has its own public page at untilnokidinneed.com.
