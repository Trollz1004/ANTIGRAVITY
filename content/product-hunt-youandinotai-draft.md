**Description** (use as-is or trim)

YouAndINotAI is a human-verified platform for real connections. Every profile requires selfie verification so you know you're talking to a real person.

Matching is prompt-first: like or comment on a specific prompt, photo, or interest for conversations with context. Move from chat to clear first-date plans with public meetup details and optional check-ins.

Built around what works in great dating apps — verification, rich profiles, safer chat, clear intent — minus the bots.

- Visible trust cues and verification status on every profile  
- Prompt-rich discovery and contextual matching  
- Plans feature for safer first meetups  
- Report, block, restrict, and support tools always accessible  
- Founding membership for full verification + matching access  

Tired of fake profiles and wasted time? Join the verified network.
