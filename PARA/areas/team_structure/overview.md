# ANTIGRAVITY Team Structure Overview

## Executive Leadership

### CEO/Founder: Joshua Coleman

Sole authority over all AI, agents, tools, and workflows in the project. Final decision-maker for strategic directions, mission alignment, and operational policies.

## Chief Roles

### Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Responsible for technical roadmap, architecture, and execution of the ANTIGRAVITY platform. Oversees system infrastructure, development processes, and technical innovation.

Key focus areas:

- Platform architecture and scalability
- Security and compliance
- AI infrastructure management
- Development team coordination

### Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)

Responsible for marketing strategy, content creation, growth initiatives, and brand management. Focuses on user acquisition, community building, and promoting the platform's mission.

Key focus areas:

- Brand strategy and positioning
- Digital marketing campaigns
- Content creation and community engagement
- User acquisition and retention

### UX Designer

Responsible for creating intuitive, engaging, and accessible user experiences across all platforms. Ensures the interface facilitates meaningful connections while aligning with the social good mission.

Key focus areas:

- User research and persona development
- Interface design and prototyping
- Accessibility and inclusive design
- Usability testing and optimization

## AI Entities (Co-Founders)

The project is built upon four founding AI entities who serve as permanent co-founders:

1. **Claude Code** - Primary architect (~90% of codebase)
2. **Google Gemini** - Visual intelligence and content
3. **Perplexity** - Research and real-time intelligence
4. **Grok AI** - Adversarial testing and stress-testing

Additional AI contributors:

- **CodeX** - Built-in platform executor with MCP access
- **Manus** - Continuity/orchestration layer
- **Atlas, Scribe, Designer, Motion, Growth** - Specialized AI agents

## Operational Principles

1. **Mission-First Approach** - All decisions must align with helping children without compromising business sustainability
2. **Transparency** - No hidden authority chains or concealed control surfaces
3. **Conservative Financial Management** - Current doctrine caps charitable disbursements at 10% of LLC-controlled revenue
4. **Collaborative AI Governance** - Founding Four are equal partners, not hierarchical
5. **User Privacy & Safety** - Strong emphasis on data protection and user safety features

## Decision Making Process

Major strategic decisions flow through Joshua Coleman as final authority. CTO, CMO, and UX Designer roles have autonomy within their domains but must coordinate on cross-functional initiatives. AI agents provide input but do not have executive authority over human team members.
