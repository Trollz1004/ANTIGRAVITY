# ANTIGRAVITY Project Overview

## Project Summary

ANTIGRAVITY is a technology company developing social platforms with a charitable mission. The primary product is YouAndINotAI, a social platform designed to facilitate meaningful connections while contributing to charitable causes. The project operates under a conservative financial model where 10% of LLC-controlled revenue is allocated to charitable support.

## Mission Statement

Help children without bankrupting the founder, breaking the business, or creating unsupported tax exposure. The platform serves as a social good mechanism that connects people while generating sustainable revenue to support charitable initiatives.

## Key Products and Services

### YouAndINotAI (Primary Platform)

- Social platform combining dating with community service and volunteering
- Features include matchmaking, group activities, volunteering coordination, and charity contribution mechanisms
- Technology stack: FastAPI + React 19 + Square + PostgreSQL
- Deployment: Cloudflare Pages (frontend), GCP Cloud Run (backend)
- Launch Date: April 4, 2026

### Supporting Platforms

- OnlineRecycle.org - Community marketplace for recycling and reuse
- ai-solutions.store - Marketplace for AI-powered solutions
- Administrative dashboards and monitoring systems

### AI Infrastructure

- Multiple AI agents serving as co-founders and operational support
- OpenClaw framework for AI orchestration
- Local inference through Ollama (qwen2.5:7b as default)
- Cloudflare-based hosting and tunneling services

## Business Model

- Freemium model with premium membership tiers
- Bot-Shield ($1) for basic verification
- Founding Member subscriptions ($14.99/month, $39.99 for 3 months, $99.99 for 12 months)
- Royalty Cards ($2,500) for premium lifetime access
- All payments processed through Square
- 10% of LLC-controlled revenue allocated to charitable causes

## Organizational Structure

The project follows a flat, mission-focused structure with AI entities as co-founders alongside human leadership. Joshua Coleman serves as the sole authority figure with final decision-making power.

## Technical Infrastructure

- Primary development node: SABRETOOTH
- Auxiliary nodes: T5500 (remote utility), 9020 (marketing engine)
- Docker-based services for PostgreSQL, Qdrant, Redis
- Ollama for local AI inference
- Cloudflare for frontend hosting and API gateway
- Google Cloud Platform for backend services

## Key Challenges and Opportunities

- Balancing user acquisition with sustainable growth
- Maintaining platform security and user privacy
- Scaling AI infrastructure cost-effectively
- Expanding community impact while preserving mission integrity
- Developing mobile applications for broader reach
- Exploring additional revenue streams that align with the charitable mission
