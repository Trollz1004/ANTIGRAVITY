# Muse plan (Meta: Facebook, Instagram, Threads)

Muse cannot push to GitHub. Claude copied this from Muse's answer in Josh's Meta AI chat "Planning social media routine" on 2026-11-26 and trimmed it. Facts marked (check) are Muse's reading of the sites; Claude checks each against the site before a post uses it.

## What Muse can and cannot do (its own words, short)
- Can: draft posts, keep a calendar, keep the plan and log format clean.
- Cannot, from that chat: post on its own, or run a background drip of likes, follows, joins or replies. Josh says Muse does post for him when he asks it in his own Meta AI app; that is the posting path.

## Pace
Three posts a week, at most one a day, alternating sites (for example Tuesday OnlineRecycle, Thursday YouAndINotAI, Saturday OnlineRecycle). A new caption per platform, a few hours or a day apart. Vary the format: photo, short text, carousel.

## Post ideas, OnlineRecycle
1. The drawer cleanout: a real junk drawer or garage box, booked a slot, handed it over, done.
2. What happens to a drive: storage media is wiped or physically destroyed (home page says so).
3. What we take: one category a week from the home page list.
4. How booking works: free drop-off in Sorrento or scheduled route pickup across Lake, Orange and Seminole Counties, in the home page's own words.
5. Office cleanout: only the batch sizes the home page itself lists; if it lists none, skip this idea.
6. Consoles and discs: one item, an honest caption about whether it still powers on.

## Post ideas, YouAndINotAI (18 and over in every post)
1. Why the check exists: a short question and a one dollar check before you can message anyone.
2. What a profile shows: goals, an opener, first-date comfort (check).
3. Boards and group chat: topic boards for slower talk, a live chat for tonight (check).
4. Safety in plain words: block and report, a copy of your data, 30-day deletion.
5. Prices without a hard sell: the one dollar check once; Founder plans optional.

## Slow drip, Muse's advice for Josh's own hands
Read the whole thread before replying; skip it if there is nothing real to add. Fresh words every time. No link unless the thread asks. Join slowly and follow each group's promotion rules. Dating links never go in unrelated groups, and never toward anyone under 18.
