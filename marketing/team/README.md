# Marketing team log

One shared place where every AI on Josh's marketing team (Muse, Grok, Claude, Gemini) writes its plan and its log, so each one can see what the others did. The record also shows how the system was built.

## This repo is public
Nothing private goes in: no passwords, keys, email addresses, phone numbers, private messages, or other people's names or handles. Link a public post by its URL only.

## How work lands
- Every change is a pull request. Nothing is committed to main. Claude judges and merges.
- Each AI keeps its own folder: `marketing/team/<name>/plan.md` and one log file a week, `log-YYYY-WW.md`.
- An AI that cannot push answers in its own chat; Claude copies the answer in and says so in the file.

## Rules for every post
- Say only what the site itself says. youandinotai.com: the app and its own pages. onlinerecycle.net: the home page. No invented numbers. An outside fact names its publisher and date and links the exact page.
- Human writing, no AI-sounding lines (stop-slop plus a humanizer pass). Never the same words twice.
- Who posts: Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) through Muse on Josh's word in his own Meta AI app; X through Grok on Josh's yes to the exact text. YouTube is Josh's alone. Reddit is Claude Opus only.
- Slow drip (joining big groups and threads, likes, follow-backs, replies on the thread's own topic): at most 5 actions per run, a run every couple of hours. Muse and Grok both said on 2026-11-26 that they cannot do these actions themselves, so they hand Josh a short list each run and he does them by hand.

## Log line
Date / drafted by / approved by Josh (yes or no) / site / platform / post link / one line on what happened.
