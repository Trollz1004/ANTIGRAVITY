Small uppercase mono pill for tags, plan markers, counts. Tinted/outlined by default; `solid` for a filled "Most Popular"-style marker.

```jsx
<Badge tone="cyan">New</Badge>
<Badge tone="pink" solid>Most Popular</Badge>
<Badge tone="gold" icon={<ShieldCheck size={11} />}>Verified</Badge>
```

Tones: `cyan` `pink` `rose` `gold` `slate` `success`.
