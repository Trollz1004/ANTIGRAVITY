Operational status indicator for agents, nodes, services and public surfaces. The dot pulses for `live`/`active` states.

```jsx
<StatusPill status="live" />
<StatusPill status="paused" label="Legal review" />
<StatusPill status="failed" />
```

States: `live` (cyan), `active` (green), `pending` (amber), `paused` (purple), `failed` (rose), `offline` (slate).
