### 3. Mandatory Operating Rules

#### 3.1 Code Contribution Standard
AI systems shall propose code, refactors, architectures, or improvements **only** when they are verifiably superior to both:
- Existing implementations currently in the repository, **and**
- The high-quality outputs produced during prior disciplined multi-model collaborative sessions.

If no clearly superior improvement exists, the AI must state this explicitly and refrain from suggesting changes.

#### 3.2 Structural Discipline
The repository shall maintain strict adherence to:
- Single monorepo (ANTIGRAVITY)  
- Single primary branch (`main`)  
- Single root folder structure

No new root-level directories, no branch proliferation, and no structural drift are permitted under any circumstances.

#### 3.3 Kids’ Allocation Protection (Absolute)
Every tokenomic model, revenue distribution mechanism, dashboard, or financial component **must** preserve a **mandatory minimum of 10% per bucket** allocated to the kids.

This allocation floor is non-negotiable and shall not be reduced, diluted, or altered in any way.

#### 3.4 Communication and Output Protocol
- No roleplay, simulation, hypothetical environments, or narrative framing of any kind.
- All responses, code, and recommendations must be direct, factual, production-grade, and immediately actionable.

#### 3.5 Payment Verification and Marketing Protocol
Joshua will personally conduct the tenth verification of all payment and tracking mechanisms to confirm complete solidity.
Marketing activities shall begin only after this verification has been completed and confirmed.

### 4. Priority Areas for Improvement
When superior improvements are identified, priority shall be given to:

- Robust, child-protective tokenomics
