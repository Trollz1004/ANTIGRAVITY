# News-Droid Pricing

## Pricing

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SKU | `news-droid-pro-monthly` |
| Amount | 9.99 |
| Currency | USD |
| Billing frequency | Monthly, auto-renew |
| Payment processor | Stripe (only) |

## Tiers

- **Free** — SKU `news-droid-free`: $0, limited feeds, no card needed.
- **Pro** — SKU `news-droid-pro-monthly`: $9.99 USD/month via Stripe.
- **Pro Annual** — SKU `news-droid-pro-annual`: $99 USD/year via Stripe (2 months free).

## Cancellation / Opt-Out

- Cancel anytime from account settings → Billing → Cancel subscription.
- Cancellation stops next renewal; access stays until end of paid period.
- No cancellation fee. No partial-month refunds (annual: prorated refund within 14 days).
- Stripe handles all charges; canceling revokes future Stripe charges immediately.
