# Service Areas: Central Florida Electronics Recycling & Free Pickup

## Overview
OnlineRecycle.net provides free electronics recycling and pickup services across Central Florida. You can drop off items for free in Sorrento or schedule a free route pickup at your home or business. We serve Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

## Service Locations

### Sorrento
Sorrento is our central drop-off location. Customers can schedule one of nine daily 30-minute drop-off intake windows between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM, Monday through Sunday. Call or text (321) 396-2008 for curbside coordination.

### Scheduled Route Pickup Counties
Central Florida route pickups run across three counties with five daily 2-hour dispatch windows:
- Lake County: Scheduled 2-hour route pickup windows for household cleanouts and commercial loads.
- Orange County: Scheduled 2-hour route pickup windows for household cleanouts and commercial loads.
- Seminole County: Scheduled 2-hour route pickup windows for household cleanouts and commercial loads.

## Accepted Equipment
- Computing and Servers: Desktop towers, workstations, MacBooks, notebooks, rackmount servers (1U–4U), blade enclosures, patch panels, managed switches, NAS units, motherboards, GPUs, RAM, and processors.
- Mobile and Office Electronics: Smartphones, tablets, enterprise switches, routers, firewalls, monitors, docking stations, printers, cables, and UPS battery backups.
- Storage Media: NVMe M.2, SATA SSD, 2.5"/3.5" HDD, SAS enterprise drives, and external arrays. Solid-state and magnetic drives are isolated at intake for multi-pass erasure or physical destruction.
- Game Consoles and Digital Media: PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Steam Deck, retro cartridge decks, docks, controllers, video game discs, cartridges, movie DVDs, Blu-rays, 4K UHD discs, and audio CDs.
- Home and Car Stereo: Stereo receivers, power amplifiers, bookshelf/floor speakers, soundbars, DIN head units, and subwoofers.
- General Electronics: Most consumer or office electronics that plug in or run on a battery.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- Q: Where can I recycle electronics for free in Central Florida?
  A: OnlineRecycle.net offers free electronics drop-off in Sorrento and free pickup throughout Central Florida, including Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County.
- Q: What hours are available for drop-off and pickup?
  A: Intake and dispatch hours are Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Sorrento drop-off has nine 30-minute windows from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Central Florida route pickup has five 2-hour windows.
- Q: Do you accept game consoles, DVDs, and stereos?
  A: Yes. We accept game consoles, handhelds, video games, movie discs, DVDs, Blu-rays, CDs, and home and car stereo equipment.
- Q: What happens to personal and business data on storage devices?
  A: Solid-state and magnetic drives are pulled for multi-pass erasure or physical destruction before any chassis refurbishment. Customers should still factory-reset devices before turning them in whenever possible.
