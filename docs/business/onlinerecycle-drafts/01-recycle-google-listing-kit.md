## 3. Business Description (686 characters)
OnlineRecycle.net is a for-profit electronics recycling and resale service operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC in Sorrento, Florida. We provide free drop-off in Sorrento and scheduled route pickup across Lake, Orange, and Seminole Counties. We accept computers, laptops, servers, phones, tablets, networking gear, printers, gaming consoles, digital media, and home and car audio equipment. Storage media is wiped or physically destroyed. Tested working gear is refurbished for resale, and non-resalable material enters responsible recycling streams. Schedule a 30-minute drop-off window or a 2-hour pickup window on our calendar, or call or text (321) 396-2008.

## 4. Services (One sentence each)

- Central Florida Route Pickup: Schedule a free 2-hour pickup window across Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County.

- Mobile and Office Gear Recycling: We accept smartphones, tablets, enterprise switches, routers, firewalls, monitors, docking stations, printers, cables, and UPS backups.
- Game Consoles and Media Recycling: We accept consoles, handhelds, controllers, video game discs, cartridges, DVDs, Blu-rays, and audio CDs.
- Home and Car Audio Recycling: We accept stereo receivers, power amplifiers, speakers, soundbars, DIN head units, and subwoofers.
- Storage Media Sanitization: Solid-state and magnetic drives are separated at intake for multi-pass erasure or physical destruction.
