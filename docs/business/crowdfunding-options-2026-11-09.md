# Crowdfunding Options for DREAM Online (2026-11-09)

CLAUDE's N Joshua's · #TeamClaudeForLife · Built with Google Gemini

**Outcome:** Among third-party rewards platforms, **Ko-fi** (paired with direct first-party Founder Pack checkout on `dream-online.net` via Square) is the best first campaign for **DREAM Online** because it charges `0%` to `5%` with zero upfront cost, pays out instantly to a Florida LLC PayPal Business account without a multi-week reserve hold, permits AI-assisted art and code when disclosed honestly, and supports digital cosmetic and convenience reward tiers.

This study evaluates rewards-based funding paths only for **DREAM Online**, a free-to-play 3D browser MMORPG built in-house on WebGPU and WebGL2 (`src/engine/`) and operated by a Florida limited liability company (`Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC`). Equity crowdfunding, profit shares, tokens, and company stakes are excluded so the business never triggers securities registration expenses. Every reward tier evaluated below delivers product value only through visual cosmetics and account convenience, and nothing sold for money ever grants combat stats, gear power, or gameplay advantage. No accounts were opened and zero dollars were spent for this research (`cost_usd === 0` until Joshua says `finalize`).

## 1. Kickstarter

- **Who it suits, fees, and payout timing:** Kickstarter suits large, fixed-goal milestone launches that already have a warm mailing list of several thousand players ready to pledge on day one ([Kickstarter Rules](https://www.kickstarter.com/rules)). Kickstarter runs on an all-or-nothing funding model where the platform takes a `5%` fee on total funds raised plus Stripe payment processing fees of `3% + $0.30` per pledge of `$10` or more, and `5% + $0.08` per pledge under `$10` ([Kickstarter Fees](https://www.kickstarter.com/help/fees)). Money does not arrive during the campaign; Stripe spends `14 calendar days` after the campaign ends collecting and retrying backer cards, and the net bank transfer takes another `3 to 14 business days`, meaning funds arrive `17 to 28 days` after the campaign closes ([Kickstarter Terms of Use](https://www.kickstarter.com/terms-of-use/nov2022)).
- **Terms on games, AI-made art, and AI-written code:** Video games are a primary category on Kickstarter, provided the creator shows honest working footage rather than simulated renders ([Kickstarter Rules](https://www.kickstarter.com/rules)). Kickstarter permits AI-assisted art and AI-written code only when human creative direction leads the project, requires a mandatory disclosure questionnaire during submission that places a public `Use of AI` label on the campaign page, and requires the creator to confirm rights and credit for training data ([Kickstarter AI Policy](https://updates.kickstarter.com/introducing-our-new-ai-policy/)). Projects that rely entirely on generative output without substantial human creation or that hide AI use face suspension ([Kickstarter AI Policy](https://updates.kickstarter.com/introducing-our-new-ai-policy/)).
- **In-game cosmetic rewards for a free-to-play game:** Kickstarter allows digital rewards such as in-game cosmetic skins, founder titles, soundtrack downloads, and digital artbooks, while banning financial instruments, raffles, lotteries, and gambling items ([Kickstarter Prohibited Items](https://www.kickstarter.com/rules/prohibited)). Because DREAM Online is free to play and sells no combat power, cosmetic skins and account convenience unlocks delivered by redemption code comply with Kickstarter reward rules ([Kickstarter Rules](https://www.kickstarter.com/rules)).
- **Florida LLC eligibility:** A Florida LLC can register as a United States business entity on Kickstarter using its federal Employer Identification Number (EIN), a United States business checking account, and government identification for the LLC's responsible representative ([Kickstarter Terms of Use](https://www.kickstarter.com/terms-of-use/nov2022)).

## 2. Indiegogo

- **Who it suits, fees, and payout timing:** Indiegogo suits fixed-goal or flexible-goal campaigns that want post-campaign late pledges and built-in pledge management ([Indiegogo Fees](https://www.indiegogo.com/en/info/fees)). Indiegogo charges a `5%` platform fee on total funds raised plus payment processing fees of `3% + $0.20` per transaction in United States dollars ([Indiegogo Help: Fees](https://help.indiegogo.com/article/596-fees)). While Indiegogo sends `95%` of net funds on a weekly payout cycle starting roughly `15 business days` after campaign completion, its Reserved Funds Policy withholds `5%` of total campaign funds for `180 days` to cover chargebacks unless the creator proves 100% reward fulfillment early ([Indiegogo Reserved Funds Policy](https://www.indiegogo.com/en/terms/reservedfunds)).
- **Terms on games, AI-made art, and AI-written code:** Indiegogo hosts video game campaigns and requires creators to clearly disclose any generative AI used in campaign text, promotional art, or game assets ([Indiegogo Creator Terms](https://www.indiegogo.com/en/terms/creators)). Undisclosed AI assets or synthetic media that misrepresent team identity can trigger campaign suspension or removal from platform discovery ([Indiegogo Creator Terms](https://www.indiegogo.com/en/terms/creators)).
- **In-game cosmetic rewards for a free-to-play game:** Indiegogo allows digital perks including cosmetic item packs, character customization unlocks, and convenience slots, provided the campaign page states clear delivery dates and does not offer equity, revenue participation, or gambling mechanics ([Indiegogo Creator Terms](https://www.indiegogo.com/en/terms/creators)).
- **Florida LLC eligibility:** A Florida LLC can run an Indiegogo campaign by verifying its United States EIN, Florida business address, matching business bank account, and authorized representative identity ([Indiegogo Creator Terms](https://www.indiegogo.com/en/terms/creators)).

## 3. Patreon

- **Who it suits, fees, and payout timing:** Patreon suits ongoing monthly memberships and developer logs where players back continuous world updates rather than a single one-time funding goal ([Patreon Creator Fees Overview](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview)). Patreon charges new creators a `10%` platform fee on membership and digital shop sales plus payment processing fees of roughly `2.9% + $0.30` for standard pledges above `$3` and `5% + $0.10` for micro-pledges of `$3` or less ([Patreon Creator Fees Overview](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview)). Creators can trigger manual payouts once every `24 hours` or enable automatic monthly transfers on the `5th of each month`, with bank deposits arriving in `1 to 5 business days` ([Patreon Paying Out Your Earnings](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/203913499-Paying-out-your-earnings)).
- **Terms on games, AI-made art, and AI-written code:** Patreon welcomes indie game studios and allows AI-generated art, voice, and code on `All Audiences` pages such as DREAM Online, provided the work follows community guidelines and does not impersonate real individuals without consent ([Patreon Community Guidelines](https://www.patreon.com/policy/guidelines) and [Patreon AI Policies](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/34055590411789-Understanding-Patreon-s-AI-policies-for-Adult-18-creators)).
- **In-game cosmetic rewards for a free-to-play game:** Patreon supports recurring or one-time digital rewards such as monthly cosmetic armor dyes, companion spirit visual effects, Discord role sync, and extra character slots, as long as rewards do not involve raffles or chance-based loot boxes ([Patreon Community Guidelines](https://www.patreon.com/policy/guidelines)).
- **Florida LLC eligibility:** A Florida LLC can register on Patreon by submitting an IRS Form W-9 with the LLC name and EIN and linking the LLC's United States bank account or PayPal Business account ([Patreon Paying Out Your Earnings](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/203913499-Paying-out-your-earnings)).

## 4. Ko-fi

- **Who it suits, fees, and payout timing:** Ko-fi suits independent studios that want a public crowdfunding goal bar, one-time tips, recurring memberships, and a digital reward shop on a single page without waiting weeks for payouts ([Ko-fi Beginner FAQs](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/29745501815709-Beginner-FAQs)). On the free plan with optional Contributor status turned off, Ko-fi charges `0%` platform fee on one-time tips and `5%` on memberships and shop sales, while the optional `$12/month` Ko-fi Gold plan drops the platform fee to `0%` across all sales; supporters pay direct through PayPal or Stripe, which deduct standard processor fees of roughly `2.9% to 3.49% + $0.30 to $0.49` ([Does Ko-fi Take a Fee](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002506494-Does-Ko-fi-take-a-fee)). Because Ko-fi never holds funds in a platform escrow wallet, money arrives **instantly** in the creator's own PayPal Business or Stripe account at the moment of purchase ([Ko-fi Beginner FAQs](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/29745501815709-Beginner-FAQs)).
- **Terms on games, AI-made art, and AI-written code:** Ko-fi allows game developers to fund browser and desktop games and permits AI-assisted art and code provided the creator is transparent and never misleads buyers into believing AI-generated illustrations were hand-drawn ([Ko-fi Stance on AI](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/19789627403293-Ko-fi-s-stance-on-AI)). Ko-fi also blocks third-party AI training bots from scraping creator pages ([Ko-fi Stance on AI](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/19789627403293-Ko-fi-s-stance-on-AI)).
- **In-game cosmetic rewards for a free-to-play game:** Ko-fi Shop and Ko-fi Membership tiers allow instant delivery of digital redemption keys, cosmetic weapon skins, companion visual effects, and account convenience upgrades for a free-to-play game ([Ko-fi Terms](https://more.ko-fi.com/terms)).
- **Florida LLC eligibility:** A Florida LLC can connect its PayPal Business account directly to Ko-fi so all receipts display the legal business name (`Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC`) rather than a personal name ([Ko-fi Beginner FAQs](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/29745501815709-Beginner-FAQs)).

## 5. itch.io

- **Who it suits, fees, and payout timing:** itch.io suits playable HTML5 browser demos, indie game storefronts, and pay-what-you-want supporter packs ([itch.io HTML5 Games](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5)). itch.io uses Open Revenue Sharing where the creator sets the platform fee anywhere from `0%` to `100%` (default `10%`), plus PayPal or Stripe processing fees of `2.9% + $0.30` ([itch.io Pricing](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing)). Creators can choose `Direct to you` mode, where payments land immediately in the creator's connected PayPal or Stripe account, or `Collected by itch.io` mode, where funds clear after a `7-day` hold and require a manual payout request that takes several business days to process ([itch.io Payments](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) and [itch.io Payout Modes Comparison](https://itch.io/t/2215892/comparing-payout-modes-collected-by-itchio-paid-later-and-direct-to-you)).
- **Terms on games, AI-made art, and AI-written code:** itch.io is built specifically for games and embeds HTML5/WebGL/WebGPU builds directly inside a browser page ([itch.io HTML5 Games](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5)). However, itch.io requires a mandatory `Generative AI` disclosure covering graphics, sound, text, and code (excluding traditional procedural generation or pathfinding), tags the project as `AI Generated`, hides it from users who enable the `No AI` browse filter, and removes search indexing completely if AI usage is left undisclosed ([itch.io Quality Guidelines](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines) and [itch.io Generative AI Disclosure Tagging](https://itch.io/t/4309690/generative-ai-disclosure-tagging)).
- **In-game cosmetic rewards for a free-to-play game:** Because DREAM Online is free to play on `dream-online.net`, an itch.io page can host a free browser launcher build and sell optional Supporter Pack files or redeemable cosmetic keys above a `$0` minimum price ([itch.io Pricing](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing)).
- **Florida LLC eligibility:** A Florida LLC can operate an itch.io seller account by completing the built-in IRS Form W-9 tax interview with the LLC's EIN and linking the LLC's PayPal Business or Stripe account ([itch.io Payments](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments)).

## 6. GitHub Sponsors

- **Who it suits, fees, and payout timing:** GitHub Sponsors suits open-source software libraries, developer tools, and public engine repositories where backers fund ongoing code maintenance ([About GitHub Sponsors](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/getting-started-with-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors)). GitHub charges `0%` platform and processing fees on sponsorships from personal accounts (`100%` goes to the recipient), and charges up to `6%` (`3%` GitHub service fee plus `3%` credit card fee) on sponsorships sent by organization accounts ([About GitHub Sponsors](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/getting-started-with-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors)). Payouts run through Stripe Connect: the very first sponsorship payout is held for a `60-day` probation window, and subsequent payouts arrive monthly around the `22nd` or third Thursday of the month once the balance reaches a `$100` minimum threshold ([GitHub Sponsors Additional Terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-sponsors-additional-terms)).
- **Terms on games, AI-made art, and AI-written code:** GitHub Sponsors allows AI-written code and AI-made assets inside hosted repositories, provided the repositories comply with the GitHub Acceptable Use Policies ([GitHub Sponsors Additional Terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-sponsors-additional-terms)).
- **In-game cosmetic rewards for a free-to-play game:** GitHub Sponsors is a weak fit for in-game cosmetic sales because the GitHub Sponsors Additional Terms frame sponsorships around supporting open-source development and developer recognition (such as badge roles, README credits, or sponsor-only repo access) rather than operating a commercial retail shop for game items ([GitHub Sponsors Additional Terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-sponsors-additional-terms)).
- **Florida LLC eligibility:** A Florida LLC can register a GitHub Organization account for GitHub Sponsors, submit an IRS Form W-9, and receive transfers into a United States business bank account via Stripe Connect ([GitHub Sponsors Additional Terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-sponsors-additional-terms)).

## 7. Other Rewards Platforms Relevant to a Free Browser MMORPG

### BackerKit Crowdfunding (Evaluated and Ruled Out)

- **Who it suits, fees, and payout timing:** BackerKit Crowdfunding suits tabletop and video game studios with an established backer community, charging a `5%` platform fee plus `2.9% + $0.30` Stripe processing fee in the United States, with funds held for `7 days` and deposited roughly `14 days` after campaign close ([BackerKit Pricing](https://www.backerkit.com/pricing) and [BackerKit How Do I Receive Funds](https://help.backerkit.com/article/798-how-do-i-receive-funds)).
- **Why it is ruled out for DREAM Online (AI Policy):** BackerKit's policy explicitly bans crowdfunding projects from using content or assets created solely with generative AI tools ([BackerKit Policies and Guidelines](https://help.backerkit.com/article/929-backerkit-policies-and-guidelines)). Because DREAM Online openly celebrates its AI co-builders and live AI NPCs, BackerKit is incompatible with our studio model.

### First-Party Founder Packs on `dream-online.net` via Square (Best Direct Path)

- **Who it suits, fees, and payout timing:** Selling Founder Packs directly on `dream-online.net` through the Florida LLC's existing Square checkout suits a self-hosted WebGPU browser MMORPG that wants zero third-party platform lock-in (`docs/standards/ALIENWARE-SOUL.md`). There is `0%` crowdfunding platform fee, only Square's standard online card processing fee of `2.9% + $0.30` per transaction, and funds transfer to the LLC bank account on the **next business day** ([Square Payments Fees](https://squareup.com/us/en/payments/our-fees)).
- **Terms on games, AI-made art, and AI-written code:** Square's Seller Agreement allows digital software, browser games, and AI-built creative products provided they deliver clear product value and do not involve illegal gambling or deceptive billing ([Square General Terms](https://squareup.com/us/en/legal/general/ua)).
- **In-game cosmetic rewards and Florida LLC fit:** Because `dream-online.net` controls the game database and the Florida LLC (`Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC`) already operates Square, a purchase on `dream-online.net` can unlock cosmetic skins and account convenience entitlements on the player's account immediately upon webhook confirmation.
