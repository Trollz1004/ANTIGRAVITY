## 1. The Hook: What Shows What Is New About DREAM Online

### Evaluating the Four Canon Candidates

From the in-house game design canon (`docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md`, `docs/gdd/03-life-skills-economy.md`, and `docs/DREAM-MASTER-DIRECTIVE.md`), four signature systems set **DREAM Online** apart from traditional MMORPGs:

- **Live AI NPCs That Remember (100% Free and Unlimited):** Every named NPC is an event-driven agent with persistent memory scoped across `player`, `npc`, `zone`, `global_event`, and `world_event` layers, governed by a strict 3,000-millisecond dialogue budget and deterministic world-native fallbacks so gameplay never waits on cloud inference (`docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md`).
- **The 15-Minute Gossip Tick with Truth Tags (`fact`, `rumor`, `fable`, `dream`):** Every fifteen minutes, the world state tick doubles as an inter-NPC gossip network where every message carries a hidden truth tag (`fact`, `rumor`, `fable`, or `dream`), NPCs show behavioral tells based on that tag, and 22-hour world bosses spawn at locations that exist only inside seeded gossip rather than predictable external timers (`docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md`).
- **The Deterministic No-Fail Forge with FABLE's Armor Truck (`FABLE's Popup Camper / Portable Anvil`):** Weapons and armor upgraded across the five rarity tiers (`Common`, `Uncommon`, `Rare`, `Legend`, and `Mythos`) never break, never downgrade, and never use fail-stacks, while `FABLE` operates both the town blacksmith anvil for in-game `Sols` repairs and a deployable portable anvil for convenient field repairs and stash access in a world with zero fast travel (`docs/gdd/03-life-skills-economy.md` and `docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md`).
- **Life Skills as Deep as Combat:** Ten interconnected professions (Gathering, Processing, Cooking, Alchemy, Farming, Fishing, Hunting, Crafting, Workers, and Trading) plus dual-era ocean shipbuilding stand alongside directional action combat as full progression paths (`docs/gdd/03-life-skills-economy.md`).

### The Picked Hook for the Playable Slice: Live AI NPCs That Remember, Fed by the 15-Minute Gossip Tick

For a five-minute playable slice on `dream-online.net` and a 90-second crowdfunding trailer, **Live AI NPCs That Remember**—directly voiced through **the 15-minute gossip tick with truth tags**—is the single strongest lead hook, while **the deterministic no-fail forge with FABLE's portable anvil** and **one gathering node** serve as the immediate tactile progression payoff inside the same five-minute loop.

As `docs/DREAM-MASTER-DIRECTIVE.md` (Section 0 and Section 55) defines the project, the goal is not to advertise a chatbot, but to build an action MMORPG where a player repeatedly experiences *"Wait… did that NPC actually notice what I just did?"* followed minutes later by *"Wait… it remembers me."* In five minutes inside a browser tab, deep multi-hour life-skill chains (such as turning raw timber into treated planks and tool handles) cannot fully play out on screen, whereas an NPC witnessing the player's exact perfect dodge in the golden-hour Day Dream and calling it back by name after the world transforms into the cyberpunk Nightfall metropolis delivers an unforgettable proof in under three hundred seconds.

### Why This Hook Beats Current Competitors

- **It defeats static script-tree MMORPGs:** In legacy theme-park and sandbox MMORPGs, town blacksmiths and quest guides repeat identical pre-written paragraphs to every player regardless of whether that player just solo-dodged a boss beam, broke their blade, or went outlaw. In **DREAM Online**, `Mireth` (the Moon Gate guide) and `FABLE` (`fable-smith`, the Haven blacksmith) react to structured gameplay telemetry (`player.perfect_dodge`, `player.died_repeatedly`, `player.crafted_item`), remember the player's gear durability history and outlaw record across sessions, and pass along truth-tagged world gossip (`fact`, `rumor`, `fable`, `dream`) that makes static third-party wiki walkthroughs obsolete (`docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md`).
- **It defeats bolted-on AI tech demos:** Standalone AI NPC demos typically paste an unguarded cloud LLM onto a bare scene where characters hallucinate lore, break the fourth wall, freeze the frame while waiting on an API call, or lock conversation behind a paid monthly token subscription. **DREAM Online** enforces the Four NPC Laws (`docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md`): NPCs emit only schema-validated proposals (`suggest_hint`, `suggest_marker`, `suggest_event_pause`, `suggest_quest_note`) that the authoritative server validates, hold the fourth wall in world-native language, answer within a hard 3,000-millisecond ceiling before triggering an instant deterministic fallback line, and remain 100% free and unlimited for every player (`docs/DREAM-MASTER-DIRECTIVE.md`).
- **It pairs living memory with anti-frustration progression:** When the NPC's memory callback guides the player to `FABLE`'s anvil and portable Armor Truck to repair and upgrade their weapon without any chance of item destruction or downgrade, backers see both a living world and a fair progression system that rejects the predatory enhancement-failure loops common in commercial free-to-play MMORPGs (`docs/gdd/03-life-skills-economy.md`).

## 2. The Five-Minute Playable Slice on `dream-online.net`

### Engine Rule: 100% In-House TypeScript Engine (`src/engine/`)

The playable slice runs directly in the browser on `dream-online.net` using **DREAM Engine**, our 100% in-house TypeScript 3D engine built on native WebGPU ([MDN WebGPU API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGPU_API)) with an automatic WebGL2 fallback ([MDN WebGL2RenderingContext](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGL2RenderingContext)) in `src/engine/renderer/`. Per `docs/tech/engine-decision-2026-09-30-own-engine.md` and `docs/standards/AGENT-STANDARD.md`, the runtime uses zero Unreal Engine, zero Unity, zero Godot runtime, and zero third-party game engine libraries, ever.

### What a Backer Plays in Five Minutes on `dream-online.net`

1. **Minute 0:00 to 0:45 — Instant Spawn in Day Dream and the First Seven Seconds (`day_dream_3d`):** The backer clicks play on `dream-online.net` with zero download and zero paywall. Within the first seven seconds (`specs/005-tutorial-slice/spec.md`), the over-the-shoulder camera frames the Dreamwalker at the Moon Gate Sanctuary camp under warm golden-hour sunlight, with cast shadows across weathered stone ruins, `Sup@` (HUD alias `SupO`) hovering at the right shoulder, and the `GeminEYE` looting pet hovering at the left shoulder. Every HUD element uses the calm, dark, see-through panel style of `docs/gdd/09-interface-style.md` in plain white-on-dark typography, stamped in the corner with the live git commit hash (`SB-12`). Pressing `L` opens the three-column see-through Combo List screen (`src/ui/ComboListModal.tsx`), showing every working key combination and its defensive tag (`invulnerability frames`, `guard`, or `none`).
2. **Minute 0:45 to 2:15 — Directional Action Combat and I-Frame Dodge Proof:** The backer sprints (`WASD` plus `Shift` or double-tap auto-sprint) along the rutted camp road to the **Hollow Sentinel** construct. When the Sentinel winds up its 1.4-second telegraphed amber eye beam, the player performs a directional dodge (`Shift` plus direction) through the beam using invulnerability frames (`i-frames`). The engine triggers an 80-millisecond hit-stop, spawns floating damage numbers on counter-attack, and emits a validated `player.perfect_dodge` event envelope (`src/engine/contracts/world-event.ts`) witnessed by `Mireth`. The player finishes the Sentinel using the six working starter moves: the three-step light attack chain (`LMB`), the heavy posture-breaking strike (`RMB`), the directional guard stance (`Q`), `Dream Lunge` (`W + F`, covering 6 meters in 0.25 seconds), and `Nightveil Burst` (`R`, a 4-meter radial shockwave).
3. **Minute 2:15 to 3:15 — Life-Skill Gathering, Deterministic No-Fail Forge, and FABLE's Armor Truck:** After `GeminEYE` auto-loots a `Forge Catalyst` and raw ore from the defeated Sentinel, the player harvests one roadside resource bloom node (`E` interact, moving the node from available to cooldown in local storage) and walks past the quiet, unexplained figure of `Ember` (`specs/005-tutorial-slice/spec.md` FR-014) to `FABLE`'s blacksmith station and parked portable Armor Truck (`FABLE's Popup Camper / Portable Anvil`). Here the player spends earned in-game `Sols` to repair weapon durability and clicks once to execute a 100% deterministic, zero-break forge upgrade (`src/engine/combat/mold-breaker-classes-forge-pvp.ts`), igniting the weapon's progressive 3D blue-flame aura (`Rare` to `Legend` visual preview) while inspecting how the portable anvil saves walking distance in a world with no fast travel.
4. **Minute 3:15 to 4:15 — Nightfall World Shift, Live NPC Recall, and Truth-Tagged Gossip:** A local gate-flicker world event transitions the zone into **Nightfall** (`nightmare_3d`). With zero loading screen, the exact same terrain coordinates morph into a rain-slicked cyberpunk city street with wet-asphalt reflections, violet rim lighting, and lit tower grids (`src/engine/renderer/render-profile.ts`). When the player speaks to `Mireth` and `FABLE`, they recall the player's daytime perfect dodge count and newly forged weapon from the Live NPC Lab memory store (`:9127`, or local browser fallback if offline) and share a 15-minute gossip tick entry tagged as a `[FACT]` or `[RUMOR]` warning that the **CrossEyed Duo** (`OpenAEye` and `GeminEYE`) have synchronized in the nearby Arena of Lenses.
5. **Minute 4:15 to 5:00 — CrossEyed Duo Boss Wave and Checkpoint End Card:** The player steps into the floodlit Nightfall arena ring for a 45-second repeatable boss wave against `OpenAEye` (precision prediction beam and line-of-sight mark) and `GeminEYE` (which splits into a mirrored clone at 50% HP), experiencing coordinated boss telegraphs before reaching the camp checkpoint, which saves their inventory, stamina, node cooldown, and NPC memory locally and displays a calm see-through summary card linking to the optional Founder Packs on `dream-online.net`.

## 3. Living World with a Small Team: Real, Checkable Tools and APIs

A small team cannot hand-script thousands of branching quests without running out of time, and cannot run one dedicated cloud LLM process per NPC per frame without going broke. **DREAM Online** makes the world feel alive by combining **event-driven proximity routing**, **tiered inference**, and **the 15-minute gossip tick** using only real, verifiable tools and APIs already present in our stack:

- **Browser Client Rendering and Audio (Zero Server GPU Cost):** The client renders 3D scenes locally on the player's device through the standard browser **WebGPU API** ([MDN WebGPU API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGPU_API)) and **WebGL2 API** ([MDN WebGL2RenderingContext](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGL2RenderingContext)), synthesizes ambient soundscapes through the **Web Audio API** (`src/engine/audio/ambient-synth.ts`, [MDN Web Audio API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Audio_API)), and caches local checkpoint and fallback memory in **Web Storage (`localStorage`)** ([MDN Web Storage API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Storage_API)).
- **Event-Driven HTTP Gateways and Strict Timeout Enforcement:** When a salient event occurs (`player.perfect_dodge`, `player.defeated_boss`, `player.crafted_item`), the client dispatches a JSON envelope over the standard **Fetch API** ([MDN Fetch API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Fetch_API)) to the **Node.js HTTP** service (`node:http` in `game/server/live-npc-lab/src/server.js`, [Node.js HTTP Documentation](https://nodejs.org/api/http.html)) and **Express** studio server (`server.ts`, [Express Documentation](https://expressjs.com/)). Every outbound provider call in `game/server/live-npc-lab/src/providers.js` wraps its request in a standard **`AbortController`** ([MDN AbortController](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/AbortController)) with a hard 2,500-to-3,000-millisecond timeout (`DEFAULT_DIALOGUE_TIMEOUT_MS = 3000`). If any model is slow or unreachable, `npcEngine.js` immediately returns one of five world-native deterministic fallback lines so no player ever waits on inference.
- **Tiered NPC Routing Ladder (`T0` through `T3` plus `Sup@`):**
  - **T0 (Ambient Chatter, Guards, and Instant Fallbacks):** Served at `$0` marginal cost by deterministic state tables in `npcEngine.js` and local **Ollama** inference (`adapters/ollama-local/manifest.yaml`, [Ollama API Documentation](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/blob/main/docs/api.md)).
  - **T1 and T2 (Persistent Named NPCs like `Mireth` and `FABLE`, and Boss Encounter Directors for `OpenAEye` and `GeminEYE`):** Orchestrated by **Hermes Agent** (`adapters/hermes/manifest.yaml`, [Nous Research Hermes Agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent)) routing through our self-hosted **OmniRoute** gateway (`docs/DREAM-MASTER-DIRECTIVE.md` Section 31) to replaceable cloud inference endpoints including the **Google Gemini API** via the official `@google/genai` SDK (`package.json`, [Google Gen AI SDK Documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/sdks)), **OpenRouter** ([OpenRouter API Documentation](https://openrouter.ai/docs/quick-start)), or **1min.ai** (`game/server/live-npc-lab/src/providers.js`, [1min.ai API Documentation](https://docs.1min.ai/)). Per `npcEngine.js`, each player is capped at `20` cloud NPC calls per hour (`DEFAULT_MAX_CALLS_PER_PLAYER_PER_HOUR = 20`) and `500` tokens per reply (`DEFAULT_MAX_TOKENS = 500`), preventing runaway cloud spend.
  - **T3 (The 15-Minute Gossip Tick and Epoch Compaction):** Instead of generating unique world lore per player, a scheduled 15-minute batch job reads the append-only `world-events.jsonl` log, summarizes what actually happened in the zone (how many players dodged the Sentinel, who forged a `Legend` blade, who turned Red-Named outlaw), and writes four shared gossip records into the `zone` and `world_event` memory scopes tagged with a hidden truth tag (`fact`, `rumor`, `fable`, or `dream`). When any player talks to `Mireth` or `FABLE`, the NPC pulls that pre-computed gossip slice and exhibits a canon behavioral tell (for example, `FABLE` tapping his anvil iron twice before stating a verified `fact`, or glancing toward the Moon Gate mist when repeating a `rumor` or `dream`). One batch call every 15 minutes updates the shared town memory for every player on the shard.
  - **Sup@ (`SupO`) Companion Sphere:** Per `docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md` (Section 3) and `docs/standards/AGENT-STANDARD.md` (Section 8), `Sup@` is the only entity routed through the real signed-in **Claude Code CLI** (`adapters/claude/manifest.yaml`, [Anthropic Claude Code Documentation](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/agents-and-tools/claude-code/overview)) and never uses an Anthropic API key. In the public browser slice, `Sup@`'s onboarding whispers use pre-verified world-native lines alongside deterministic memory recall so public web traffic never violates CLI sign-in terms (`docs/DREAM-MASTER-DIRECTIVE.md` Section 29).
- **Durable Memory Storage and Epoch Decay:** Prototype slice memories are stored in append-only JSON Lines (`npc-memory.jsonl` and `npc-memory-summaries.jsonl` via `node:fs/promises`, [Node.js File System Documentation](https://nodejs.org/api/fs.html)) with production durability backed by **PostgreSQL** ([PostgreSQL Documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/)) and **pgvector** ([pgvector Repository](https://github.com/pgvector/pgvector)). Memory is keyed per NPC with player slices (`canon-lore-inhouse.md` Section 8), and on every weekly Nightmare Shift and four-week epoch swap, old memories drop one importance tier (`compactMemory` in `npcEngine.js`) while nothing is deleted.

## 6. Pitfalls: Common MMORPG Crowdfunding Failures and How This Plan Avoids Each One

### Pitfall 1: Overpromising Scope (The "100-Biome Concept Art" Trap)

- **Why MMORPG campaigns fail here:** Crowdfunded MMORPGs such as *Chronicles of Elyria* raised millions on sprawling promises of dozens of procedural kingdoms, aging dynasties, and massive continents before a single playable loop existed, burning through backer funds and shutting down without a working game ([IGN Report on Chronicles of Elyria Closure](https://www.ign.com/articles/chronicles-of-elyria-crowdfunded-mmo-shuts-down-after-raising-8-million)).
- **How this plan avoids it:** We enforce Section 43 of `docs/DREAM-MASTER-DIRECTIVE.md` (*"Do not build the whole MMO. Build one complete proof"*) and refuse to offer scope-inflating stretch goals. Instead of promising ten empty continents, our **One World, Dreamed Twice** architecture (`docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md` Section 2) doubles the gameplay depth of a single geographical footprint by transforming the exact same world coordinates from Golden-Hour Ruins (`day_dream_3d`) into the Cyberpunk Neon Metropolis (`nightmare_3d`) through shader and lighting profiles (`src/engine/renderer/render-profile.ts`), while the four-week epoch shift refreshes world state without requiring dozens of hand-built zones.

### Pitfall 2: Missing Delivery (The "Third-Party Engine Rewrite and Physical Merchandise" Trap)

- **Why MMORPG campaigns fail here:** Indie MMOs routinely miss delivery dates by years when third-party engine licensing changes force mid-project rewrites or when manufacturing and shipping physical reward boxes consume the engineering budget ([Polygon Report on Crowdfunded MMO Delays](https://www.polygon.com/2020/3/27/21197362/chronicles-of-elyria-soulbound-studios-layoffs-shut-down-kickstarter-crowdfunding)).
- **How this plan avoids it:** First, the five-minute playable slice is already live in the browser on `dream-online.net` *before* the campaign asks for a single backer dollar, running on our own zero-dependency TypeScript WebGPU and WebGL2 engine (`src/engine/`, `docs/tech/engine-decision-2026-09-30-own-engine.md`) where no external vendor can revoke a license or break a C++ build. Second, 100% of our Founder Pack rewards are digital in-game cosmetics and account convenience unlocks delivered automatically via the Square checkout webhook upon purchase, with zero physical merchandise, zero shipping logistics, and zero fulfillment delay.

### Pitfall 3: Runaway Server and AI Inference Cost

- **Why MMORPG campaigns fail here:** Browser and indie online games fail financially when server hosting or uncapped per-frame AI API bills scale faster than player revenue ([Game Developer Crowdfunding Postmortems](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/crowdfunding-postmortems-what-goes-wrong)).
- **How this plan avoids it:** All 3D rendering, shader math, and particle simulation run on the player's own GPU via browser WebGPU and WebGL2 (`src/engine/renderer/`), leaving the Node.js server to validate compact JSON state envelopes. On the AI side (`docs/tech/live-ai-runtime-architecture.md` and `game/server/live-npc-lab/src/npcEngine.js`), NPCs are triggered only by proximity and salient events; ambient `T0` lines run free on local rules and local Ollama; cloud calls are hard-capped at `20 calls per player per hour` (`DEFAULT_MAX_CALLS_PER_PLAYER_PER_HOUR = 20`) and `500 tokens` per reply; the 15-minute gossip tick runs as a single batch job per zone rather than per player; and any rate-limited or slow call (`> 3,000 ms`) drops cleanly to zero-cost deterministic fallback lines.

### Pitfall 4: Cheating, Exploits, and Economy Duplication

- **Why MMORPG campaigns fail here:** Online RPGs that trust client-side damage, movement, or currency math suffer immediate item duplication, speed hacking, and gold inflation that ruin the player marketplace in the first week ([OWASP Web Security Testing Guide](https://owasp.org/www-project-web-security-testing-guide/)).
- **How this plan avoids it:** First, runtime AI agents have **zero authority** to mint or transfer `Sols` or `NEEDs`, grant gear, settle marketplace orders, or alter payment state (`docs/DREAM-MASTER-DIRECTIVE.md` Section 21 and `docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md` Section 7: *"Proposals Only"*). Second, combat resolution, pooled party damage loot (`docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md` Section 4), and deterministic forge upgrades are validated server-side while observer models scan telemetry for wash trading, impossible inventory deltas, and duplication (`docs/DREAM-MASTER-DIRECTIVE.md` Section 23). Third, when deterministic rules confirm an exploit, **`THE BAN HAMMER`** turns enforcement into a public in-world comic spectacle (`docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md` Section 3), and **`C0D3X`** (`c0d3x-rider` astride `MOLLMA`) stitches the zone or ledger back to the last verified all-green checkpoint (`docs/tech/c0d3x-world-recovery.md`).

### Pitfall 5: A Dead World at Launch (The "Empty Shard" Death Spiral)

- **Why MMORPG campaigns fail here:** When a new MMORPG splits players across dozens of instanced channels, scatters a modest launch population across massive maps with instant fast-travel teleporters, or depends entirely on peak concurrent player counts for town activity, players logging in at off-peak hours find an empty world and churn.
- **How this plan avoids it:** First, the **One-World Law** (`docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md` Section 1) places every player inside a single shared continuous world with **zero dungeon instances, zero load-screen sharding, and zero fast travel**, concentrating all player foot traffic, `Storage Runner` and `Market Runner` couriers, and Oathcall hunts along the physical roads between the Moon Gate, Haven, Lake Lumen, and the Arena of Lenses. Second, because `Mireth`, `FABLE`, `Camp Guard`, `Field Gatherer`, and `Sunny Ledger` retain persistent cross-player zone memories fed by the 15-minute gossip tick, a solo player logging in at an off-peak hour still steps into a town that remembers who fought the night boss two hours earlier, who forged a `Legend` weapon at dusk, and which outlaw is wanted on the Low Road.
