# Public Surface Policy

Customer-facing product surfaces must stay focused on business and product value.

Do not include charity, mission, donation, impact-allocation, personal long-term goals, family goals, DAO treasury goals, or similar language in public UI, marketing, onboarding, emails, customer docs, or public README copy.

Private backend/admin notes may track internal allocation logic when needed, but that language must not leak into public marketing or customer-facing product surfaces.
