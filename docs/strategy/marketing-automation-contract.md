# Marketing Automation Contract

Status date: 2026-06-22

All marketing automation must follow the business-only public lane.

## Content Rules

Allowed themes:

- membership
- verification
- safety
- support
- uptime
- checkout
- account access
- receipts and refunds

Disallowed themes:

- private owner decisions
- future community structures
- payment-routing claims
- promises beyond the product being sold

## Approval Workflow

1. Generate content from product-first templates.
2. Scan for business-only compliance.
3. Queue for human approval before posting.
4. Publish only after approval.
5. Track performance by channel and offer.

## Ownership

CMO owns messaging, approval queue, and content performance.
CTO owns automation, security, platform integrations, and logs.
