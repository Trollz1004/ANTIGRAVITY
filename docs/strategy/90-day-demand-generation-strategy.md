# 90-Day Demand Generation Strategy

Status date: 2026-06-22

This strategy is reset to the business-only public lane.

## Positioning

YouAndINotAI sells a product:

- membership
- verification
- safety
- support
- uptime
- checkout
- account access
- receipts and refunds

## Channels

- organic social
- search content
- local community events
- referral offers
- email follow-up
- support-led retention

## Messaging

Lead with product utility and trust. Do not use private owner decisions,
future community structures, or payment-routing claims as public sales copy.

## KPIs

- signup conversion
- paid conversion
- checkout completion
- support response time
- account verification completion
- retention
- refund rate
