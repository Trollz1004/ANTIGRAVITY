# 90-Day Demand Generation and Monetization Strategy

## YouAndINotAI - Social Platform for Good

### Executive Summary

This 90-day strategy focuses on cross-channel growth to increase qualified signups and paid conversions while maintaining mission trust. Building on the recently completed date app monetization groundwork (TRO-27), we will establish a sustainable demand generation engine that supports our "Until No Kid In Need" mission through contractual revenue disbursement.

---

## 1. Channel Mix and Acquisition Cadence

### Primary Channels

1. **Organic Social Media** (Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok)
   - Daily posting schedule with rotating content pillars
   - 24/7 automated engagement using content agents (pending TRO-61 approval)
   - Community building through real-world meetup promotion

2. **Community Partnerships**
   - Local dating/relationship meetup groups
   - Volunteer organizations aligned with charity mission
   - Tech/innovation communities interested in ethical AI

3. **Content Marketing**
   - Educational blog posts (dating safety, relationship building, AI ethics)
   - Weekly newsletter featuring user stories and mission impacts
   - SEO-optimized content targeting "ethical dating app" keywords

### Weekly Cadence Framework

#### Week 1-4 (Foundation Phase)

- Monday: Educational content (dating tips, safety guides)
- Wednesday: Story content (user testimonials, mission impact)
- Friday: Offer content (subscription benefits, promotions)
- Sunday: Community content (meetups, charity updates)

#### Week 5-12 (Growth Phase)

- Increased posting frequency (2-3x per day)
- Cross-channel promotion campaigns
- Partnership announcements and collaborative content

#### Week 13-12 (Optimization Phase)

- Data-driven content optimization
- A/B testing of messaging approaches
- Expansion to new platforms and audiences

## 2. Campaign Structure and Messaging

### Core Message Framework

**Primary Value Proposition**: "Real humans, AI-assisted safety checks, charity-funded platform."

### Content Pillars

1. **Safety First** - Bot-Shield verification and account-bound checkout features
2. **Real Connection** - Genuine dating and community building focus
3. **Mission Driven** - Contractual revenue disbursement supporting kids in need
4. **Tech Innovation** - Ethical AI implementation in dating

### Subscription Conversion Funnel

#### Awareness Stage

- Content highlighting platform differences from traditional dating apps
- Educational pieces about AI ethics in dating
- Mission-focused storytelling

#### Consideration Stage

- Free trial or freemium model promotion
- Feature comparison content (safety, verification, community)
- User success stories and testimonials

#### Decision Stage

- Subscription benefit breakdown
- Limited-time promotional offers
- Social proof through community growth metrics

### Retention Messaging

- Weekly "Impact Reports" showing platform contributions to kids in need
- Exclusive community features for subscribers
- Early access to new features and meetup invitations

## 3. KPI Framework

### Acquisition Metrics

- **Signup Rate**: Target 15% conversion from landing page visits
- **CAC Proxy**: Cost per qualified lead through content engagement
- **Channel Performance**: Track leads by channel source

### Monetization Metrics

- **Paid Conversion Rate**: Target 3% conversion from free to paid users
- **30-Day Retention**: Target 70% retention of new paid subscribers
- **Lifetime Value**: Average subscriber tenure and revenue contribution

### Engagement Metrics

- **Monthly Active Users**: Growth in engaged platform users
- **Community Participation**: Meetup attendance and volunteer engagement
- **Content Performance**: Engagement rates across social channels

### Mission Impact Metrics

- **Contractual Revenue Disbursement**: Monthly amounts allocated to kids in need
- **Community Hours**: Volunteer hours contributed by platform community
- **Success Stories**: Documented positive outcomes from user connections

### Legal-Safe Phrasing Requirements

✅ Use: "contractual revenue disbursement", "revenue-sharing for good", "platform that gives back"
