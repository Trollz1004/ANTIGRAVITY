## YouAndINotAI - Continuous Growth Operations Framework

### Purpose

This policy establishes the operating framework for 24/7 social growth operations that support the YouAndINotAI mission of connecting people who give a damn. The policy balances aggressive growth objectives with brand safety, compliance requirements, and platform sustainability.

---

## 1. Platform-Safe Cadence Rules

### Daily Activity Limits (Per Account Per Day)

| Activity             | Maximum Frequency | Notes                            |
| -------------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Follows              | 30                | Spread evenly throughout day     |
| Likes                | 150               | 2-3 per hour maximum             |
| Comments             | 20                | Only on engagement opportunities |
| Thread Joins         | 10                | Relevant communities only        |
| Page/Subreddit Joins | 5                 | Niche interest communities       |

### Hourly Distribution Guidelines

- Distribute activities across all 24 hours
- Peak activity times: 7-9 AM, 12-1 PM, 7-9 PM (local time rotation)
- Minimal activity during 2-5 AM to appear human-like
- Weekend vs weekday variation to match typical user patterns

### Platform-Specific Approaches

**Twitter/X**

- Focus on trending topics within dating/relationship niches
- Engage with accounts discussing ethical dating, real relationships
- Use "like 5 follow 5 follow back 5" approach systematically

**Instagram**

- Follow users posting about real connections, not superficial content
- Like authentic relationship/dating posts with genuine comments
- Join relevant hashtags: #RealConnection, #EthicalDating, #CommunityDating

**Facebook**

- Join local dating/relationship meetup groups
- Participate in parenting, volunteering, and community groups
- Focus on meaningful engagement over quantity

**LinkedIn**

- Engage with professionals discussing dating apps, relationship psychology
- Connect with relationship coaches, therapists, community organizers
- Share thought leadership on ethical tech in dating

**TikTok**

- Follow creators making authentic relationship content
- Like videos featuring real couples, dating advice, relationship growth
- Avoid engagement with predatory or superficial dating content

### Anti-Detection Measures

- Rotate engagement patterns regularly
- Vary timing within 30-minute windows
- Mix engagement types (follow, like, comment) per session
- Maintain natural pauses between rapid actions
- Never exceed platform daily limits

## 2. Human Approval Queue System

### Pre-Publication Approval Requirements

All automated outbound content must pass through a human approval queue before publishing. This includes:

- Scheduled social media posts
- Auto-generated content variants
- Promotional messaging
- Community engagement responses

### Approval Workflow

1. **Content Generation**: Automatically created by content agents
2. **Queue Placement**: Content automatically placed in approval queue markdown file
3. **Human Review**: Joshua Coleman (joshlcoleman@gmail.com) reviews within 4 hours (business hours) or 8 hours (off-hours)
4. **Approval Decision**: Approved, Request Changes, or Reject
5. **Publication**: Approved content scheduled for publication
6. **Documentation**: All decisions logged with timestamp and reviewer

### Emergency Bypass Protocol

For time-sensitive content (press releases, event announcements):

1. CMO can approve content directly during business hours
2. Pre-approved templates available for standard situations
3. 24/7 urgent contact protocol for critical issues

### Approval Queue File Structure

## Approved Content

## 3. Content Moderation and Brand Boundaries

### Prohibited Content Categories

1. **Dating Predatory Content**
   - No engagement with predatory dating profiles or content
   - Block and report suspicious accounts immediately
   - Never promote or engage with scam dating sites

2. **Inappropriate Topics**
   - Explicit sexual content
   - Hate speech or discriminatory content
   - Violence or harmful behavior promotion
   - Illegal activity promotion

3. **Brand-Inconsistent Messaging**
   - No negativity toward competitors
   - No misleading claims about platform capabilities
   - No violation of "contractual revenue payout" messaging doctrine

### Required Content Standards

1. **Voice and Tone**
   - Warm, authentic, community-focused
   - Real person perspective, not corporate speak
   - Avoid buzzwords: "disruptive," "innovative," "groundbreaking"

2. **Compliance Requirements**
   - Always use legal-safe phrasing:
     ✅ "contractual revenue payout"
     ✅ "revenue-sharing for good"
     ✅ "platform that gives back"
     ❌ "payment," "payment," "outreach"
   - Reference mission as platform feature, not  appeal
   - Emphasize human connection over monetary benefits

3. **Community-Focused Approach**
   - Highlight real user stories and connections
   - Promote offline meetups and volunteer opportunities
   - Showcase  impact without direct asking
   - Encourage authentic relationship development

### Escalation Protocol for Questionable Content

1. **Suspicious Account Detection**: Flag and isolate for review
2. **Content Concerns**: Pause and escalate to CMO
3. **Brand Violations**: Immediate correction and policy reinforcement
4. **Legal Issues**: Contact CEO immediately with documentation

## 6. Exception Handling and Crisis Protocols

### Account Suspension Response

Immediate actions:

1. Document suspension details
2. Analyze contributing factors
3. Temporarily pause similar activities
4. Contact platform support for reinstatement
5. Post-incident analysis and policy update

### Negative Publicity Management

Response protocol:

1. Immediate acknowledgment of concern
2. Private communication with affected parties
3. Public statement focusing on corrective actions
4. Policy review and strengthening measures
5. Transparent communication about improvements

### Policy Violation Consequences

Internal consequences for policy violations:

- First offense: Warning and mandatory training
- Second offense: Temporary suspension of privileges
- Third offense: Permanent removal from growth operations
