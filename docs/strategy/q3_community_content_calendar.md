# Q3 Community Events & Connections Content Calendar

## Core Themes

- **Human Connection First**: Highlighting face-to-face interactions and meaningful relationships
- **Local Community Spotlight**: Showcasing neighborhood gatherings and local initiatives
- **Shared Experiences**: Focusing on activities that bring people together around common interests
- **Authentic Moments**: Capturing real interactions over polished presentations

## Content Types

### Weekly Features

- **Community Hero Monday**: Stories of individuals fostering real connections
- **Event Highlight Wednesday**: Showcase upcoming local gatherings
- **Member Moment Friday**: Feature photos/stories from recent meetups
- **Behind-the-Scenes Saturday**: Organizers preparing for events

### Monthly Deep Dives

- **Neighborhood Spotlight**: In-depth features on specific communities
- **Organizer Interview**: Conversations with community leaders
- **Impact Stories**: Measurable outcomes from community initiatives
- **Event Recaps**: Photo essays and testimonials from major gatherings

## Distribution Channels

### Primary Platforms

- **Instagram**: Visual storytelling with event photos and videos
- **Facebook**: Event listings and community group engagement
- **Newsletter**: Personal stories and curated event calendars
- **Website Blog**: Long-form articles about community impact

### Secondary Platforms

- **Twitter**: Real-time event updates and community conversations
- **LinkedIn**: Professional networking events and B2B community building
- **TikTok**: Short-form authentic interaction videos

## Key Messaging Points

### Core Messages

- "Where digital meets physical - real connections happen in person"
- "Your neighbors have amazing stories to share"
- "One conversation can change your entire perspective"
- "Community starts with showing up"

### Supporting Messages

- Small gatherings create big impacts
- Everyone has something valuable to contribute
- Regular attendance builds lasting relationships
- Local events strengthen global communities

## Content Calendar Overview

### July: Summer Social Season

**Focus**: Outdoor gatherings and summer festivals

- Theme weeks: "Summer Block Parties", "Outdoor Adventures"
- Content mix: 60% event promotion, 30% community stories, 10% behind-the-scenes
- Key messaging: "Make the most of summer - connect outdoors"

### August: Back to School Community Building

**Focus**: Family-friendly events and educational gatherings

- Theme weeks: "Family Connections", "Learning Together"
- Content mix: 50% event promotion, 40% parent testimonials, 10% resource sharing
- Key messaging: "Learning is better with others"

### September: Autumn Engagement

**Focus**: Seasonal celebrations and volunteer opportunities

- Theme weeks: "Harvest Celebrations", "Giving Back"
- Content mix: 55% event promotion, 25% volunteer stories, 20% seasonal content
- Key messaging: "Stronger communities through service"

## Call-to-Action Framework

### Connection-Oriented CTAs

- Register for the next local meetup
- Invite a neighbor to this weekend's event
- Share your own community story
- Nominate someone who builds community well
- Start your own local gathering

### Engagement CTAs

- Comment with your favorite community memory
- Tag friends you'd bring to this event
- Share what kind of gathering you'd like to see
- Tell us about your neighborhood traditions

## Measurement & Success Metrics

### Engagement Indicators

- Event attendance rates
- Story submission volume
- Cross-platform sharing frequency
- Direct messaging about local connections

### Community Impact Metrics

- New member registrations after events
- Repeat attendee percentages
- Community partnership formations
- Local business collaborations initiated
