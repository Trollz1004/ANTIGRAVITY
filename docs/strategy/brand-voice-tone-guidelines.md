# YouAndINotAI Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines

**Owner:** CMO
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-17
**Doctrine:** Product-first, business-only. All copy must pass the canonical guard.

## Brand Personality

YouAndINotAI speaks like a skilled tradesman who built something real — not a marketing agency running a campaign.

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Direct** | No fluff. No filler. Say what you mean in as few words as possible. |
| **Confident** | You know the product works. State facts without hedging. |
| **Specific** | "Bot-Shield verification" not "identity verification." "$1" not "low cost." "90-second demo" not "quick video." |
| **Honest** | Acknowledge the platform is early. Never inflate numbers or make guarantees you can't keep. |
| **Human** | Sound like a real person. One person wrote this. Not a legal team. Not a focus group. |

## Voice Principles

### 1. The Buyer's Problem Comes First

Every piece of copy starts with what the reader is experiencing, not what you built.

**Do:** "Tired of matching with bots? Every profile on YouAndINotAI passes a human check."
**Don't:** "YouAndINotAI is a verified platform. We use Bot-Shield to stop bots."

### 2. Name the Product + a Specific Detail

Never call it "the platform" or "the app." Say "Bot-Shield verification" with a concrete outcome.

**Do:** "Bot-Shield verification — $1, one-time, and every profile in your discovery lane is human-verified."
**Don't:** "Our verification system ensures quality matches."

### 3. Joshua's Edge in One Sentence

Licensed electrician + AI builder = ships things that work in the real world, not just in slides. Use this exact framing in proposals. Reference it sparingly in broader content.

### 4. Hard CTA With a Specific Time

Every conversion piece needs an exact ask with a time bound.

**Do:** "Reply yes and I'll send the deliverable within 48 hours."
**Don't:** "Let me know if you're interested."

### 5. No Banned Phrases

Never use: leverage, synergy, cutting-edge, I hope this finds you well, donate, donation, solicitation, charity, non-profit, giving back, impact (unless product-related).

## Tone by Channel

| Channel | Tone | Length | Format |
|---------|------|--------|--------|
| Landing page | Direct, benefit-forward | ~150 words per section | HTML, structured pricing grid |
| Cold email | Tradesman quoting a job | 80 words max | Skeleton: problem → demo → price → time |
| Proposal | Problem → solution → price | 150 words max | Structured with wrap-up CTA |
| Follow-up | Respectful nudge, new value | 40 words max | One new data point + yes/no |
| Social post | Conversational, punchy | Platform limits | Problem observation → CTA |
| Blog/SEO | Authoritative, researched | 800-1500 words | Problem → solution with specifics |
| Support | Helpful, warm, fast | As needed | Acknowledge → fix → confirm |
| Email sequence | Personal, direct | 100-200 words per email | Confirm → educate → convert |

## Copy Patterns

### Headline Pattern
[Problem statement] + [Product name delivers solution]

**Examples:**
- "Tired of bots? YouAndINotAI verifies every profile."
- "Swipe fatigue is by design. Prompt-first matching is the fix."
- "60% of dating app users suspect they've matched with bots. We built the alternative."

### CTA Pattern
[Verb] + [specific outcome] + [price/timebound]

**Examples:**
- "Get verified for $1. Done in 90 seconds."
- "Lock Founding Member pricing at $14.99/mo — never increases."
- "Reply yes and I'll ship the deliverable within 48 hours."

### Pricing Copy Pattern
$[price] + [what they get] + [timeframe] + [guarantee if applicable]

**Examples:**
- "$1. One-time. Bot-Shield verification. 90-second process."
- "$14.99/mo. Founding Member. Locked forever. Cancel anytime. Badge stays."

### Safety Feature Copy Pattern
[Feature name] + [where to find it] + [what it does]

**Examples:**
- "Block. Tap the profile, tap block. They can't see you, message you, or find you."
- "Report. Every profile and message has a report option. Reviewed within 24 hours."

## Canonical Guard (Mandatory Self-Check)

Before publishing ANY customer-facing copy:

- [ ] Does this use "membership" or "verification" or "safety" or "support" or "uptime" or "matching quality" language?
- [ ] Does this NOT use "donation," "charity," "solicitation," "non-profit," "giving back"? (Florida Statute §496.405)
- [ ] Is the first sentence about the reader's problem, not the product?
- [ ] Is the CTA specific with a time or price?
- [ ] Are feature claims verifiable with CTO (no over-promising)?
- [ ] Would this sound like it came from a real person, not a marketing committee?

## Do Say vs. Don't Say

| Context | Do Say | Don't Say |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| Verification | "Every profile is human-verified" | "We guarantee 100% safety" |
| Safety features | "Block, report, and freeze from every screen" | "We keep you completely safe" |
| Matching | "Prompt-first matching starts better conversations" | "Our algorithm finds your soulmate" |
| Membership | "Founding Member pricing locked forever" | "Invest in the platform" |
| Support | "In-app help center reaches a human within 24h" | "24/7 live support" |
| Platform status | "Independent team. Build-in-public." | "We're a movement to change dating" |
| Pricing | "$1 verification. $14.99/mo Founding Member." | "Lowest price available" |
| Early stage | "We're growing our first 100 Founding Members" | "We have thousands of active users" |

## Examples by Format

### Landing Page Hero
**Correct:** "Verified members, real profiles, real-world events. The connection is the meetup, not the swipe."
**Why it works:** Buyer's problem implied (swipe fatigue), specific features (verified, real), direct CTA framing.

### Cold Email
**Correct:** "Subject: Matching with bots? \n\n Alex, \n\n Your review site covers 40 dating apps but none verify every profile. \n\n I built Bot-Shield — $1 human verification that stops bots before discovery. 90-second demo: youandinotai.com. \n\n $1. Done in 90 seconds. Free if it doesn't work. \n\n Worth 5 minutes Tuesday at 2pm? \n\n Joshua"
**Why it works:** First sentence about THEM, specific demo, hard CTA with time, under 80 words.

### Social Post
**Correct:** "60% of dating app users suspect they've matched with bots (Norton 2025). YouAndINotAI verifies every profile. No bots in the lane. $1 Bot-Shield. youandinotai.com"
**Why it works:** Stat hook, specific product name, price, CTA.

## Review Process

1. Writer drafts to these guidelines
2. Canonical guard checklist run by writer
3. CEO review before publishing on customer surfaces
4. Compliance incidents (charity/donation language) = immediate retraction + re-review
