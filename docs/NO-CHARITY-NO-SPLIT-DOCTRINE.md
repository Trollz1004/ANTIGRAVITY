# No Charity / No Split Doctrine

Effective immediately, all active ANTIGRAVITY business, product, frontend, backend, automation, agent, and marketing work uses a business-first doctrine.

## Permanent Rules

- No charity positioning.
- No donation, fundraising, charitable-giving, nonprofit, beneficiary, or social-good revenue claims.
- No revenue split doctrine.
- No automatic allocation, disbursement, payout, routing, reserve, or percentage-to-cause language.
- No 60/30/10, 100%-to-anything, CharityRouter, GospelDonation, charitable cap, or split-era language in active code, docs, prompts, agents, UI, or marketing surfaces.
- No agent may reintroduce these frames as compliance-safe synonyms.

## Approved Positioning

ANTIGRAVITY and related apps are commercial products and services. Messaging should focus on:

- useful software
- verified real users
- safety and trust
- clean onboarding
- paid subscriptions and checkout clarity
- customer value
- operational reliability
- business growth

## Implementation Guidance

When old language appears, replace it with plain business/product language. Do not preserve old language in active files for historical explanation. Historical archives may be quarantined separately, but active code and active docs must remain clean.
