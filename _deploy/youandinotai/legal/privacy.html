3. How Square handles payment data

Checkout on the Platform is provided by Square (Block, Inc.). When you click a tier button, your browser is sent to Square's checkout pages and Square processes your payment under its own privacy policy and terms.

  • Square acts as our payment processor.
  • We do not see, store, or transmit your full card number, CVV, or bank login.
  • Square may use your payment data to detect fraud, meet anti-money-laundering law, and run its own services under its own terms.
  • Read Square's privacy notice: squareup.com/legal/privacy-no-account.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not run advertising trackers on the Platform.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not share your email with marketing partners.
  • We do not use third-party analytics that follow you across other sites.
  • We do not allow accounts for anyone under 18.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn we have, we delete it.

5. How long we keep data

  • Order and receipt records: 7 years, to meet U.S. tax and accounting rules.
  • Profile and messages on an active account: as long as the account is open.
  • Profile and messages after you close your account: deleted within 30 days, except where a longer period is required by law (for example, an open safety investigation).
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the case closes, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days, then aggregated or deleted.
  • Bot-Shield verification result: for the life of the account, plus 1 year.

6. Who else receives your data

We share the minimum needed to operate the Platform:

  • Square — processes payments and sends us order confirmations.
  • Cloudflare — serves the Platform and protects it from abuse.
  • Hosting and database providers — store the data needed to run your account.
  • Email provider — sends you receipts, activation messages, and replies to your support emails.
  • Tax and legal authorities — when we are legally required to disclose, including valid law-enforcement requests in a safety matter.
  • A buyer or successor — if the Operator sells or transfers the Platform, the receiving party must honor this Policy.

We do not share your data with any other party for any other reason.

7. What other members can see

By design, other adult members of the Platform can see the profile data you choose to add: your display name, your photos, the bio text you write, your city, and the preferences you tag. They can see messages you send them and event sign-ups you make. They cannot see your real legal name (unless you put it in your profile), your email, your billing address, your payment card, or your IP address.

8. Cookies and similar

The Platform sets only essential cookies:

  • A session cookie to keep you signed in.
  • A Cloudflare cookie to keep the Platform working and protected.
  • A small preference cookie if you change a display setting (for example, theme).

When you click a tier button, Square may set cookies on its own checkout domain. Those cookies are governed by Square's privacy notice, not this one.
