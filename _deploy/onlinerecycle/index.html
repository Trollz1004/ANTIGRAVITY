Florida E-Waste Recycling — Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I recycle electronics for free in Florida?

OnlineRecycle.org offers free electronics drop-off and free pickup throughout Central Florida including Sorrento, Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County. Schedule online and we come to you — no cost, ever.

What electronics can I recycle in Central Florida?

We accept computers, laptops, phones, tablets, TVs, printers, gaming consoles, cables, batteries, and most other consumer electronics. If it plugs in or has a battery, we take it.

Does my electronics recycling actually help kids?

Yes — 60% of all net proceeds from OnlineRecycle.org go directly to Shriners Children's Hospitals via our Protocol Omega revenue split, verified on-chain. Until no kid is in need. #ForTheKids

Is there an electronics recycling drop-off near Sorrento, FL?

Yes. OnlineRecycle.org is based in Sorrento, Florida and serves all of Lake County and surrounding Central Florida counties. Drop-off and pickup scheduling available at onlinerecycle.org.
