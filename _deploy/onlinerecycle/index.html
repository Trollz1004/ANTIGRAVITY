Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I recycle electronics for free in Florida?

OnlineRecycle.org offers free electronics drop-off and free pickup throughout Central Florida including Sorrento, Lake County, Orange County, and Seminole County. Schedule online and we come to you — no cost, ever.

What electronics can I recycle in Central Florida?

We accept computers, laptops, phones, tablets, TVs (certain models), printers, gaming consoles, cables, batteries, and most other consumer electronics. If it plugs in or has a battery, we take it.

Does my electronics recycling actually help kids?

Yes. 60% of the net proceeds from OnlineRecycle.org operations are automatically routed directly to Shriners Children's Hospitals. We utilize automated public ledgers to ensure transparent, publicly verifiable donations for every batch of recycling we process. #ForTheKids

Is there an electronics recycling drop-off near Sorrento, FL?

Yes! We are based right here in Sorrento, Florida and serve all surrounding Central Florida communities. We provide the specific local drop-off address to you immediately upon booking to prevent unannounced dumping and ensure security.
