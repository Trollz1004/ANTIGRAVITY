About OnlineRecycle.org

A one-person Florida operation recycling electronics responsibly — with secure data destruction, transparent charitable allocation, and zero cost to you.

Who We Are

Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC

OnlineRecycle.org is operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC, a for-profit limited liability company registered in the State of Florida. We are based in Sorrento, FL (Lake County) and serve the greater Central Florida region including Orange, Seminole, and Lake counties.

We collect unwanted electronics — computers, phones, TVs, printers, servers, and more — through free local pickup and drop-off. Items in resalable condition are listed on our Square storefront and marketplace channels. Items that cannot be resold are responsibly dismantled and recycled. 60% of all net proceeds are contractually allocated to children's medical care via a locked on-chain DAO structure.

Our Process

Ethical & Secure Data Destruction

Every device we receive is treated as if it contains sensitive data — because it might. Our data destruction process follows industry-standard protocols before any device is resold, dismantled, or recycled.

🔍

Intake & Assessment

Every device is logged, photographed, and assessed on arrival. We record make, model, condition, and any visible data storage components.

🗑️

Data Wipe or Destruction

Storage drives are either wiped using multi-pass overwrite protocols or physically destroyed. No drive leaves our possession with readable data intact.

♻️

Responsible Recycling

Non-resalable components are sorted by material type and routed to appropriate recycling streams. We do not landfill electronics.

📋

Transparent Ledger

Revenue from resale is tracked against the on-chain DAO split. Allocation records will be publicly verifiable by wallet address once the routing bridge is complete.

Important: We make reasonable efforts to destroy data using industry-standard protocols, but cannot guarantee complete destruction in all cases. Customers are strongly advised to perform their own factory reset before surrendering any device. See our Disclaimer for full details.
