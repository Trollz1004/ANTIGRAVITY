About OnlineRecycle.org

OnlineRecycle.org is a Central Florida electronics recycling and resale service focused on secure intake, responsible reuse, and practical local pickup and drop-off.

Who We Are

Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC

OnlineRecycle.org is operated by Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC, a Florida limited liability company based in Sorrento. We collect unwanted electronics for reuse, resale, parts recovery, or responsible recycling.

Our service is built for households and small businesses that want a simple way to clear out old devices without dumping them in a landfill or guessing how to handle data-bearing equipment.

Our Process

Secure Handling and Responsible Recycling

Every device is treated like it may contain personal or business data. We assess condition, identify storage media, and decide whether the item should be wiped, dismantled, recycled, or prepared for resale.

🔍

Intake and Assessment

Devices are logged, photographed, and reviewed for resale value, recycling needs, and any obvious data-storage components.

🗑️

Wipe or Destruction

Storage devices are wiped or physically destroyed before resale or recycling. Customers should still factory-reset devices before turning them in whenever possible.

♻️

Reuse First

Usable gear is evaluated for resale or parts recovery. Non-resalable material is sorted into responsible recycling streams.

📋

Service Records

Booking, intake, and resale records are maintained internally so the customer-facing site can stay focused on service details instead of backend operations.

Important: We make reasonable efforts to wipe or destroy data using standard processes, but customers should always back up and factory-reset devices before surrendering them.

Mission Note

Service First, Reporting Separate

OnlineRecycle.org is a for-profit service business. Customer purchases and pickup bookings are service transactions, not tax-deductible gifts to a tax-exempt entity.

A portion of qualifying net proceeds from resale supports children's medical care through the company's broader mission, but detailed mission reporting is maintained separately from this service site so the customer experience stays clear and practical.
