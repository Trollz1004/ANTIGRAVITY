## Testing Framework

### Week 1: Hook Testing
1. Post 2-3 variations daily (total 10-15)
2. Track views, "what's the app?" comments, saves
3. Identify 3x median performer
4. Do NOT change format, ONLY hook text

### Week 2+: Scale Winning Hook
1. Create 30 variations of top performer
2. Add platform-specific hashtags
3. Cross-post to TikTok/Instagram Reels
4. Target: 10K+ views per variation
