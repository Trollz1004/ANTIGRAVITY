## Phase 3: Product Launch Campaign

### Legal Review Hold

- Review-gated mechanics, automatic routing concepts, and investment-style claims are offline until attorney review is complete.
- Campaign copy should mention current products, current membership access, founder story, and verified implementation status only.
- Public links should route to `https://youandinotai.com` unless counsel approves another surface.

### Seven-Day Push

**Day 1 - Preview**
- "Something's coming: a dating app built around accountable verification and honest product updates."

**Day 2 - Founder Story**
- "I was an electrician for 10 years. Now I am building a dating app around accountable verification."

**Day 3 - Problem Statement**
- Discuss dating-app trust problems without promising complete elimination of bots, impersonation, or abuse.

**Day 4 - Trust Message**
- "We are building in public and publishing only claims we can verify."

**Day 5 - Founding Member Push**
- Describe current membership benefits and pricing.

**Day 6 - Product Launch**
- Product launch announcement only. No review-gated roadmap announcement.

**Day 7 - Conversion Push**
- First-week product updates and founding-member availability.

## Phase 4: Public Copy Safety

### Allowed Phrasing

- "Product-first launch."
- "Verification claims publish only after implementation proof."
- "Legal-review items are offline until attorney review is complete."
- "Current product access and membership benefits."

### Disallowed Until Attorney Review

- Review-gated benefits, automatic routing, investment-style claims, contractual public-impact support, or purchase-linked public-impact claims.
- Absolute safety claims unless live implementation proof exists and counsel approves the claim.

### Message Placement

1. Bio/profile sections: current product status only.
2. Posts: avoid absolute verification and safety guarantees.
3. Payment pages: product access only.
4. Email sequences: product onboarding only.
5. Roadmap descriptions: legal-review hold language only.

### Payment Verification

- Square payments route to the business account.
- Payment-proof claims publish only after a live data feed exists.
- Square webhook and backend records must be verified before any public metrics.
