## Target audiences by product

### Claude Droid — Video creators / news publishers

- Pain point: Creating daily YouTube Shorts is time-consuming
- Solution: Automates script, voiceover, rendering, and upload
- Channels: Twitter/X, YouTube creator communities, indie hackers

### Income Droid — Monetization-focused creators

- Pain point: Want to monetize YouTube but dont have the volume
- Solution: 4 revenue-optimized videos/day at peak times
- Channels: r/PartneredYoutube, r/NewTubers, creator forums

### Marketing Content Engine — Small businesses / solopreneurs

- Pain point: Toobusy running the business to post on social media
- Solution: Auto-generates and posts 4x/day on autopilot
- Channels: Twitter/X, r/SmallBusiness, r/Entrepreneur

### Jules AI Assistant — DevOps / technical founders

- Pain point: Managing Git, CI/CD, and cloud services is tedious
- Solution: Gemini-powered ops automation
- Channels: Hacker News, GitHub discussions, dev communities

### Affiliate System — SaaS founders / product makers

- Pain point: Building an affiliate program from scratch is expensive
- Solution: White-label platform with tiered commissions, tracking, payouts
- Channels: Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, MicroConf
- **Note:** This also supports the TRO-14 initiative

### YouAndINotAI Dating Platform — App founders

- Pain point: Building a dating app with verification from scratch
- Solution: Full source code, human verification built in, $2,499 one-time
- Note: This is the dating platform itself sold as a product
