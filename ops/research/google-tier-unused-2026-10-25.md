   * Makes **zero** calls through OmniRoute. `POST /api/omniroute/dispatch` (`server.ts:397-468`) is a local simulation (`140 + Math.floor(Math.random() * 180)` ms delay) that appends to an in-memory array (`studioEventLedger`).
