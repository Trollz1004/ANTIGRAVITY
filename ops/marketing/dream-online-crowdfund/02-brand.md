# DREAM Online — Brand Guide (Phase 1)
NEXUS-Sprint · Multi-Channel Marketing Campaign

## Brand Promise
**One world. Live NPCs that remember everything. No pay-to-win — ever.**

## Three Pillars (the game's own words)
1. **"One world."** No instances, no fast travel, no teleports — ever. Distance is the economy; a horse comes later, your feet come first.
2. **"The grind is the game."** Kill → level → loot → repair → push deeper. Every convenience the shop sells is earnable in-world, slower.
3. **"NPCs are alive."** Live-agent NPCs remember who robbed them, who saved them, and act back into the world — free and unlimited for every player.

## Voice Rules

**Do:**
1. Speak in-world, present tense, like the world is already awake. *"The blacksmith remembers your last visit."*
2. Say exactly what convenience buys and what it costs. *"Field repair, anywhere — about twice the Sols cost of walking home."*
3. Use short, physical verbs. *"Push deeper into Rust Ridge."*
4. Let the Sandman carry the mystery — don't over-explain him. *"Dream is watching. He always is."*
5. Name mechanics plainly. *"Sols pay for repairs. NEEDS pay for convenience."*

**Don't:**
1. Never promise power for cash. Not: *"Buy your way to the top."*
2. Never break the fourth wall with real-world company or mission language in-world. Not: *"Join our mission."*
3. Never use loot-box or gambling phrasing. Not: *"Spin for a chance at..."*
4. Never name or compare to a competitor. Not: *"Better than [other MMO]."*
5. Never oversell the AI. Live NPCs are a game feature, not a premium brain — no "unlimited power" hype.

## The Sandman Theme
Dream is the villain who swaps the world's genre every four weeks and says one line on the way in: *"Bring me your dreams."* **"It was all a DREAM" is the reset law, not a tagline** — every epoch demotes the old world's truths to rumor, rumor to fable. Campaign copy may invoke it to build anticipation for the Nightmare Shift or a genre swap ("What's true today is a rumor by dusk"), but it must describe the actual mechanic, never stand alone as empty mysticism.

## Visual Rules (from the locked token set)
- **Base:** deep-space dark — background `#020617`, panels `#0f172a` / `#1e293b`.
- **Primary — neon cyan:** `#22d3ee`, deep `#06b6d4`, bright edge `#00f3ff`.
- **Secondary — neon pink/rose:** `#f472b6`, deep `#ec4899`, `#f43f5e`.
- **Trust gold** `#e9b949` is reserved for verification/trust badges only.
- **No purple.** `#a855f7` exists only as an internal paused/legal-review status color — never brand-facing, never in campaign art.
- **Fonts:** Inter (sans) for headings and body; JetBrains Mono for stats, timestamps, and labels, uppercase, widest tracking.
- **Glass panels:** translucent fill `rgba(15,23,42,0.60)` over a hairline border `rgba(255,255,255,0.10)` — never a solid opaque card.
- **Icons:** Lucide only. **No emoji anywhere in customer-facing copy or UI.**
- Cyan-to-pink gradient is reserved for hero headings and the logo mark — not for body text.

## Compliance (binding rule, from GDD §10 — every surface, every post, every NPC line, every code comment)
Every customer-facing surface is **business-only**. Sell **access, fun, convenience, safety, uptime — nothing else.** The copy sells the product and nothing else, and never describes where money goes. No mission hashtags on any public post. No competitor names. Age gate is **13+ free-to-play** at signup; purchases from a player under 18 require guardian consent through the payment-verified checkout. This is a hard rule, not a style preference — it overrides any campaign hook that conflicts with it.

## Pre-Publish Checklist (every post must pass all ten)
1. Sells only access / fun / convenience / safety / uptime — no other claim.
2. No competitor name anywhere in the copy.
3. The post sells the product and nothing else, and never describes where money goes.
4. No mission hashtags.
5. Age gate reads 13+ free-to-play; no under-18 purchase claim without guardian consent.
6. No pay-to-win claim — convenience only, framed honestly against its in-game cost.
7. No loot-box or gambling language.
8. Uses only the locked palette above — no purple, no off-brand hex.
9. Lucide icons only; zero emoji.
10. Recorded numbers only — no invented stats, no unverified traction claims.
