When you're swiping through dating apps, it's easy to feel like you're stuck in a never-ending cycle of false promises and shallow connections. The problem is often not with the apps themselves, but with how they operate. Bots are notoriously difficult to detect, and many apps rely on automated systems to fill their databases and generate engagement metrics. However, there's anecdotally simple change that can be made to reduce bot activity: requiring profile verification through a secure, human-verified process. This doesn't have to involve anything complicated or time-consuming. A good starting point is to look for apps that offer phone or email verification as part of their sign-up process. This adds an extra layer of authenticity to profiles and helps weed out bots from genuine users. Another way to spot potential bots is to look for red flags in a profile, such as overly generic language or a lack of specific details about interests or hobbies. If a profile seems too perfect or lacks any real substance, it may be worth exercising caution.
