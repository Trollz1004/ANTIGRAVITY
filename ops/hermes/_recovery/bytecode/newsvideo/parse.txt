## docstrings

<module>:
Parse a content/yesterday-news/<YYYYMMDD>/ day folder into structured stories.

Source of truth is the day folder itself: script.txt carries the narration and the
per-story article links, metadata.json carries the title/tags/sources. Nothing here
invents content -- every field is derived from those two files.


  _strip_html:
Tags out, entities decoded, whitespace collapsed.

  Story:
Story

    narration:
Tightened spoken copy: headline as the hook, then a capped summary.

The feed summaries run long and are usually cut off mid-sentence by the RSS
"[...]" truncation. Only complete sentences survive, so the voiceover never
trails off into a fragment.


  Day:
Day

    narration_segments:
(segment_id, spoken text) in render order.

    <genexpr>:
\d{8}
