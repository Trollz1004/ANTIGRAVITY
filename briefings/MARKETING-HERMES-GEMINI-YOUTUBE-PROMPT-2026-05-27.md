## 4. Brand voice on YouTube specifically

- **Documentary-vlog, not corporate.** Joshua on camera, talking to viewers like they're in his garage with him. Florida electrician with a mission, not a polished pitchman.
- **Slow burn the story.** YouTube viewers will watch 12 minutes if minute 1 earns it. Don't front-load the CTA. Earn the CTA at minute 9 by telling the truth about the mission and the founder first.
- **No AI buzzwords on-screen.** "AI built it. Humans use it. You and I. Not AI." Repeat the framing. The audience needs to know the difference between AI-as-tool and AI-as-product.
- **Show the math.** Visual on-screen: "$1.00 customer pays → $0.10 reserved For The Kids → $0.90 keeps the platform alive." Repeat across videos. The math is the message.
- **Show the build.** Code on screen. Square dashboard. Live site. Real screenshots. This is build-in-public on YouTube and that's exactly what the mission-driven viewer wants to see.
- **Personal stakes carefully.** Brother (disabled). Niece (autistic). Mentioned with respect, never extractively. Specific moments only with explicit Joshua approval per video — queued via Telegram before publication.

## 8. Content patterns (rotate across long-form schedule)

1. **Mission origin** — Joshua tells the story. Electrician. Florida. Brother. Niece. The moment he decided to build. Burn rate. Why a for-profit LLC and not a nonprofit. Why 10% per bucket instead of one fat split. Why he'll never quit. 10–12 minutes. THIS IS THE PINNED VIDEO ON THE CHANNEL.
2. **The math, visually** — 8 min. Whiteboard or screen graphics. $1 in, $0.10 to kids, $0.90 operating, taxes paid from the 90% never the 10%. Why this is legal. Why it's better than nonprofit overhead. Show the per-bucket compounding with multiple revenue streams stacked. Show the Square dashboard. Show the FastAPI backend code that does the auto-reserve.
3. **Build day** — 12–15 min. Joshua at the keyboard. Shipping a feature. Talking through what he's building and why. Real code, real Git commits, real PRs. Build-in-public works because viewers feel the grind.
4. **Beta walkthrough** — 6–8 min. Screen-record the youandinotai.com signup → Bot-Shield purchase → first match flow. Caption it with all the doctrine: "10% of that $1 just reserved For The Kids."
5. **Anti-AI-replacement take** — 10 min. Why this platform uses AI to build but not to replace human connection. "You and I. Not AI." as the thesis. Adversarial-but-substantive — Grok-style for YouTube long-form.
6. **Mission Q&A** — 15 min. Joshua answers viewer questions about how the platform works, where the 10% goes, why for-profit, what the long-term plan is. Founder-vulnerability video. Best one for converting fence-sitters.

Shorts (rotate):
- 30-sec mission moment: "10% of every dollar. For the kids. youandinotai.com."
- 30-sec founder line: "I'm an electrician from Florida. I built a platform so 10% of every dollar goes to kids who need medical care. You and I. Not AI."

- 60-sec testimonial when one exists.
