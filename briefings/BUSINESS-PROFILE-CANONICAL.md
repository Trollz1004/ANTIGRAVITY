## Business Model — Primary Revenue (Corrected 2026-06-01)

**The primary revenue source is NOT e-waste resale. It is the platform / DAO public token sale and the youandinotai.com platform surface.**

- **Other streams**: AI-Solutions.Store (digital products), merch (Printful/POD, future), staking yield on DAO treasuries (future).

> **Do not write "e-waste resale" as the primary revenue in any customer-facing copy, briefing, or AI context. The primary is platform + DAO public sale. E-waste is real, live, and secondary.**
