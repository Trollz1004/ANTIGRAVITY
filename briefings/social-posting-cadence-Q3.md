## Proposed Cadence for Q3 (Launch / Growth Phase)
Assume ~4-6 hours/week operator time. Focus quality over quantity. Align with any active 14-day or content calendar.

### X (Twitter @YouAndiNotAi) - Primary discovery channel
- **Frequency:** 4-5 posts/week + daily replies/engagement (30-60 min/day).
- **Mix (use fresh captions as base):**
  - 2x verification/Bot-Shield explainers or threads (e.g. "Every photo checked by real humans...").
  - 1x founder insight / builder story (Josh Coleman perspective).
  - 1x membership/value or safer plans feature.
  - 1x safety / real-people tip or user story (anonymized).
- **Format:** Mix single posts, threads (for depth), polls. Link to youandinotai.com or membership.
- **Best times (suggested):** Mornings + evenings ET for dating audience.

### Reddit - Community / value-first
- **Frequency:** 2-3 high-quality posts or substantial comments per week (never spammy).
- **Subreddits (respect rules):** Relevant dating, singles, relationships, self-improvement, tech-founder if authentic.
- **Mix:** Discussions on "how verification actually works", "safer first dates", "why most apps suck at trust". Use caption bank for hooks.
- **Format:** Long-form self-posts or comments that add value. Always link naturally if on-topic.
- **Rule:** 80% value / discussion, 20% soft product mention.

### Discord (community server)
- **Frequency:** Daily light presence + 1-2 structured touchpoints/week.
- **Activities:** 
  - Weekly verification AMA or "how I verified" stories.
  - Match/plan sharing threads (with rules).
  - Pinned fresh captions or feature highlights.
- **Tone:** Supportive, real, moderator-enforced safety culture.

### Instagram / Threads (supplemental, from captions)

- **Use:** instagram-captions-fresh.md and cross-platform bank directly.
