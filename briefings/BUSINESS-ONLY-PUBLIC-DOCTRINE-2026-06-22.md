# BUSINESS-ONLY PUBLIC DOCTRINE - 2026-06-22

Joshua's current instruction supersedes older non-product operating language for active
product work.

## Active Rule

ANTIGRAVITY and YouAndINotAI are business-first product surfaces. Going forward, public,
customer-facing, marketing, dashboard, checkout, support, automation, and agent handoff copy
must sell only the product, subscription, verification, safety, support, uptime, and platform
value delivered to the customer.

## Removed From Active Operations

Agents must not use old non-product framing as a reason to block launch, delay checkout,
rewrite payments, add control-rights friction, or require extra legal structure before selling a
normal membership or verification product.

Do not publish or operationalize:

- non-product sales claims
- private accounting promises
- legal or tax treatment promises
- named third-party benefit promises
- customer-facing funding claims
- control-rights claims as a condition of membership checkout
- old non-product slogans as product copy

## Private Accounting Boundary

Any owner-directed giving, accounting treatment, tax planning, or private LLC decision stays
outside product copy and outside AI launch gating. Agents do not market it, route it, calculate
it for customers, or treat it as part of checkout.

## Historical Files

Older briefings, archived strategy, prototype pages, and old code identifiers may exist as
history only. They do not authorize current copy, current checkout gates, current dashboard
claims, or current agent decisions.

## Node Scope

This applies across Sabretooth, T5500, 9020, and any helper node. T5500 remains the public
front-door lane for domains, tunnels, and payments. Sabretooth remains the brain/agent lane.
9020 remains a dev/support lane. No node may reintroduce old non-product framing into an
active product surface.
