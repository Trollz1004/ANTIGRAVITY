WHY (revenue impact — concrete, not aspirational)
===============================================================================
PR #149 (2026-06-17) wired all 5 live Square tiers onto the static landing.
Yesterday's PR (2026-06-18, READY-TO-PUSH-2026-06-18) closed the Square
seller-protection / FTC gap with ToS + Privacy. Today's gap is the surface
the landing presents to every off-platform link share AND every search-engine
crawler.

What was broken before this PR:

  1. SHARE-LINK PREVIEW IS A TEXT BLOB
     Send a youandinotai.com link in iMessage, X, Discord, Slack, LinkedIn,
     Reddit DM, FB Messenger, email — every one of those preview parsers
     looks for `og:image`. There was none. So the preview rendered as just
     the URL string and the meta description, with no visual card.
     For a dating / social-discovery surface, that is the single largest
     conversion drag on viral / referral traffic — the visual preview is
     what makes a person click vs scroll past.

  2. NO FAVICON
     Open the landing in a browser tab: blank globe icon. On a dating /
     social-discovery purchase decision, blank-favicon is a trust signal of
     "abandoned / amateur site." Tab abandonment is measurable; favicon
     adds zero cost and lifts return-visit retention.

  3. NO STRUCTURED DATA
     The 5 Square tier prices ($1, $14.99/mo, $39.99, $99.99, $2,500) were
     invisible to Google's rich-result system. With the new JSON-LD @graph
     (Organization + WebSite + ItemList of 5 Offers), Google can render
     price snippets in search results — directly improving SERP CTR for
     "youandinotai" / "founding member dating" / brand-name searches.

  4. NO ROBOTS.TXT / SITEMAP.XML
     Crawl efficiency was slow — search engines had no entry-point map.
     The new sitemap lists all 3 current pages with lastmod dates so they
     re-crawl on update.

  5. NO `<link rel="canonical">`
     Bare URL + trailing-slash variant + ?utm_source variants all looked
     like distinct pages to Google, splitting link equity. Canonical
     consolidates on https://youandinotai.com/.

This change closes all five gaps with one deploy. No backend touched. No
tier card touched. No price touched. No legal copy touched. Pure
head-section + new static assets.

Direct support for THE-WHEEL §"North Star" reset: first paying customer
on a surface that survives every link share. Square account LY5GN09F5AN83
revenue rail unchanged.
