WHY (revenue impact — concrete, not aspirational)
===============================================================================
PR #149 (2026-06-17, merged) wired all 5 live Square tiers onto the static
landing — Bot-Shield $1, Founding $14.99/mo, 3-Month $39.99, 12-Month $99.99,
Royalty $2,500. Real checkout density was multiplied 5x.

But the page that now collects 5 streams of Square payments shipped with:
  - NO Terms of Service link
  - NO Privacy Policy link
  - NO age (18+) disclosure
  - NO Square processor disclosure on the surface itself

That gap is a revenue blocker for three concrete reasons:

  1. SQUARE SELLER PROTECTION
     Square's seller agreement requires merchants on dating / social-
     discovery surfaces to publish accessible Terms of Service and a Privacy
     Policy. Square reviews these on dispute / chargeback escalation; a
     surface that takes cards without them is exposed to account holds and
     reserve increases. Square account LY5GN09F5AN83 is THE revenue rail
     for youandinotai.com — losing it kills the wheel.

  2. PRIVACY / FTC EXPOSURE
     Collecting an email + billing data on a U.S.-resident surface without
     a Privacy Policy is FTC §5 exposure. Cheap to fix, expensive to ignore.
     FL §501.171 (state breach notice) also assumes the existence of a
     written policy.

  3. CONVERSION DRAG
     The bot-skeptical / pre-purchase visitor reflex on a dating-tier card
     is "where are the terms?" A missing Terms of Service link on a $2,500
     Royalty Card tier kills the highest-LTV bucket on the page. Existing
     tier copy ("Cancel anytime. Original Founder badge stays.") makes a
     promise the visitor can't verify against a written ToS — that's a
     trust break.

This change closes all three with a single deploy. Same pattern that ships
on ai-solutions.store today (the proven legal scaffold from commits
aadf028 + 3269630). No backend touched. No tier card touched. No price
touched. The static fallback page now stands on its own legally for every
visitor who lands on it from any traffic source.
