### 3. Code Software
Software / code marketplace or storefront. Sells tools, scripts, or AI-built software products.
Its own bucket, its own 10% rail. Josh to define what goes here.

### 4. ForKids Mart
Marketplace specifically oriented around kids. Could overlap with NEEDS (ForKids = the
mission-branded storefront where purchases directly route to kids in need).

Any platform Josh builds: kids 13 and under get free access. This is doctrine, not a feature.
It is the mission in product form. Every platform must have a free tier for minors.

Lives at: `apps/opuspawclaw/` (Vite + Electron + React 19 desktop AI workstation)
