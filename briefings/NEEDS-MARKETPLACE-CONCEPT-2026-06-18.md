## The Concept

A trade-enabled marketplace platform. Either expand **AI-Solutions.store** or spin a second
platform from **Business Exchange** — key differentiator from eBay and everything else:
**it accepts TRADES**.

## How It Works

- Sellers list items for **sale, trade, or both**
- To trade, both parties agree to set a declared value on their items (TOS-enforced)
- Platform charges a **% fee based on the declared trade value** — clean, simple, auditable
- Cash listings work the same as eBay; trade listings are the moat
- No category limit: trading cards, comics, cars, trucks, anything

## The Currency: NEEDS

- Points / currency used to facilitate trades are called **NEEDS**
- Every NEED purchased or spent → a percentage routes to a child who actually needs something
- The name IS the mission: you spend a NEED, a kid gets a need met
- Hashtags baked into the brand: **#ForTheKids** · **#UntilNoKidInNeed**

## Why This Works

- Trade markets are underserved — eBay doesn't do it well, nobody does it at scale
- Fee % on declared trade value = new revenue bucket (its own 10% mission reserve)
- NEEDS as currency creates a mission-branded token that doesn't need to be a DAO to be powerful
- Naturally viral: every trade has a story; every NEED spent has a kid behind it

## Platform Relationship

- Could live under **ai-solutions.store** as an expanded market tab, OR
- Could be the second platform born from **Business Exchange** (49% DAO, launch at $10k treasury)
- Either way: its own bucket, its own NEEDS token, its own 10% reserve rail
