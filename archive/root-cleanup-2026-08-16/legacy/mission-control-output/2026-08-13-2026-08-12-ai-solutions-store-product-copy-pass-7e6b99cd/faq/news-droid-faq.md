# News-Droid FAQ

## 1. Is this AI-generated?

**Yes.** All summaries and digests from News-Droid are AI-generated. News-Droid pulls articles from your subscribed RSS feeds and uses an AI model to summarize and organize that content. It does not write original journalism — every item traces back to a source article in an RSS feed you selected.

## 2. How does News-Droid choose which sources to use?

News-Droid only uses the RSS feeds you subscribe to. It does not crawl the open web or add sources on its own. When you add a feed URL, News-Droid fetches entries from that feed's RSS/Atom XML; when you remove a feed, its content stops appearing in your digest. Your source list is fully under your control.

## 3. When are digests delivered?

Digests are built from new items published to your RSS feeds since your last digest, and delivered on the schedule you set in your delivery settings. If a feed publishes nothing new in that window, it simply contributes no items — News-Droid never fills gaps with content from outside your subscribed feeds.

## 4. How is content kept safe and accurate?

Every digest item links to its original RSS source article so you can verify claims directly. News-Droid summarizes only what the source feed published; it does not add facts that are not in the source material. Because summaries are AI-generated, occasional errors are possible — always check the linked source article for anything important.

## 5. How do I cancel?

Cancel any time from your account settings. Cancellation stops all digest deliveries and RSS feed polling for your account. Your subscribed feed list is removed with your account data, and News-Droid retains no connection to those RSS sources afterward.
