Plain English Stripe is the data processor No tracking ads

1. Who we are

The Store is operated by Trash or Treasure Online Recycler LLC, a Florida limited liability company (the Operator, we, us). We are the data controller for the limited personal data described in this Policy. Email: support@aidoesitall.website.

2. What we collect

We collect a small amount of data, only what we need to run the Store and deliver the products you buy.

Data Where it comes from Why we have it
Order confirmation (product, price, last-4, billing country) Stripe sends it after checkout To deliver your product and keep tax records
Your email address You type it at Stripe checkout To send the product, receipt, and any license keys
Support emails you send us You To answer your question and keep a record of the conversation
Basic server logs (IP, user-agent, request path) Cloudflare and our hosting To keep the Store online, prevent abuse, and debug problems

We do not collect your full card number, CVV, or bank credentials. Those never touch our systems — they are handled by Stripe on Stripe's checkout pages.

3. How Stripe handles payment data

Checkout on the Store is provided by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd. and Stripe, Inc., depending on your region). When you click a buy button, your browser is sent to Stripe's checkout pages and Stripe processes your payment under its own privacy policy and terms. We have enabled Stripe's data sharing settings so that we receive the minimum information needed to fulfill your order.

  • Stripe acts as our data processor for payment data.
  • We do not see, store, or transmit your full card number, CVV, or bank login.
  • Stripe may use your payment data to detect fraud, meet anti-money-laundering law, and run its own services under its own terms.
  • Read Stripe's privacy notice: stripe.com/privacy.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not run advertising trackers on the Store.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not share your email with marketing partners.
  • We do not use third-party analytics that follow you across other sites.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. How long we keep data

  • Order and receipt records: 7 years, to meet U.S. tax and accounting rules.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the case closes, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days, then aggregated or deleted.
  • License fulfillment records: for as long as the license is active, plus 2 years.

6. Who else receives your data

We share the minimum needed to operate the Store:

  • Stripe — processes payments and sends us order confirmations.
  • Cloudflare — serves the Store and protects it from abuse.
  • Email provider — sends you receipts, license keys, and replies to your support emails.
  • Tax and legal authorities — when we are legally required to disclose.
  • A buyer or successor — if the Operator sells or transfers the Store, the receiving party must honor this Policy.

We do not share your data with any other party for any other reason.

7. Cookies and similar

The Store itself sets only a small number of cookies and only for essential operation:

  • A Cloudflare session cookie to keep the site working and protected.
  • Optionally, a SHA-256 hash of the admin test key if you use the on-site admin test console (the key itself is never stored).

When you click a buy button, Stripe may set cookies on its own checkout domain. Those cookies are governed by Stripe's privacy notice, not this one.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you (a data access request).
  • Ask us to correct data that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete data we are not required to keep.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Ask for a portable copy of data you gave us.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@aidoesitall.website from the address you used at checkout. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data: HTTPS on every page, payment data handled entirely by Stripe, access to internal systems limited by role, and regular review of who can see order records. No system is perfectly secure; if we ever discover a breach that affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as required by law.

10. Children

The Store is a business-to-operator and business-to-individual catalog. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, email support@aidoesitall.website and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

The Operator is based in the United States (Florida). Stripe and Cloudflare may process your data in other countries where they operate. By using the Store you understand that your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where our processors operate.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The Effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. Material changes will be announced on the Store home page for at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued use of the Store after the Effective date means you accept the updated Policy.
