Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 23, 2026  ·  Trash Or Treasure Online Recycler LLC  ·  Sorrento, Florida

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact information: name, email address, and phone number when you contact us or schedule service
  • Pickup details: address, preferred time windows, and item descriptions
  • Payment information: processed through Square or other payment providers; we do not store your full card number

Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics data: page views, session duration, and interaction patterns when analytics are enabled
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and referrer URL
  • Cookies: essential cookies for site functionality and optional cookies for analytics or preferences

2. How We Use Your Information

Purpose Data Used
process transactions contact info, payment info, pickup details
schedule and coordinate service name, phone, email, address, availability
send confirmations and updates email, phone
improve site performance analytics data, session behavior
maintain internal accounting and service reporting transaction and scheduling records

3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services that collect limited information in the normal course of providing the site or service:

  • Square: payment processing (Square Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts: typography delivery
  • Analytics tools: only when optional analytics are enabled

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it only when needed to provide service, comply with the law, or complete a business transfer.

5. Data Retention

  • Transaction records: retained as needed for accounting, tax, and dispute resolution
  • Contact information: retained while needed to provide service or comply with legal obligations
  • Analytics data: retained in aggregated form when analytics are enabled

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal retention obligations. Contact us at collaboration@youandinotai.com.

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to keep the site working and, if you allow it, to measure site performance. You can manage cookie settings in the site banner or your browser.

8. Data Security

We use HTTPS and reasonable operational safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

OnlineRecycle.org is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted here with an updated effective date.
