Questions business owners ask before automating

Clear answers about what Agensy builds, how it works, and what implementation involves.

What does an AI automation agency do?

An AI automation agency builds software agents that handle repetitive business work - answering calls and chats, qualifying leads, processing invoices, producing content - connected to the tools a business already uses. Agensy scopes one workflow, agrees a measurable KPI, and delivers a working agent in a 28-day sprint.

What should my business automate first?

Start with a repetitive, high-volume workflow tied to a measurable outcome - response time, qualified leads, processing hours, or support load. We map the workflow, define the KPI, and recommend the smallest automation that can prove ROI.

How much does an AI voice agent or receptionist cost?

Agensy works on fixed-cost project pricing agreed before the build starts - no hourly billing and no surprise invoices. The exact number depends on the workflow, call volume, and integrations, so we quote it on the free call after mapping your workflow.

What happens if the AI agent can't answer a question?

It hands the conversation to a human. Every agent we build has explicit rules for what it must not answer, and escalation paths - transfer the call, tag a teammate, or open a ticket - are pressure-tested with real cases before launch.

Can Agensy connect AI with the tools we already use?

Yes. We connect agents and automations with CRMs, calendars, helpdesks, inboxes, phone systems, databases, and internal tools through native integrations or APIs. Your team keeps working in the systems it already knows.

How quickly can our first automation go live?

Most focused projects launch within 28 days. If the agreed KPI isn't hit by day 28, we extend the sprint free until it is - you don't pay for a system that doesn't work.
