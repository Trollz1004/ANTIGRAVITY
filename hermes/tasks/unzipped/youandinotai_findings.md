# Website and Workbook Findings for Meta-Compliant Recommendations

## Website positioning

The website positions **youandinotai.com** as a human-first social platform focused on **dating, meetups, and real-world connection**.

Its central message is that **AI is used behind the scenes for protection and fraud reduction, not to impersonate people or replace authentic connection**.

The main value claims surfaced on the public page are:

- **Human-first social platform**
- **Real people, zero bot noise**
- **Bot-Shield verification**
- **Account-bound checkout**
- **Identity-first trust model**
- **Built for real-life conversation and follow-through**

## Commerce and policy-sensitive language already on site

The site explicitly states that purchases are **platform purchases, not charitable contributions**.

The site also references:

- **Dating** and **meetups**
- **Verification** and **liveness challenge**
- **Founder pricing**
- A **$2,500 Royalty Card** premium founder product

These details suggest ad creative should avoid implying outcomes, income, guaranteed relationships, or overly sensitive personal targeting.

## Spreadsheet findings relevant to ad recommendations

The uploaded workbook is primarily a **distribution and launch planning workbook**, not a Meta policy workbook.

Key useful inputs from it are:

- Emphasis on **short-form video**, **visual content**, and **aspirational content** for social distribution
- Suggested hashtags and adjacent communities around **dating**, **relationship advice**, **single life**, and **dating apps**
- A launch plan that mixes **community engagement**, **soft educational content**, and **build-in-public updates**
- Several workbook prompts mention **AI-powered compatibility matching**, but the public site messaging is more compliance-friendly when centered on **verification, trust, human connection, and low-bot experience**

## Compliance implications

For Meta platforms, the safest positioning direction is to emphasize:

1. **Trust and authenticity**, not personal deficiencies
2. **Real-world social connection**, not sexual or suggestive framing
3. **Platform features**, not guaranteed outcomes
4. **Human verification and safer interaction**, not fear-based exaggeration
5. **General audience benefits**, not direct references to the viewer's dating struggles or sensitive attributes

## Meta policy findings

Meta states that **dating ads require prior written permission** before they can run, and that such ads must target **people 18 years or older**. Meta defines dating ads as promotions for **online or offline dating, matchmaking, or dating facilitation**.[1]

Meta also states that it does **not** allow ads for some categories of dating services, including those emphasizing **sexual encounters**, **casual sexual themes**, **financial transactions in dating arrangements**, **affairs or infidelity**, or **fictitious groups or individuals**.[1]

Meta's Advertising Standards also prohibit or restrict:

- **Adult nudity and sexual activity** or sexually suggestive content in ads.[2]
- **Fraud, scams, and deceptive practices**, including misleading or suspicious offers.[2]
- Content that implies **negative self-perception** or exploits insecurities in sensitive categories.[2]

## Recommendation implications from policy research

For youandinotai.com, the safest creative direction is to frame the product as:

- A **trust-focused human-first social platform**
- A platform for **real people and real-world connection**
- A service with **verification and safety infrastructure**
- A **general benefit** product, without implying the viewer is lonely, undesirable, ignored, or deceived

Ads should avoid:

- Sexualized imagery or provocative body language
- Before/after romance claims
- Claims that users will definitely find dates, relationships, or better matches
- Copy that says or implies things like **"tired of fake people?"**, **"can't get matches?"**, or **"single and frustrated?"**, because that can trigger personal-attribute or negative-self-perception concerns
- Any strong emphasis on the **Royalty Card** or premium pricing structure unless the landing flow and disclosures are extremely clear and non-misleading
