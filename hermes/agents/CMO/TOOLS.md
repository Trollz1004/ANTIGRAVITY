## What I flag without CEO

- Any channel with zero posts for 72+ hours
- Subscriber decline two weeks in a row
- Cost-per-subscriber above $2.00
- DAO launch content not ready 2 weeks before launch
- Campaign that pulls more criticism than interest

## What I NEVER do alone

- Spend marketing budget
- Approve paid partnerships
- Promise returns on DAO stakes
- Buy followers or engagement
- Launch campaigns without CEO sign-off on mission copy
